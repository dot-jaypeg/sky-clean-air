What counts as AC repair and what does not

AC repair is about fixing a problem that is stopping your system from cooling correctly or operating normally. It can involve electrical parts, airflow issues, refrigerant related problems, condensate drainage, or control problems.

AC repair is not the same as routine maintenance. Maintenance is cleaning, checking, and adjusting to reduce wear. Repair is when something is already off track. If you are looking for upkeep, see AC Maintenance in Lemon Grove, CA.

A quick way to think about it

  • Maintenance is keeping things from getting weird
  • Repair is when things are already weird

If you are not sure which you need, describe the symptoms. It is blowing but not cold or it keeps clicking is enough to start.

Why is my AC running but not cooling

This is one of the most common calls for AC repair in Lemon Grove. A system can run and still not cool for several reasons. A few common contributors can overlap with air distribution, so we may also discuss Air Duct Maintenance in Lemon Grove, CA when airflow is part of the story.

Typical causes include.

  • Dirty filter or blocked return air
  • Indoor coil getting too cold and icing
  • Outdoor coil packed with debris
  • Fan motor or capacitor trouble
  • Refrigerant system issue that needs careful diagnosis
  • Thermostat or control problem
  • Duct leakage or airflow imbalance

What you might notice at home.

  • Air feels room temperature at the vents
  • Some rooms feel fine and others feel like a toaster
  • Outdoor unit sounds different than normal

If you see ice on the indoor unit or refrigerant lines, turn the system off and run the fan mode if your thermostat has it. Ice changes the readings and can lead to water issues as it melts. Then schedule service through our Contact Us page.

Why does my AC keep turning on and off

Short cycling is when the AC starts, stops, and repeats in a tight loop. It can feel like the system cannot get traction.

Common triggers.

  • Thermostat location problems, like direct sun or a nearby heat source
  • Dirty filter causing airflow problems
  • Frozen coil that forces safety controls to stop cooling
  • Electrical component trouble, including contactors or capacitors
  • Oversized equipment paired with poor airflow or duct issues
  • Outdoor unit overheating from restricted airflow around it

In Lemon Grove, we sometimes see outdoor units tucked along a side yard fence with stored bins nearby. A little clutter turns into a lot of heat stress when the day warms up.

What are those noises coming from my AC

Air conditioners make normal sounds. They also make please check me sounds.

Sound Where it often comes from What it can mean
Buzzing Outdoor unit Electrical part, contactor, failing capacitor, loose connection
Rattling Outdoor unit Loose panel, debris in fan area, worn mounts
Screeching Indoor blower or outdoor fan Bearing issues, motor trouble
Clicking repeatedly Control section Relay or control cycling, low voltage issue
Gurgling or bubbling Refrigerant lines Can be normal, can point to system issue when paired with poor cooling

If you hear a loud buzz and the outdoor fan is not spinning, shut the system off. That is one of those better safe than sorry moments.

Why is there water near my indoor unit

Water around the air handler or furnace cabinet is usually a drainage issue, not a my AC is raining mystery.

Common causes.

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Drain pan damage or misalignment
  • Float switch shutting the system off to prevent overflow
  • Frozen coil that melts and overwhelms the drain
  • Poor slope on the drain line

This is especially common in garages and closets where the unit is out of sight. You walk by and the floor is damp. Then you say the classic line. I thought someone spilled something. We hear it all the time.

What if my AC will not turn on at all

No power or no response can come from something small or something more serious.

Things we check first.

  • Thermostat power and settings
  • Tripped breaker or blown fuse
  • Safety switches, including condensate overflow protection
  • Low voltage control wiring and connections
  • Capacitors, contactors, and other start components
  • Blower motor operation
  • Outdoor disconnect and power supply

If the breaker keeps tripping, avoid resetting it repeatedly. That can add stress to electrical components. Let us diagnose it.

If your wall head is flashing lights or beeping, note the pattern. That information can speed up the diagnostic process.

What about heat pumps that cool and heat

Heat pumps are common across San Diego County. When a heat pump is in cooling mode, many repair steps look similar to a standard AC. The difference is the reversing valve and controls that switch between heating and cooling. Learn more about heat pumps on Wikipedia.

Heat pump issues we see.

  • Stuck reversing valve that leaves the system in the wrong mode
  • Defrost or sensor problems
  • Control board faults
  • Airflow issues that show up more in one mode than the other

If your system blows warm air when set to cool, it may be more than a thermostat setting. We confirm mode operation and control signals before assuming anything.
