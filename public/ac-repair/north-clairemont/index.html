What counts as an AC repair and what does not

An AC repair is work that fixes a failed or failing part, an electrical issue, or an airflow problem that prevents normal cooling. It is not the same as a tune up, and it is not a full replacement. If your system is near the end of its service life, we may discuss AC Replacement in North Clairemont, CA instead of repeated repairs.

Common AC repairs we handle in North Clairemont

  • Replacing a failed capacitor that stops the outdoor unit from starting
  • Fixing a contactor that is chattering or stuck
  • Diagnosing a thermostat that is not communicating with the system
  • Clearing a clogged condensate drain line that is causing a leak
  • Addressing a blower motor that is weak, noisy, or not running
  • Troubleshooting low airflow from dirty coils, a failing blower, or duct issues
  • Finding the cause of a unit that freezes up and then quits cooling
  • Correcting loose wiring or a tripped safety switch

My AC is running but not cooling

This is a classic North Clairemont call during the first warm weekend.

Common causes include

  • Dirty air filter reducing airflow and causing coil temperature problems
  • Indoor coil freezing from airflow issues or other system faults
  • Outdoor coil clogged with dirt and debris
  • A capacitor failing so the compressor or fan cannot start properly
  • Thermostat issues or incorrect settings after a power outage
  • Duct leaks pulling in warm attic air, especially in older attic runs
  • Refrigerant related problems that need careful diagnosis

What you can check quickly before scheduling

  • Confirm the thermostat is set to cool and the fan is set to auto
  • Replace the filter if it is visibly loaded
  • Make sure supply vents are open and not blocked by furniture
  • Look at the outdoor unit and confirm the fan is spinning when cooling is called

If the outdoor unit is silent, humming, or repeatedly trying to start, shut it off and call. If you need to schedule service, use the Contact Us page or call 619-304-8822.

What it means if the AC keeps turning on and off

Short cycling can come from a few different directions, and it is not always the same fix.

  • Thermostat location issues like direct sun or a nearby heat source
  • Dirty filter or coil causing poor airflow and system stress
  • Electrical components that fail under load then reset
  • Oversized equipment that cools too fast and shuts off early
  • Drain safety switch trips from a backed up condensate line
  • Loose connections creating intermittent control problems

In North Clairemont, short cycling can also tie back to return airflow problems in homes that were remodeled over the years. A return grille that got downsized or blocked can make the system act like it is suffocating, and sometimes the correction involves duct steps like Air Duct Repair in North Clairemont, CA.

Why the AC is leaking water inside

Water around an indoor unit is common, especially when the system runs longer on warmer days.

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Cracked or rusted drain pan in older units
  • Improper slope or a disconnected drain line
  • Frozen coil that later thaws and overwhelms drainage
  • Drain safety switch shutting the system down to prevent overflow

If you see water, turn off cooling and run the fan setting only if recommended for your setup, then call for service. If the unit is in a hallway closet, a little water can become drywall and flooring trouble fast.

Quick guide to AC noises

If the sound is sudden and loud, shut the system off and schedule service. If it is mild but new, it still deserves a look before it becomes a bigger interruption.
