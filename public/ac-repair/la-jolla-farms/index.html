What it means when the AC keeps turning on and off

Short cycling is when the system starts, runs briefly, then shuts off, repeating more often than normal. It can make your home feel uneven and can wear parts faster. Short cycling can also show up in systems that are due for AC Maintenance or have airflow restrictions that need attention.

  • Thermostat placement that gets hit by direct sun or nearby drafts
  • Oversized equipment that satisfies the thermostat too quickly
  • Dirty filter or coil causing temperature or pressure issues
  • Refrigerant system faults
  • Electrical or control problems including a weakening capacitor
  • Drain safety switch shutting the system down when water backs up

In La Jolla Farms, we also see short cycling when a return grille is partly blocked by furniture in a hallway or a closet door gets closed on a louvered return. It is a small change that can cause a big comfort headache.

What to do before the technician arrives

A few simple steps can make the visit smoother.

  • Set the thermostat to cool and a few degrees below room temperature
  • Replace the filter if you have a spare and it is clearly clogged
  • Clear access to the indoor unit, often in a closet, garage, or attic entry
  • Clear access to the outdoor unit, including moving patio items
  • Make a short list of symptoms and when they started

If the system is tripping a breaker, leave it off and mention it when you call. Repeated resets can make troubleshooting harder and can stress electrical components.
