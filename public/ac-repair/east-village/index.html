What counts as AC repair and what counts as maintenance

Repair is when something is not working as it should and needs diagnosis and correction. Maintenance is the routine care that helps avoid surprises.

AC repair usually involves

  • Electrical troubleshooting and replacing failed components
  • Fixing airflow issues that cause freezing or weak cooling
  • Addressing condensate drain problems that can lead to leaks
  • Resolving thermostat and control issues
  • Locating and correcting refrigerant related issues where appropriate
  • Correcting fan, motor, or capacitor problems

Maintenance usually involves

  • Basic cleaning and inspection
  • Filter replacement reminders and fit checks
  • Checking temperatures and general operation
  • Catching early wear before it becomes a no cool call

Why AC coils freeze up

Frozen coils are common when airflow is restricted or the system is not transferring heat correctly. In East Village, dust from nearby streets and construction can build up quicker than you would expect, especially if filters are neglected or ill fitting.

Common causes of freezing

  • Dirty filter or blocked return grille
  • Dirty evaporator coil
  • Blower motor or blower wheel issues
  • Closed supply vents or furniture blocking returns
  • Refrigerant related problems that need a technician to evaluate

What you should do if it freezes

  • Switch cooling off to prevent compressor strain
  • Run the fan to help thaw
  • Check the filter and replace if dirty
  • Do not chip ice off coils or poke around with tools

Yes, we have heard the I used a hair dryer story. It usually ends with and then it made a new noise.

What to check before scheduling

You do not need to be a technician. A few basic checks can rule out the simple stuff.

Quick checklist

  • Is the filter clogged or collapsed
  • Are supply vents open and not blocked
  • Is the outdoor unit clear of debris and running

  • Do you see ice on lines or the indoor coil area
