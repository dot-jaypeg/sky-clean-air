What to do right now if the AC stops working

A few safe checks can save time and help you describe the problem clearly. If the system seems dirty and restricted, it may also be helpful to consider AC Cleaning in Beach Barber Tract, CA after the immediate issue is resolved.

  • Check the thermostat and confirm it is set to cool and below room temperature and replace batteries if needed
  • Check the filter and replace it if dirty since a clogged filter can cause icing and poor airflow
  • Look at the breaker and reset it once if tripped and stop if it trips again
  • Check the outdoor unit and note whether it is running and whether the fan is spinning and whether there is unusual buzzing
  • Look for ice and if present turn the system off set the fan to on and let it thaw
  • Water around the indoor unit means turn the system off to avoid overflow since a clogged drain line is common

If you just got back from a beach day and the house feels like a sauna, it is tempting to keep flipping switches. Do one reset and gather a few observations and then stop.

What happens during an AC repair visit

We keep it practical and tidy. A typical diagnostic flow looks like this and we may note airflow details that connect to duct performance and services like Air Duct Maintenance in Beach Barber Tract, CA.
