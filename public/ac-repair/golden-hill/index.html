What counts as an AC repair and what does not

AC repair usually includes work like this

Why is my AC running but not cooling

This is the most common request for AC repair in Golden Hill. The system can run and still not cool for several reasons. When the cause is airflow related, duct issues can also be involved, and Air Duct Repair in Golden Hill, CA may be part of the longer term fix.

Common causes we see

  • Dirty air filter restricting airflow
  • Indoor coil icing from low airflow or control issues
  • Outdoor unit not rejecting heat because the fan is not working
  • Electrical start components failing so the compressor cannot start
  • Thermostat issues or wiring problems
  • Refrigerant related problems tied to leaks or restrictions

What you can check before we arrive

  • Make sure the thermostat is set to Cool and the temperature is set below room temp
  • Replace the filter if you have the correct size
  • Confirm the indoor vents are open and not blocked by furniture
  • Look at the outdoor unit and see if the fan is spinning

If the outdoor fan is not spinning, turn the system off and give us a call. Running it in that condition can create more trouble than it is worth. If you need to schedule, use Contact Us or call +18583465551.

Why is my AC making a loud noise or rattling

Golden Hill has a mix of older construction and newer remodels, and some outdoor units sit on small pads near walkways. A little vibration can turn into a lot of noise.

Noises that often lead to repair

  • Rattling from a loose panel or screws
  • Buzzing from a contactor or electrical issue
  • Clicking when trying to start, often tied to start components
  • Screeching from a motor bearing that is wearing out
  • Banging or thumping from blower imbalance or mounting issues

Small anecdote. We hear this one a lot. Homeowner says it is making a noise like a shopping cart wheel. Tech says that is the sound of something that wants attention before it quits at 9 pm.

If you hear a new loud sound, shut the system off and note when it happens. Start up, shut down, constant, or only when the fan runs. That detail helps narrow the cause faster.

Why does my AC keep turning on and off

Short cycling can make a place feel clammy and it can wear parts faster. In Golden Hill, we see this when units are oversized for a smaller home, or when airflow is restricted after a remodel. If airflow keeps coming up, consider adding Air Duct Maintenance in Golden Hill, CA to your plan.

Common triggers

  • Dirty filter or blocked return grille
  • Thermostat placed in a warm spot like near a sunny window
  • Refrigerant or coil freezing issues
  • Electrical controls that are failing intermittently
  • Drain safety switch tripping from a backed up drain line
  • Overheating outdoor unit from coil blockage or poor clearance

If your system starts, runs a few minutes, shuts off, then repeats, turn it off and call. The fix can be straightforward, but repeated cycling tends to stress electrical components.

What if my AC is leaking water inside

Water on the floor near the air handler is a fast track to a bad day, especially in a hallway closet or attic access. Golden Hill homes often have compact indoor equipment locations, so a little drip becomes a big mess quickly.

Typical causes

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Cracked drain pan
  • Frozen coil that later melts all at once
  • Improper slope on the drain line
  • Condensate pump failure in setups that require pumping

What you can do safely

  • Turn the system off to stop making more water
  • Put a towel or small container under the drip
  • If you can access the filter, replace it if it is dirty

Avoid pouring random chemicals into the drain line. We have seen that turn a small clog into a bigger repair.
