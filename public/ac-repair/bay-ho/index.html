My AC is running but not cooling

This is one of the most common reasons people search for AC Repair in Bay Ho.

Typical causes include

  • Dirty air filter or airflow restriction
  • Dirty outdoor coil
  • Refrigerant issues
  • Weak capacitor
  • Thermostat or control problem
  • Frozen evaporator coil

What you can do right away

  • Set the thermostat to cool and fan to auto
  • Check that return vents are not blocked by furniture
  • Replace the filter if you have the correct size on hand
  • If you see ice on the indoor copper lines or indoor coil area, turn cooling off and leave fan on
  • Let it thaw before we arrive so we can test properly

We once heard It is cooling great in the hallway. A closed bedroom door and a blocked return can make comfort uneven. Airflow basics matter, and sometimes that leads to Air Duct Cleaning in Bay Ho, CA to help address dust and restriction that show up at the registers.

Why is my AC leaking water inside

Water where it should not be can lead to drywall stains, warped flooring, and that what is that smell moment. In Bay Ho, warm days followed by cooler evenings can create heavy condensation and your system should drain it away.

Common reasons for leaks

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Cracked or misaligned drain pan
  • Frozen coil thawing fast
  • Improper unit level
  • Drain safety switch triggered

If you see a steady drip near the indoor unit, it is worth shutting the system off to avoid more water spread. If you are in a condo, that also helps avoid becoming that neighbor.

What do strange AC noises mean

No system is silent, but new noises are the system waving a little flag.

Common sounds and likely culprits

  • Buzzing often tied to an electrical issue, contactor, loose connection, or failing capacitor
  • Rattling often tied to a loose panel, debris in the outdoor unit, or a fan issue
  • Squealing often tied to blower belt on older setups, motor bearings, or blower wheel problems
  • Clicking on and off rapidly often tied to control issues, thermostat issues, relay problems, or short cycling causes
  • Humming with no start often tied to a capacitor or motor issue

You say it is making a noise like a jar of pennies. We say that is not part of the original design.

What is short cycling and why does it matter

Short cycling is when the AC turns on, runs briefly, then shuts off, and repeats. It is hard on components and it usually means comfort is uneven.

Common causes

  • Oversized system for the space
  • Dirty filter or blocked airflow
  • Thermostat location issues
  • Low refrigerant or coil freeze ups
  • Electrical issues like a failing capacitor or contactor
  • Overheating outdoor unit from blocked airflow

In Bay Ho we see this when outdoor units are tucked behind a fence with limited clearance, or when patio storage creeps closer to the condenser over time. If the system is aging or mismatched, AC Replacement in Bay Ho, CA can be a better long term step than repeated repairs.

What if the outdoor unit is not turning on

If the indoor fan runs but the outdoor unit is quiet, you might be getting air movement but no cooling.

Common causes

  • Tripped breaker or shutoff disconnect issue
  • Failed capacitor
  • Contactor not engaging
  • Compressor or fan motor problem
  • Safety switch or control board issue

Look for a tripped breaker. If it trips again after resetting once, leave it off and call. Repeated resets can turn a small electrical issue into a bigger one.
