What counts as an AC repair, and what does not

Sometimes people think any comfort issue is an AC problem, but it can be a few different things.

AC repair usually covers issues like

  • The system blows warm air
  • The outdoor unit will not run
  • The indoor fan is not moving air
  • Breaker trips when the AC starts
  • Strange noises like buzzing, rattling, or squealing
  • Water pooling near the indoor unit
  • Short cycling where it turns on and off repeatedly
  • Uneven cooling from room to room when the system used to be consistent

Not exactly AC repair but often related

  • Thermostat setup issues or dead batteries
  • Dirty filters and airflow restrictions
  • Duct leakage or disconnected duct runs
  • Poor insulation or hot attic conditions that overwhelm the system

We handle the repair side, and we also point out airflow and duct issues when they are clearly affecting comfort. If the issue looks duct related, we may recommend Air Duct Repair in Burlingame, CA or Air Duct Maintenance in Burlingame, CA so you are not fixing a symptom while the cause keeps coming back.

My AC is running but not cooling, what is usually going on

This is one of the most common calls for AC Repair in Burlingame.

Frequent causes include

  • Dirty air filter restricting airflow
  • Frozen evaporator coil from low airflow or other system issues
  • Outdoor unit not rejecting heat well because the coil is dirty or the fan is failing
  • Electrical component failure like a capacitor or contactor
  • Refrigerant issue that affects cooling performance
  • Thermostat or control issue that stops the system from staging correctly
  • Duct problems that dump cooled air into an attic or crawlspace

A homeowner near California Drive described it as the air feels like it is trying, but it is not. We found low airflow from a clogged filter and a coil starting to ice. Restoring airflow and letting the coil thaw was the first step. Then we checked operation to make sure the icing was not a symptom of something else.

Why is my AC making a weird noise

ACs are like cars. A new sound is information.

Common noises and what they often point to

  • Buzzing Electrical issue, loose connection, contactor problems, failing capacitor, or debris near the outdoor fan
  • Rattling Loose panel, screws backing out, debris in the condenser, or a fan blade issue
  • Screeching or squealing Motor or blower issues, belt issues on older setups, or bearings wearing
  • Clicking Some clicking is normal at startup. Repeated clicking can be a control or relay issue
  • Hissing Airflow or refrigerant related concerns, sometimes a duct leak or a valve issue

If you hear metal on metal, shut it off and call. That sound is never a compliment.

What causes water leaks around the indoor unit

Water around the air handler is usually a drainage issue, not a your AC is producing too much water issue.

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Cracked drain pan or rusted pan in older units
  • Improper slope or installation issues that show up over time
  • Frozen coil that thaws and overwhelms the drain
  • Disconnected drain line or loose fitting
  • Secondary drain protection or float switch tripping

In Burlingame, we sometimes see drain issues when systems run less frequently, then suddenly run a lot during a warm spell. The line that was fine gets a workout and shows its age.

Why does my breaker trip when the AC turns on

Breaker trips can happen for a few reasons, and the safest approach is to treat it seriously.

What we look at

  • Capacitor condition and compressor start behavior
  • Outdoor fan motor amperage
  • Contactor pitting and electrical resistance
  • Wiring condition and signs of overheating
  • Compressor health indicators
  • Breaker condition and correct sizing for the equipment

If the breaker is tripping, avoid resetting it repeatedly. That can turn a small electrical problem into a bigger one.

How long does AC repair take, and what affects the timing

Some repairs are quick. Some take longer. A good diagnosis usually saves time overall.

  • Access Tight closets, attic installations, or rooftop units take longer to reach and work on safely
  • System condition A well maintained system is easier to diagnose. A system with multiple worn parts can take more time to sort out
  • Parts availability Some common electrical parts are straightforward. Less common components may require ordering
  • Environmental factors Heat, wind, or coastal moisture can complicate outdoor work
  • Prior modifications Systems that have been creative over the years can take longer

We will walk you through what we are seeing on site so timing makes sense, not guesses.

You do not need to memorize any of that. The point is we confirm the issue before we touch parts.

How to keep your AC from acting up again

No one wants to babysit an AC. A few habits can prevent a lot of common problems.

  • Replace filters on a consistent schedule that matches your home and pets
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of leaves and debris
  • Do not block returns with furniture
  • Pay attention to new sounds and changes in airflow
  • If a room suddenly feels different, check the vent and listen for duct noise
  • After power outages, watch for odd cycling behavior

If you want, we can also point out airflow bottlenecks we notice during the repair, even if they are not the main reason you called.
