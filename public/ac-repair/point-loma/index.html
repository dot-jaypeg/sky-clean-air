My AC is running but not cooling

This is the classic It is on but it is not doing anything complaint. A few likely causes are listed here. If the equipment is aging or the fix does not make sense long term, we can also discuss AC Replacement in Point Loma, CA as a next step.

  • Filter is clogged, choking airflow
  • Outdoor unit is not running, or the fan is not spinning
  • Coil is dirty and cannot transfer heat
  • Refrigerant charge is off, often tied to a leak
  • Duct leakage is dumping cool air into the attic
  • Thermostat settings or sensor placement issues

You might say It is set to 72 and it is 78 inside and it is trying really hard. Trying hard can still mean not moving enough air or not rejecting heat outdoors. We check both sides of the system.

My AC keeps turning on and off

Short cycling can be annoying and hard on equipment. It can also make humidity control worse, which is a big deal near the coast.

  • Oversized system for the space

  • Dirty condenser coil causing high pressure trips
  • Low airflow causing coil freeze and safety trips
  • Electrical parts that are failing intermittently

In Point Loma, we see thermostat placement issues in open layouts and remodeled homes. A thermostat near the kitchen or a sunny window can act like it is in a different house.

Why is my indoor unit leaking water

Water around the air handler is usually a drain problem, not mystery water. Common causes include these items.

  • Drain line blockage from dust and buildup
  • Drain pan issues on older units
  • Improper slope on the drain line
  • Frozen coil melting during off cycles, often caused by low airflow

If you see water, turn the system off and let us know what you see. If the unit is in a closet, put a towel down so it does not soak the baseboards.

What to do before the technician arrives

A few small steps help the visit go smoother.

  • Make sure the thermostat is accessible

  • Clear a path to the indoor unit and outdoor unit
