What if the AC will not turn on at all

Why is my AC making weird noises

Noises are your system’s way of complaining. Some are harmless. Some are a warning.

  • Rattling may point to loose panels screws or debris in the outdoor unit
  • Buzzing may point to electrical component strain contactor issues or fan motor trouble
  • Screeching may point to blower motor or fan motor bearing problems
  • Clicking may point to relay activity thermostat calls or a failing component cycling
  • Thumping may point to a loose blower wheel duct movement or mounting issues

If the sound shows up more at night when it is quiet in Morena, it can feel extra loud. A quick inspection can usually tell if it is a loose panel or something that needs parts and repair.

What does frozen coil mean and what should I do

A frozen coil happens when the indoor coil gets too cold and moisture turns to ice. Then airflow drops and cooling gets worse, which can spiral.

What you may notice

  • Weak airflow from vents
  • Indoor unit making a hissing or dripping sound
  • Ice on refrigerant lines near the indoor unit
  • Water around the unit after it thaws

What to do right away

  • Turn cooling off
  • Set the fan to on if it will run
  • Check the filter and replace if dirty
  • Let the ice thaw before running cooling again

Then schedule service. Frozen coils are usually linked to airflow problems or refrigerant related issues. If you keep forcing it to run, you can create more damage.
