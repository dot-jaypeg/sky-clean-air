Why is my AC running but not cooling

If the system is running and you still feel warm air, it is usually one of these buckets.

  • Airflow problem A dirty filter blocked return closed registers or a struggling blower can keep cold air from moving
  • Outdoor unit not rejecting heat A dirty condenser coil or a fan that is not moving enough air can cause high temperatures and poor cooling
  • Refrigerant related issue Low refrigerant from a leak or a metering issue can reduce cooling and may lead to icing
  • Electrical issue under load Components can kind of run until the hottest part of the day then fail

You might hear this at the door. Homeowner It is on so it should be cooling right. Tech It should. Let us see what it is actually doing not just what it is saying it is doing.

What does it mean if the AC keeps turning on and off

Short cycling is when the system starts runs briefly then shuts off and repeats. It can make the house feel clammy and it can stress parts.

Common causes include

  • Thermostat placement issues or thermostat faults
  • Oversized equipment for the space
  • Dirty coils or restricted airflow leading to overheating or freeze protection events
  • Low refrigerant conditions
  • Electrical controls or safety switches doing their job because something else is wrong

In Jamacha Lomita, we also see short cycling after homeowners close too many vents in unused rooms. It seems logical to push more air where you want it but it can change system pressures and airflow in ways the unit does not like.

What is that noise coming from the outdoor unit

Air conditioners make normal sounds. They should not sound like a toolbox falling down a staircase.

Noises and what they often point to

  • Buzzing Electrical issues contactor chatter or a failing capacitor
  • Rattling Loose panel debris in the condenser or fan blade issues
  • Screeching or squealing Motor bearing trouble or a belt issue on certain systems
  • Clicking Normal relay action can click but repeated clicking while failing to start is a red flag

If you are near Sweetwater Reservoir area and you notice more leaves and debris around the outdoor unit after windy days, a quick visual check can explain a lot. If you see sticks or seed pods inside the fan guard area, it is time to shut it off and have it inspected.

Why is water leaking from my indoor unit

Water around the air handler is a common surprise puddle in garages closets and hallway utility spaces. In Jamacha Lomita, warm days and longer run times mean more condensation, so drain issues show up fast.

Typical causes

  • Clogged condensate drain line
  • Cracked drain pan or rusted pan on older systems
  • Poor slope or drain connection problems
  • Frozen evaporator coil that melts and overwhelms the pan
  • Condensate pump failure if your setup uses one

What you can do right away

  • Turn the system off to limit overflow
  • Check the filter and replace it if it is heavily loaded
  • Keep towels down and clear valuables away from the area

If the system has a safety float switch, it may shut cooling off to prevent water damage. That feels like the AC just stopped but it is often the system trying to save your floor.

Should I repair the AC or think about replacing it

If you are on the fence, we can help you look at it logically. Repair makes sense when the issue is isolated and the rest of the system is stable. What can I check before scheduling AC repair

You do not need a tool belt to do a few safe checks.

Try these quick steps

  • Confirm the thermostat is set to cool and the temperature is set below room temp
  • Replace the air filter if it looks gray and loaded
  • Make sure supply vents and the big return grille are not blocked by furniture
  • Check the breaker is not tripped and that the outdoor disconnect is on
  • Look at the outdoor unit and clear leaves and debris from around it
  • If the indoor unit is leaking, turn the system off

Avoid these

  • Do not open sealed refrigerant lines
  • Do not keep resetting a breaker that keeps tripping
  • Do not run the AC if you see ice on the refrigerant line or indoor coil area

If you tell us what you found, it helps us show up prepared. Filter changed breaker is on outdoor fan is not spinning is music to a technician’s ears.

How do you diagnose refrigerant and airflow issues

Cooling problems are often a tug of war between refrigerant behavior and airflow. Getting it right means measuring and observing, not guessing.

We may check

  • Temperature split between return air and supply air
  • Static pressure indicators to spot airflow restriction
  • Blower performance and cleanliness
  • Coil condition and signs of icing or oil residue
  • Outdoor coil condition and heat rejection
  • Electrical readings on motors and controls
  • System run patterns over a steady period
