Dryer Vent Services in San Diego & Orange County

If towels need two cycles, the dryer probably isn't the problem. The vent behind it is. We clean, fix and reroute dryer vents across SD and OC.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

The lint screen catches a lot, but not everything. The rest ends up in the duct that runs from the back of your dryer to the outside of the house, sometimes a few feet through a wall, sometimes a long way up through the attic to a roof cap. Over time that line narrows, the hot, damp air has nowhere to go, and your dryer runs longer and hotter to get the same load dry.

That's more than an inconvenience. Lint is highly flammable, and buildup in dryer vents is one of the leading causes of home dryer fires. Keeping the vent clear is one of the easier safety jobs in a house, and one of the most often forgotten.

Picking the right service

Here's how to match what you're seeing to what you need:

  • Clothes take longer to dry, the dryer is hot to the touch, the laundry room feels steamy. Start with dryer vent cleaning. It's the most common fix and often the only one needed.
  • The vent is crushed, disconnected, or made of the old plastic or foil accordion hose. That's dryer vent repair. We replace damaged sections and swap unsafe material for metal.
  • The run is very long, has a lot of bends, or dips where water and wet lint collect. Rerouting the line to a shorter, straighter path can fix chronic slow drying for good.
  • The run can't be shortened, like in a condo or a home where the laundry room is in the middle of the house. A booster fan helps push air the full distance.
  • Everything is working and you want to keep it that way. Regular dryer vent maintenance keeps you on a schedule so buildup never gets bad.

Local homes, local quirks

A lot of townhomes and condos in places like Mission Valley, Carmel Valley and Irvine vent up to the roof, where caps and dampers jam with lint. Beach neighborhoods from Imperial Beach to San Clemente get salt and fine sand caked on exterior vent hoods, and lint sticks to that rough surface fast. Older homes in North Park and South Park sometimes run their vents through crawlspaces with low spots that hold moisture. We see all of it regularly.

Dryer Vents FAQs

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

About once a year works for most households. Big families, pet owners and homes with long vent runs may need it more often.

Is a clogged dryer vent really a fire risk?

Yes. Lint burns easily, and a clogged vent traps heat right where the lint is. Buildup in the vent is one of the leading causes of dryer fires in homes.

Can I just clean the lint trap?

You should clean it every load. But the lint trap doesn't catch everything, and what gets past it builds up in the vent line where you can't reach.

Do you service dryer vents in apartments and businesses?

Yes. We work in single-family homes, condos, rentals and commercial buildings. See our commercial dryer vent cleaning page for businesses.
