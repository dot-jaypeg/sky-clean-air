Commercial HVAC & Air Services in San Diego & Orange County

For offices, shops, restaurants and multi-unit buildings: HVAC, rooftop units, ducts, dryer vents and air quality, scheduled so your business keeps running.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

When the building is the business

A warm office is an inconvenience. A dining room at 82 degrees on a Friday night, or a laundromat whose dryers take twice as long because the vents are packed with lint, is lost money. Commercial customers need something slightly different from homeowners: clear communication, work that fits around operating hours, and someone who shows up for the maintenance visit as reliably as for the emergency.

Sky Clean Air works with businesses and property managers across San Diego County, from Kearny Mesa and Sorrento Valley office parks to storefronts in Escondido and Chula Vista, and now across Orange County as well.

Who we work with

  • Offices and professional suites with split systems, zoning and thermostat control across multiple rooms
  • Retail and storefronts where comfort at the front door affects how long customers stay
  • Restaurants dealing with kitchen heat, grease and heavy system use
  • Property managers and HOAs responsible for many units and one maintenance budget
  • Multifamily and laundromats with stacked dryer vents, shared duct runs and high-volume laundry

What we handle

HVAC equipment, including rooftop and package units

Many commercial buildings here run on rooftop units or package units that sit on the roof in full sun, and, near the coast, in salt air. We repair, maintain and replace them, along with split systems, mini-splits and controls. Regular commercial maintenance is the best way to avoid finding out about a problem on the hottest day of the year.

Air ducts

Offices and retail spaces collect dust, and restaurants add grease and odor. Commercial duct cleaning, repair, leakage testing and balancing help with airflow, comfort complaints and the "one room is always freezing" problem.

Dryer vents for laundromats and multifamily

Long, shared or stacked dryer vent runs clog faster than residential ones, and clogged vents mean slower drying, higher energy use and a real fire risk. We handle commercial dryer vent cleaning, repair, rerouting and booster fans.

Attic, insulation and air quality

We also do commercial attic inspections, insulation, air sealing and UV system service when a building needs it.

Scheduling around your business

We know you can't shut a restaurant down at lunch or send an office home for the afternoon. When we plan work, we ask about your hours, busiest times and any tenants who need notice, and we try to schedule around them. Our office is open weekdays 8am to 6pm and Saturdays 8am to 3pm, and Saturday visits can be a good option for offices that are closed on weekends. For multi-unit buildings, we can coordinate with property managers on access and tenant notices.

Why businesses choose Sky Clean Air

We're a family-owned company, founded in San Diego in 2018 and now running nearly 15 vehicles. We're licensed and insured under CA license #1036543, with EPA-certified technicians, and rated 5 stars across roughly 1,000 Google and Yelp reviews. You'll get a written price before work starts, honest advice about whether equipment should be repaired or replaced, and a team that's easy to reach.

Commercial FAQs

Do you service rooftop units?

Yes. We repair, maintain and replace rooftop and package units, as well as commercial split systems and mini-splits.

Can you work outside our busiest hours?

We'll plan around your schedule as closely as we can, including Saturday appointments. Tell us your hours and constraints when you call, and we'll work out timing that keeps disruption down.

Do you work with property managers on multi-unit buildings?

Yes. We coordinate with property managers and HOAs on access, tenant notices and scheduling across units, for duct cleaning, dryer vents, HVAC service and more.

How often should commercial dryer vents be cleaned?

It depends on how heavily the dryers are used. A busy laundromat or a building with shared laundry needs far more frequent cleaning than a household. We'll look at your setup and usage and recommend a schedule.

Do you serve businesses in Orange County?

Yes. We serve commercial customers across San Diego County and have expanded into Orange County, from San Clemente to Brea.
