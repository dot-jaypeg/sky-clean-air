Why upgrade your thermostat now

San Diego weather can be mild one day and sticky or hot the next. Coastal mornings bring marine layer, inland afternoons push temperatures up, and Santa Ana winds can hit hard. Your thermostat should keep up without constant tinkering.

A basic switch on the wall cannot do all that. A smart model, or even a simple programmable unit, can cut waste and smooth out hot and cold spots. With the right setup, you get comfort and control without fuss. For help with schedules you can also see our Thermostat Programming service.

Thermostat placement tips that matter

Placement affects comfort and bills. Poor placement can trick your system.

Keep your thermostat

  • About 52 to 60 inches from the floor
  • On an interior wall
  • Away from direct sun from west facing windows
  • Away from supply vents and return grilles
  • Away from kitchens and appliances that give off heat
  • Far from doors that open to the outside or the garage
  • Not under attic access where heat can pool

That sunny wall in Pacific Beach might look nice. The thermostat does not care about looks. It cares about stable air. Sun tricks it into reading hot, then your AC runs too long. Let us move it if it sits in a bad spot.

Better settings for San Diego comfort and savings

Cooling setpoints

  • Aim for 76 to 78 during the day if you are home
  • Raise to 80 to 82 when you are out
  • Cool the home ahead of peak rates, then coast

Heating setpoints

  • 68 to 70 while home
  • 64 to 66 while asleep or away

Schedules

  • Weekday and weekend routines are often different, we set both
  • Ramp up or down 30 to 60 minutes before you wake or arrive

Fan settings

  • Auto for most homes
  • Circulate mode can help even temps in multi story homes

Filter reminders

  • Every 1 to 3 months for basic filters
  • More often during wildfire smoke or dusty Santa Ana periods

Room sensors

  • Use a sensor in the room you care about most at that time of day
  • Bedroom at night, living room by day

Vacation mode

  • One tap to save energy when you travel
