What does an AC installation include

An installation is more than placing a condenser outside and calling it good. Done correctly, it is a set of checks and connections that help the system operate the way it should in your specific building.

  • A site visit to review your current setup and available space
  • Equipment selection based on the building needs and electrical requirements
  • Setting and securing the outdoor unit on an appropriate pad or base
  • Installing or connecting the indoor coil or air handler
  • Connecting refrigerant lines and condensate drainage
  • Electrical connections and safety checks
  • Thermostat setup and basic user walk through
  • Startup testing to confirm cooling operation and airflow

If we see issues that can affect performance, we talk about them early. That might be duct leakage a return air problem or a condensate drain that is not routed well. Depending on what we find, Air Duct Repair in National City, CA or Air Duct Maintenance in National City, CA may be part of the conversation.

How do you size an AC for a National City home or business

Sizing is where comfort begins. Too small and it struggles. Too large and it may cycle on and off more than you would like. In National City, we also pay attention to the coastal influence and the way sun hits the building. A west facing living room can feel very different than a shaded unit closer to the bay.

  • Square footage and ceiling height
  • Insulation and window type
  • How much sun the building takes in the afternoon
  • Number of occupants and heat sources like cooking or equipment
  • Existing duct layout and return air capacity
  • Areas that always run warm like upstairs bedrooms or back offices

If the duct system cannot move the air the new unit needs, we will tell you. Installing a strong system onto weak airflow is like putting a big engine in a car with a clogged fuel line.
