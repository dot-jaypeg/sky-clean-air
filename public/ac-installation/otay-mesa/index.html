How we determine the right system size

AC sizing is about matching the building heat load and airflow reality, not guessing. We look at square footage, ceiling height, insulation, windows, sun exposure, occupancy, and heat producing equipment. We also review existing duct size and layout if you have central air, and we note rooms that run hotter like upstairs bedrooms or server closets. System options that may fit your space

There is no single right answer. The best match depends on your space, existing ductwork, and how you use the building. If you already have ducts and want a whole building approach, Central AC Installation in Otay Mesa, CA may be a fit. If ducts are not practical, a ductless option may be the better path.

Central split system

A common choice for single family homes, townhomes, and offices with ductwork already in place. It uses an outdoor condenser and an indoor coil connected to a furnace or air handler, and it works best when ducts are in decent shape and sized appropriately.

Ductless mini split

Common in additions, converted garages, bonus rooms, and small offices. It can support room by room comfort control and avoids major duct runs when adding ducts would be disruptive.

Heat pump options

Some installations are paired with heat pump systems that provide both cooling and heating. We can review what changes in equipment and wiring may be involved based on your current setup.

Replace only the outdoor unit or the full system

A matched system matters because the indoor coil and outdoor condenser are designed to work together. Mixing old and new parts can cause performance issues, control problems, or more wear. We usually start by checking the age and condition of the indoor coil or air handler, refrigerant type, duct condition and airflow restrictions, and signs of past leaks, corrosion, or drain problems. If the system is not performing well today, AC Repair in Otay Mesa, CA can help identify whether repair, replacement, or a full install plan makes the most sense.

What installation day looks like

Most installs follow a clear sequence. We confirm equipment location, access, electrical needs, and drain routing. We protect the space and keep the work area tidy.
