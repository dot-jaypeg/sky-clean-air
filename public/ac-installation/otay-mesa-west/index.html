Will you need to modify ductwork

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. Ductwork is the delivery system. Installing a new AC onto ductwork that leaks or is undersized is like pouring coffee into a cracked mug.

  • Return grilles that are too small for the airflow needed

  • Older flex duct with kinks that choke airflow
  • Supply vents that were added over time without balancing
