How we size an AC system for your house

Sizing is not guesswork, and it is not just bigger is better. An oversized system can short cycle, feel clammy, and wear parts faster. An undersized system can run longer and still miss the comfort target on warmer days. If you already have a system that feels off, AC Repair in Allied Gardens, CA can help identify issues that are not strictly sizing related.

  • Square footage and ceiling height
  • Insulation levels and attic conditions
  • Window size, orientation, and shading
  • Number of occupants and typical schedules
  • Room layout and open concept areas
  • Duct condition and airflow limits if you have ducts

A common Allied Gardens scenario is a remodel that opened up the kitchen and living room. The old unit might have been sized for smaller rooms. Once walls come down, airflow patterns change. We account for those changes instead of repeating the old setup.

Do you need new ductwork for a new AC installation

Not always. Some duct systems are in decent shape and can be reused with adjustments. Others have leaks, poor returns, crushed runs, or old insulation that no longer performs well. If you are replacing an older system, ductwork is worth a close look. When duct issues show up, we may recommend Air Duct Repair in Allied Gardens, CA or Air Duct Replacement in Allied Gardens, CA based on what we find.

Signs your ductwork may need attention

  • Weak airflow from certain vents
  • Dusty buildup around supply registers
  • Musty smells when the system turns on
  • Noisy whistling or rattling from ducts
  • A return grille that seems undersized for the house

Sky Clean Air started in services that revolve around clean airflow, so we pay close attention to how air moves through the home.
