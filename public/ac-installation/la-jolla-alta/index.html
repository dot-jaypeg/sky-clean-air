What AC installation includes

Most people think AC installation is just setting a unit in place and flipping a switch. Real life is more hands on. A proper install connects the equipment to your home airflow and electrical setup, then checks operation so the unit is not fighting the house.

Typical steps

  • A site visit to look at your layout, insulation clues, sun exposure, and existing HVAC equipment
  • System selection support based on your space and comfort goals
  • Confirming equipment location for the indoor and outdoor components
  • Setting or updating the pad, mounts, and line routing as needed
  • Electrical connections and safety shutoffs where required
  • Connecting refrigerant lines and verifying correct operation
  • Condensate drain planning so water goes where it should
  • Airflow checks at key vents and basic thermostat setup
  • Clean up, haul away planning if requested, and a quick owner walkthrough

How we figure out the right size AC

AC sizing is not guesswork and it is not based on what your neighbor installed. We look at the home itself. If the current unit struggles, AC Repair in La Jolla Alta, CA can sometimes reveal airflow or control issues that also matter for sizing decisions.
