What counts as AC installation and what does it include

An installation is more than swapping a metal box and calling it a day. A proper AC install connects the indoor and outdoor equipment correctly, sets airflow, confirms refrigerant charge, and checks that the system is matched to your space and electrical setup.

Typical AC installation steps include

  • A walkthrough of your home or business and a quick comfort chat
  • Equipment selection based on layout, insulation, windows, and usage
  • Removal of old equipment if needed, with safe handling and cleanup
  • Setting the outdoor unit on a stable pad or stand
  • Installing or connecting the indoor unit
  • Split system coil at the furnace or air handler
  • Or a ductless wall unit if you are going ductless
  • Running and protecting the line set and drain
  • Electrical connections and basic safety checks
  • Airflow setup and temperature split checks
  • Final startup, controls check, and homeowner walkthrough

How do you size an AC for my home

Sizing is where comfort starts. Too small and it runs constantly. Too large and it can cool fast but feel clammy and uneven.

We look at

  • Square footage and ceiling height
  • Insulation and attic conditions
  • Window size, age, and sun exposure
  • Number of occupants and cooking habits
  • Duct layout and return air paths
  • Rooms that run hot, like west facing spaces

A short dialogue we hear a lot in Rolando. Homeowner it is a 1,400 square foot house. So I just need whatever size that matches, right. Us maybe. But that west living room window and that low attic insulation have opinions too.
