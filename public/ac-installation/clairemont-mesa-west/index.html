Should you keep your existing ductwork or replace it

If the duct system needs attention, we may recommend Air Duct Maintenance in Clairemont Mesa West, CA or Air Duct Cleaning in Clairemont Mesa West, CA depending on what we find.

Keeping ducts can work well when

What happens during an AC installation appointment

An AC installation is not just setting a box outside and calling it a day. The steps vary by system type, yet the core flow stays similar. Some homes also schedule follow up AC Maintenance in Clairemont Mesa West, CA to help keep performance consistent.

Typical installation steps

  • Site preparation and protection of floors and work areas
  • Safe shutoff and removal or retirement of old equipment when applicable
  • Set the new outdoor unit on a stable pad or support
  • Install or connect the indoor coil, air handler, or furnace interface
  • Connect refrigerant lines, drain piping, and electrical connections
  • Address duct transitions and airflow details at the plenum or air handler
  • Start up the system and confirm operation
  • Verify temperatures, airflow behavior, and basic controls
  • Walkthrough with you so you know what to listen for and how to run it

Small detail, big difference

Condensate drain routing matters. A poorly routed drain can cause water issues or nuisance shutoffs. We take the time to set it up cleanly and check flow.

What is the difference between central AC and ductless mini splits here

Both can work well in Clairemont Mesa West. The right choice depends on your home layout, existing ducts, and how you want to control temperatures. If you are considering a whole home approach, we can also talk through Central AC Installation in Clairemont Mesa West, CA.

Central AC tends to fit when

Ductless mini splits tend to fit when
