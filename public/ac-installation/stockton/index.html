What to expect during an AC installation appointment

Most installs go smoother when you know what the day looks like. We like to keep it simple and predictable.

  • Walkthrough and measurements. We look at the layout, insulation basics, windows, and where the thermostat lives
  • Existing system review. We check the current equipment, duct connections, electrical capacity, and condenser location
  • System selection and plan. We discuss equipment type and what makes sense for your home or building use
  • Prep work. This can include pad work, disconnect planning, protection for floors, and safe access
  • Installation and connections. Setting the outdoor unit, placing the indoor coil or air handler, connecting refrigerant lines, drain, electrical, and controls
  • Airflow and performance checks. Supply and return airflow checks, temperature split checks, and basic control setup
  • Cleanup and owner walk through. Filter access, thermostat basics, and what to listen for during the first week

You will hear a little shop talk. You might also hear a classic line like “Let’s make sure this return is not trying to drink a milkshake through a coffee straw.” That is our way of saying airflow matters, and it is also why Air Duct Maintenance in Stockton, CA can be part of the bigger comfort picture.

How we choose the right AC size for a Stockton home

Bigger is not always better. An oversized unit can short cycle, which can feel clammy and uneven. An undersized unit can run long and still miss the mark on hot days.

Sizing is based on more than square footage. We look at these factors.

  • Ceiling height and open floor plans
  • Insulation condition, attic heat load, and sun exposure
  • Number and size of windows, especially west facing
  • Duct layout and return air paths
  • How you use the space, like a busy kitchen or a home office with equipment running

Stockton has a mix of older homes near Miracle Mile and newer builds out by Spanos Park. Two houses with the same square footage can need different approaches based on shade, attic temps, and duct routing.

System types that work well in Stockton

There is no single right answer. The right system is the one that fits the building and the way you live or work. If you are planning a whole home setup, we can also discuss Central AC Installation in Stockton, CA.

  • Split system AC with furnace. Outdoor condenser plus indoor coil paired with an existing furnace
  • Heat pump system. Handles cooling and heating with one system
  • Ductless mini split. Useful for add ons, converted garages, small offices, or rooms that never cool evenly
  • Packaged units for some commercial spaces. More common on rooftops for light commercial buildings depending on the property setup

If you are unsure, we walk through pros and cons based on your building. No guesswork. Just practical tradeoffs.

Will a new AC fix hot and cold spots

It can, but not always by itself. Uneven temperatures are usually a mix of system sizing, duct design, and how air returns to the equipment.

Common causes of hot spots in Stockton homes include these patterns.

  • Long duct runs to the back of the house with weak airflow
  • Not enough return air, especially in hallways
  • Duct leaks in hot attics
  • Closed doors with no return path
  • Sun exposure on west facing rooms in late afternoon

Sometimes the fix is simple, like balancing airflow or adding a return path. Sometimes it means duct modifications. We will point out what we see and explain the why in plain language.

Outdoor unit placement on your property

  • Drainage and pad stability
  • Line set routing that avoids sharp bends and protects insulation
  • Access for future service without crawling through rose bushes

In Stockton, we often see side yard placements along fences, especially in tighter lots. We make sure there is room to breathe and room to work.
