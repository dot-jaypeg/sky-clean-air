How do you figure out the right size AC for my home

Sizing is where good installs begin. Too small and it runs all day. Too large and it can cool fast without properly handling comfort details like consistent airflow. Either way, you end up fiddling with the thermostat and saying things like Why is it cold and clammy in here.

We look at practical factors, including.

  • Square footage and ceiling height
  • Sun exposure and window layout
  • Insulation condition, especially in older attics
  • Number of occupants and typical use patterns
  • Duct layout and return air placement
  • Whether certain rooms always run warmer

A small example from the neighborhood. A two story home near Tecolote Canyon can have upstairs rooms that soak up afternoon heat. Even with a strong system, the upstairs may lag if returns are limited or supply runs are long. In those cases, sizing alone is not the whole story, and options like Air Duct Installation in Clairemont Mesa East, CA may come up during planning.
