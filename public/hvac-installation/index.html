Ductwork the quiet hero of comfort

Even the best equipment cannot fix weak or leaky ductwork. In many San Diego homes ducts lose air through gaps or crushed runs. That means uneven rooms and higher bills.

What we check

  • Size and layout of duct runs
  • Leaks at joints and boots with Ductwork Inspection
  • Insulation quality
  • Return air capacity for balanced airflow
  • Supply registers and placement

Fixes that make a real difference

  • Sealing all joints to cut leaks
  • Replacing old or damaged runs
  • Adding returns to improve airflow with expert balancing
  • Upgrading insulation on ducts in hot attics
  • Balancing dampers to even out rooms

As an air duct cleaning service we do more than install equipment. We clean seal and set up the duct system so your new unit can shine. That combo helps with dust noise and comfort.

Replacement vs repair how to decide

Not sure if you should repair or replace? Think about these points.

  • Age. Systems over 12 to 15 years often cost more to keep running
  • Refrigerant. Older units with discontinued refrigerants cost more to service
  • Comfort. Uneven rooms or nonstop running point to deeper issues
  • Bills. Rising energy use even after a tune up is a sign
  • Noise. Grinding or rattling signals worn parts or poor airflow
  • Frequent repairs. If repair costs keep stacking up a new unit may save money long term

We will check your system and give you honest advice. If a simple fix makes sense we will say so. Explore HVAC System Repair and HVAC System Replacement options.

Your ductwork and air sealing plan

San Diego has many homes where ducts run through hot attics. Leaky ducts pull in dusty attic air then push it into your home. That adds to allergy issues and wastes energy.

Our crew seals ducts with long lasting products not flimsy tape. We support runs to prevent sagging and crimping. Where returns are small we add or enlarge them for better airflow. If ducts are shot we replace them with the right sizes for your system. Ask about Duct Sealing and Air Duct Installation.

Pairing new equipment with clean sealed ducts is a smart move. Your system breathes better and your home stays cleaner.

Maintenance tips for your new system

A little care keeps your system running strong.

  • Change filters on schedule usually every 1 to 3 months
  • Keep plants and clutter at least 2 feet away from the outdoor unit
  • Rinse the outdoor coil gently if it gets dusty or salty especially near the coast
  • Set your thermostat and give it time. Short bursts of changes can create waste
  • Book seasonal tune ups to keep parts clean and within spec

Want reminders? We can set up a service plan so you do not have to think about it. Ask about HVAC Maintenance.

Small touches that make a big difference

  • Low profile line set covers to keep the exterior clean
  • Quiet pads under outdoor units to reduce vibration
  • Condensate safety switches to protect against leaks
  • Thoughtful thermostat placement away from windows or ovens
  • Shoe covers and drop cloths to protect your floors

HVAC Installation: Frequently Asked Questions

What is an HVAC system?

An HVAC system controls the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning in your home or business.

How do I know if I need a new HVAC system?

If your system is old, not working efficiently, or needing frequent repairs, it may be time for a replacement.

How long does HVAC installation take?

Most installations can be completed in one to two days, depending on your space and setup.

Can Sky Clean Air install an HVAC system in any type of building?

Yes, Sky Clean Air provides HVAC installation services for homes and commercial properties.

Will I need to leave my house during installation?

In most cases, you can stay at home during the installation process.

What should I do to prepare for installation?

Clear the area around where the system will be installed and make sure pets and valuable items are out of the way.

Is regular maintenance needed after installation?

Yes, regular maintenance helps keep your HVAC running smoothly and efficiently.

Can I get advice on which system is right for my needs?

Absolutely! Sky Clean Air can help you choose the best system for your space and budget.

Does Sky Clean Air remove old HVAC systems during installation?
