What counts as air duct cleaning

Air duct cleaning is not just vacuuming a vent cover and calling it done. The goal is to address the pathway air travels through, plus the spots that commonly collect debris.

A typical appointment focuses on areas like these.

  • Supply ducts that deliver air to rooms
  • Return ducts that pull air back to the system
  • Vent openings and grilles
  • Main trunk lines where debris often settles
  • The air handler area that connects to the ductwork
  • Registers in high use rooms like living rooms and bedrooms

How do you know your ducts might be dirty

Some signs are obvious. Others are the kind that make you shrug until they stack up.

Return vents and supply vents

Supply vents push conditioned air into rooms. Returns pull air back so the system can cycle it again. Returns often collect more visible dust because they are pulling air in constantly.

A quick rule of thumb is that dust at supply vents can mean buildup inside the run, and heavy dust at returns can mean the return pathway is collecting normal household debris fast.

La Presa has a mix of single family homes and multi unit living. Shared walls and compact mechanical closets can change access and how duct runs are arranged.
