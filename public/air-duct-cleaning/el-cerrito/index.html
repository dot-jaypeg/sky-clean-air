What air duct cleaning really covers

Your HVAC system pulls air in through return vents, conditions it, and sends it back out through supply vents. Over time, particles can collect inside the ductwork. Some of it is normal household dust. Some of it is renovation debris, pet hair, or the mystery crumbs that seem to teleport from the couch.

Air duct cleaning focuses on the air pathways, not just the vent covers. A wiped vent grille looks nice, but it does not address what may be sitting several feet into the line. How to tell if ducts are actually dirty

Some signs are visible. Some are not. Here are a few practical checks you can do in an El Cerrito home without turning it into a weekend project.

  • Remove one vent grille and look inside with a flashlight. If you see clumps of lint or thick buildup right away, that is a clue
  • Check the return area. Returns often show buildup sooner than supplies
  • Notice patterns. Dust collecting fast near vents, or dark smudges around vent edges, can point to airflow and filtration issues
  • Pay attention when the system starts. If you smell a dusty puff when the HVAC kicks on, it is worth investigating
