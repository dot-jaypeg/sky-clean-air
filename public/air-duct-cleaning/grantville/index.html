What air duct cleaning includes

Most folks think of the vent covers you can see. The real work happens in the duct runs behind the walls and ceilings, plus the main trunk lines that feed multiple rooms. A typical Air Duct Cleaning visit in Grantville focuses on the areas that affect airflow and dust movement.

  • Supply ducts that deliver air into rooms
  • Return ducts that pull air back to the system
  • Registers and grilles on walls, floors, or ceilings
  • Main duct lines where dust tends to settle
  • A check for obvious restrictions like construction debris, heavy lint, or loose materials

If you have ever pulled a vent cover and thought, well that is not great, you are not alone. We hear it often, especially when there has been remodeling or when return paths pull in extra lint and dust.
