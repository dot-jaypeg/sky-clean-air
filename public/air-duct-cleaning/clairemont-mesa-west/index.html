What air duct cleaning is

Your HVAC system breathes through return vents, pushes air through ducts, and delivers it out of supply vents. Over time, normal household life leaves behind buildup. Air duct cleaning focuses on the inside of the ductwork, plus the vent openings where debris collects. It is not the same thing as changing a filter. Filters matter a lot, but they do not clean the duct interior.
