What counts as air duct cleaning and what gets cleaned

People hear duct cleaning and picture a single pipe behind a wall. In reality, most systems have a few main pieces that can collect debris over time. A typical air duct cleaning service in Old Town focuses on areas like these.

  • Supply ducts that push air out to rooms
  • Return ducts that pull air back to the HVAC system
  • Registers and grilles at the floor, wall, or ceiling
  • Main trunk lines that connect the system
  • Air handler area that interfaces with the ductwork depending on access

What is the difference between duct cleaning and changing the filter

Changing the filter helps capture particles before they reach the HVAC equipment and ductwork. Duct cleaning addresses material that is already inside the ducts or lodged at vents and returns.

Task What it targets When it helps most
Filter change Airborne particles moving through the system Ongoing maintenance and dust control
Air duct cleaning Debris already in ductwork and at vents Visible buildup, odors at startup, post remodel cleanup
