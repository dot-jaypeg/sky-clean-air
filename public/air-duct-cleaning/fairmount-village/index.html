How to tell if your ducts need cleaning

Some homes can go a long time with decent filtration and minimal dust sources. Other homes collect debris fast. If you are wondering whether your ductwork needs attention, look for signals you can verify without guesswork.

Signs that often point to duct buildup include the following signs.

  • Dust puffs out of a supply vent when the system starts
  • You see matted dust or pet hair just inside a register
  • A return grille looks like it is wearing a fuzzy sweater
  • You recently had flooring, drywall, or kitchen work done
  • Your home sat vacant for a while with the HVAC off
  • You notice odors that show up only when air starts moving

A quick at home check that is not fancy. Remove one supply vent cover with a screwdriver and take a photo inside with your phone flashlight. If you see a light coating, that is normal. If you see clumps, construction dust, or heavy debris, it is worth a closer look.

How to keep ducts cleaner longer

You do not need to become an HVAC hobbyist. A few habits go a long way.

Simple steps that help include the following steps.

  • Change filters on a consistent schedule that matches your home and system use
  • Use the correct filter size so air does not bypass the edges
  • Keep return grilles clear, not blocked by furniture or curtains
  • After remodel work, keep vents covered during sanding and cleanup
  • Vacuum near returns more often, especially if you have pets
  • If a room is always dusty, check window seals and door gaps

What is air duct cleaning, and why do homeowners in Fairmount Village, CA ask about it?

How do I know if my home in Fairmount Village might need air duct cleaning?
