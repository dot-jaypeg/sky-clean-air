What is air duct cleaning and what does it actually do

Your heating and cooling system moves air through supply ducts and pulls air back through return ducts. Over time, that system can collect.

  • Dust and lint
  • Pet hair and dander
  • Drywall dust from past projects
  • Pollen that rides in from open windows
  • Debris that settles near vent openings

Air Duct Cleaning targets the buildup inside the duct lines and around vent registers. It is not a magic wand that changes your whole house overnight, but it can help cut down on the why is there dust again cycle when buildup is part of the problem.

What about dryer vents and bathroom exhausts

People often mix these up with HVAC ducts. They are different systems.

If you are not sure which one is the culprit, a quick look at where the airflow is going usually clears it up. If dust is showing up around your HVAC vents, that points back to ductwork and returns.
