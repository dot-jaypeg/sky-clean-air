Think of your ductwork as the set of hallways your heated and cooled air travels through. Dirty ducts or something else

Some dust is normal in any home. The question is whether the duct system is acting like a storage closet for debris.

Signs that point toward duct buildup

  • Dust blowing out when the system starts
  • Dark smudging around supply vents
  • A return grille that clogs quickly after cleaning
  • Dust accumulating faster than usual even with regular housekeeping

Signs it may be filtration, leakage, or airflow balance

  • Hot and cold spots between rooms
  • Whistling at a vent or a door that slams when the system runs
  • A filter that gets loaded very fast
  • Visible gaps around the return grille or filter slot

A quick home check you can do today is to remove one supply vent cover and look inside with a flashlight, check the return grille area behind it, look at the filter and note size and fit, and if you have attic access look for duct sections that look crushed, disconnected, or sagging. If you see debris that looks like drywall dust or little bits of insulation, that is worth a closer look. What to do before and after

Before we arrive

  • Clear a path to vents and the HVAC unit
  • Move fragile items off shelves near vents if they rattle easily
  • Set pets up in a comfortable room away from the work area
  • Make a quick list of problem rooms and symptoms
  • If you have ceiling vents, make sure we can safely reach them

After the cleaning

  • Replace or maintain your HVAC filter on a regular schedule
  • Dust and vacuum once after the cleaning, especially near vents
  • Keep supply vents open and unblocked by rugs or furniture
  • Check the return grille occasionally for quick buildup

If you are chasing dust, use exhaust fans while cooking, keep doors and windows closed on high dust days near main roads, and seal obvious gaps around the return grille if you see them.
