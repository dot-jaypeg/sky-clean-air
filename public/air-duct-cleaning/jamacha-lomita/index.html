What air duct cleaning includes

Think of your ductwork as the highway your air travels on. Over time, that highway can collect dust, lint, pet hair, and bits of debris. When the system runs, some of that can shift around and end up at supply vents, on furniture, or in the air you breathe.

Air duct cleaning typically focuses on these areas when accessible and safe to reach.

  • Supply ducts and return ducts
  • Vent openings and grilles
  • Accessible duct interiors
  • HVAC air handler area that connects to the ducts when accessible
