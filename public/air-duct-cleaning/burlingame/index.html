What air duct cleaning actually does

Your ductwork is the roadway your air travels. Over time, particles can collect inside, especially near supply runs, return trunks, and around registers. Air duct cleaning focuses on removing that buildup so your system is moving air through cleaner pathways.

People often call after noticing things like the following.

  • Dust returning quickly after cleaning
  • A stale smell when the heater starts up on a cool Burlingame evening
  • Dark dust lines around vent covers
  • Allergy like irritation that seems worse indoors
  • Renovation debris after a remodel near Broadway or El Camino Real

How to tell if your Burlingame home needs duct cleaning
