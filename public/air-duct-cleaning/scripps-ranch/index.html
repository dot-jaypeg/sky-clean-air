How to tell if your ducts need cleaning

Not every comfort problem is a duct problem. Sometimes it is filtration, a loose return grille, gaps around registers, or an HVAC issue. If airflow issues are part of the concern, Air Duct Repair in Scripps Ranch, CA may be worth discussing after a quick check.

Signs that point toward duct cleaning

  • Visible dust buildup inside vents a few inches past the edge
  • Dark streaks around supply vents on ceilings or walls
  • Return grille packed with lint or pet hair even with regular filter changes
  • Debris that blows out when the system starts
  • After a remodel, drywall sanding, or heavy construction nearby

Signs that may be something else

  • One room always hotter or colder than others
  • Whistling vents or rattling ducts
  • High humidity issues or ongoing odor unrelated to HVAC run time
  • A filter that collapses or gets sucked in

If you want to do a quick at home check, remove one supply vent cover and look inside with a flashlight. Do not go digging around. Just look. If you see matted dust layers or chunks, that is useful info to share.
