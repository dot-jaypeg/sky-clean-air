What gets cleaned during air duct cleaning

Most people picture only the hidden ducts in the walls and attic. That is a big part of it, but a proper duct cleaning job focuses on the whole airflow path that collects debris over time.

  • Supply ducts that deliver conditioned air into rooms
  • Return ducts that pull air back to the HVAC system
  • Return grilles and supply registers
  • Main trunk lines where buildup can collect
  • Accessible transition points where dust likes to settle

Should you replace your air filter after duct cleaning

Yes, it is a smart move to start with a clean filter. If your filter is already near the end of its life, replacing it after cleaning helps maintain the improvement and reduces new buildup.

  • Make sure the filter size fits correctly so air is not bypassing it
