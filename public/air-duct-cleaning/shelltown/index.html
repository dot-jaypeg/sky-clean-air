How do ducts get dirty around here

Ductwork is basically a delivery system. If anything gets into the system, it can travel or collect over time.

Common causes we see around Shelltown include.

  • Older or poorly fitting air filters that let more particles pass
  • Gaps around return grilles that pull in wall cavity dust
  • Renovations that create fine drywall dust
  • Heavy pet shedding that rides the airflow
  • High indoor activity, kids, hobbies, frequent cooking
  • Doors and windows opened often during warm days
  • Long periods between filter changes

If you have ever said I swear we changed the filter, you are not alone. We sometimes find filters installed backwards, the wrong size, or sitting with a gap that lets air sneak around it. Sometimes it helps, sometimes it helps a little, and sometimes the bigger issue is elsewhere. That is the honest truth.

How do I know if my ducts need cleaning

You do not need a science lab. A few simple observations can tell you a lot.

Look for signs like.

  • Dusty buildup visible just inside the register boot
  • A stale smell that appears when the system cycles on
  • Dust collecting around vent covers and ceiling edges
  • A recent remodel or flooring project that produced fine debris
  • Pet hair showing up around returns
  • You moved into a new place and do not know the history

If you want a quick DIY check, remove one or two vent covers and take a flashlight look. If you see a light coating, that is common. If you see clumps, heavy mats, or debris piles, it may be time to schedule.

What about older homes and older ductwork in Shelltown

Older properties can have duct layouts that are less direct, with longer runs and more turns. Some have duct sections that are harder to reach. Older returns can also be undersized, which may increase airflow noise and dust pull if there are gaps.

We take a careful approach with older grilles, painted over screws, and fragile vent covers. If something looks like it might crack when removed, we slow down and talk through the safest way to handle it.

Can duct cleaning help with odors

Odors can come from many sources, but ducts can hold dust and debris that carry smells when air passes through.

Common odor complaints we hear in Shelltown include.

  • Cooking odors that linger longer than expected
  • A dusty smell that shows up during the first hot week of the season

Cleaning the duct system can remove built up material that holds odors. If the odor source is elsewhere, like a plumbing issue, a wet crawlspace, or a neglected filter, we will tell you what we are seeing so you can tackle the real cause.

Is duct cleaning messy

It should not be. The entire point is to capture debris, not relocate it onto your couch.
