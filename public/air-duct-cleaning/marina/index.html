What is air duct cleaning really

Your HVAC system pulls air in through return vents, conditions it, then pushes it back out through supply vents. Over time, the inside of those ducts can collect.

  • Dust and lint from everyday life
  • Pet hair and dander
  • Pollen tracked in from outside
  • Drywall dust after renovations
  • Bits of insulation or construction debris in some buildings
  • Odors that like to hang around longer than invited guests

Air duct cleaning focuses on the duct pathways and related components that affect airflow and cleanliness inside the system. The goal is to remove loose buildup so it is not sitting in the air channels your system uses. In some homes, related work like Air Duct Repair in Marina, CA may also come up if there are visible issues with the duct run.

If everything feels normal, you might not think about ducts at all. That is fine. Most people call after noticing a pattern.

Here are common signs we hear about around Marina.

  • Dust shows up again soon after cleaning
  • Some rooms feel stuffier than others, especially back bedrooms

  • You see dust puff out of supply vents when the system turns on
  • You recently moved in and want a reset

  • You run the fan often and allergies feel more noticeable indoors
