What counts as air duct cleaning and what does not

Air duct cleaning focuses on the hidden pathways behind your vent covers, not just the visible grilles.

Air duct cleaning usually includes

  • Supply ducts that send conditioned air into rooms
  • Return ducts that pull air back to the HVAC system
  • Main trunk lines and branch runs
  • Vent openings and register areas
  • Light cleaning of accessible interior surfaces near openings

What it is not

  • A simple wipe of vent covers and calling it a day
  • A fragrance spray to mask odors
  • A replacement for routine HVAC maintenance like filter changes

Supply vents and return vents

This is a common point of confusion and it matters because returns often carry the heavier load of dust.

Supply vents

  • Blow conditioned air into rooms
  • Often located on walls ceilings or floors depending on the home

Return vents

  • Pull air back to the HVAC system
  • Often larger grilles
  • Usually collect more lint hair and fine dust

If you only notice dust around one or two big grilles that is often a return vent doing its job sometimes a little too enthusiastically.

What is air duct cleaning, and why do Beach Barber Tract homeowners ask about it?
