How to tell your ducts might need cleaning

You do not need x ray vision. A few practical clues can point you in the right direction.

Common signs include

  • Dust that shows up quickly on furniture especially near supply vents
  • A stale or musty smell when the HVAC starts
  • Visible debris around vent edges
  • Registers that look dark or fuzzy inside when you shine a flashlight
  • Certain rooms that feel stuffy when the system is running

Here are practical ways to get better results after duct cleaning

  • Use the correct filter size and replace it on a steady schedule

  • Wipe supply vent covers so dust at the edges does not blow into the room
