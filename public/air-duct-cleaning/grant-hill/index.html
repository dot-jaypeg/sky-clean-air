Signs you might need air duct cleaning

Some people call because they see dust. Others call because something just feels off. Here are practical signs that often point to duct buildup.

  • Dust on surfaces returns quickly after cleaning
  • Musty or stale odor when the system starts
  • Vents that blow out visible particles on startup
  • A room that feels stuffy compared to the rest of the house
  • You recently moved in and do not know the maintenance history
  • You had construction, flooring work, or cabinet work done
  • Allergy irritation that seems worse indoors than outdoors
