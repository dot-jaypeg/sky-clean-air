What air duct cleaning actually does

Your HVAC system moves a lot of air. Over time, that airflow also moves particles that can settle inside returns, supply runs, and around vent openings. Air Duct Cleaning targets the areas where that material collects, especially where there are bends, transitions, and long runs.

Here is what people in Rancho Peñasquitos often notice after a proper cleaning.

  • Reduced musty or been closed up smells, especially after trips
  • A home that is easier to keep tidy between normal cleanings

No magic tricks. Just removing the gunk that does not belong in the air pathway.

Return vents and supply vents

This is an easy one to mix up.

Supply vents blow conditioned air into rooms. Return vents pull air back to the HVAC system.

In many Rancho Peñasquitos homes, the return is where a lot of visible lint and hair gathers first. If you have a large central return in a hallway, it can be a magnet for everything floating in the home.

A quick tip. If you are dusting a lot, check the return grille. If it looks fuzzy or gray, that is often a clue the system is pulling more debris than your filter can catch at the grille opening.
