How to know if your ducts need cleaning

If your HVAC works, it is easy to ignore the ductwork. It is like the plumbing behind the wall. You only think about it when something feels off. Signs that often lead East Village residents and business owners to schedule service include

  • You moved into a place and do not know the system history

If you have ever held your hand up and said this one blows that one pulls, you already understand the basics.
