How often should ducts be cleaned

There is not a single schedule that fits everyone. It depends on what gets into the system and how quickly it builds up.

You may consider cleaning more often if

  • You have multiple shedding pets
  • Anyone in the home is sensitive to dust
  • You recently completed renovations
  • You run the HVAC most days of the year
  • The home sat vacant for a long period

You may consider less frequent cleaning if

  • Filters are changed on a steady routine
  • The home has minimal carpet and fewer dust sources
  • Windows stay closed most of the time
  • There are no signs of dust puffs or musty startup odors

If you are unsure, a quick inspection and a look at the return side can tell a lot.
