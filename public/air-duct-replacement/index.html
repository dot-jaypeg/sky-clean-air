Do you really need new air ducts

Not every duct needs to be replaced. Sometimes a repair or reseal makes sense. That said, if ducts are old, damaged, undersized, or laid out poorly, replacement can pay you back in comfort and energy savings. We can help you decide during a free on-site visit and a simple HVAC Inspection or HVAC Diagnostics.

Common signs your ducts are failing

  • Hot and cold spots across rooms or floors
  • Weak airflow from some vents while others blast
  • High energy bills even after tune-ups
  • Ducts that look crushed, kinked, or chewed
  • Mysterious dust streaks around vents and registers
  • Musty or attic-like smells when the system starts
  • Rattling, whistling, or popping noises from the ductwork
  • Return grilles that pull in lint quickly, even with fresh filters
  • Visible gaps at joints or tape peeling off
  • Condensation on ducts or water stains in the attic

If you are checking more than a couple of boxes, Air Duct Replacement may be smarter than patchwork fixes.

Why ducts wear out in San Diego homes

  • Salt in the coastal air can corrode metal parts over years
  • Inland attics get hot in summer, which breaks down old tapes and insulation
  • Santa Ana winds push fine dust into tiny gaps and seams
  • Wildlife in attics and crawlspaces can crush or shred flexible duct
  • Remodels often add rooms without rethinking the duct layout
  • Older ducts may be the wrong size for your current furnace or AC
  • Settling homes create kinks or sags that never get corrected

Our Air Duct Replacement process

Step 1 Home assessment and airflow testing

  • We look at your existing ducts, registers, and returns
  • We check the air handler and measure airflow at a few vents
  • We pinpoint leaks, crushed sections, and poor connections
  • We note attic or crawlspace challenges and insulation needs
  • We talk about the rooms that give you the most trouble

Photos and simple test results make it clear what is going on. We will ask things like, Do you want more airflow in the primary bedroom, or should we pull some back from a guest room

Step 2 Smart duct layout and sizing

Good airflow starts with the right layout and duct sizes. We plan efficient runs with smooth bends and proper trunk lines. We size supply branches so each room gets the air it needs. We also make sure return air paths are strong enough so the system does not struggle. If your home needs an extra return grille, we include that in the plan.

Step 3 Material selection

Every home is different. We match materials to your space, budget, and HVAC equipment. That might mean insulated flexible duct for branches, lined sheet metal for trunks, or a mix. We choose insulation thickness that makes sense for your attic or crawlspace temperatures. If you are improving the attic we can also discuss Attic Insulation Replacement.

Step 4 Professional installation and sealing

  • Old ductwork is removed and disposed of neatly
  • New ducts are routed with gentle bends and solid supports
  • Joints are sealed with mastic and high-grade tape, not the cheap stuff from the junk drawer
  • Penetrations are sealed to keep critters and dust out
  • Ducts are insulated to the correct R-value for our region
  • Supply registers and return grilles are refitted and squared up

Ask about Duct Sealing and Attic Air Sealing to finish the job right.

Step 5 Balancing testing and cleanup

We test airflow at key vents, balance it with dampers and register adjustments, and make sure your thermostat settings match your goals. We leave the work area clean and walk you through what we did so you know exactly what changed and why. When needed we perform detailed Airflow Balancing.

Duct materials we install

The right duct type depends on your home layout, your HVAC size, and your goals.

Flexible duct

  • Good for branch runs and tight spaces
  • Faster to install which helps with budget
  • Must be supported correctly to avoid sags
  • Smooth inner liner helps reduce friction
  • Works well in most San Diego attics when installed carefully

Sheet metal ducts

  • Strong and durable for main trunk lines
  • Resistant to critter damage and rough handling
  • Good choice for long straight runs
  • Needs insulation wrap in attics or crawlspaces
  • Great for homes that need maximum airflow or higher static pressure

Duct board plenums and trunks

  • Rigid fiberglass board with built-in insulation
  • Can be shaped to fit tight mechanical rooms
  • Quiet operation thanks to internal lining
  • Often used for plenums and short trunk sections

We often combine materials to get the best performance for your home. For instance, a metal trunk with flexible branches gives you strong airflow and easier routing to each room.

Aftercare and maintenance tips

Air Duct Maintenance helps protect your investment. A few simple habits keep it that way.

  • Change filters on schedule. Most homes do well at 1 to 3 months for standard filters, or as directed for thicker media filters
  • Keep vents and returns open. Close too many and airflow drops
  • Do not stack storage on attic ducts. Even a few boxes can crush flexible duct
  • Check for new dust streaks. If you see any around vents after months of clean operation, call us to take a look
  • Schedule a quick check every couple of years. We can perform a Ductwork Inspection and make small tweaks if needed

Have pets or recent remodeling? Dust loads can spike. Upgrading to a better return grille and filter can help catch more particles without choking airflow.

A closer look at sealing and insulation

Sealing and insulation are the unsung heroes of a good duct system. They stop two big sources of waste in San Diego homes.

  • Leakage at joints and seams. We use mastic, a thick sealant that stays flexible, to close gaps where ducts connect. It performs well in hot attics and over time
  • Heat gain and loss through duct walls. We choose insulation thickness based on your attic or crawlspace temperatures. Proper insulation cuts losses during long cooling cycles in summer and early heating cycles on chilly mornings

We also seal the gaps where ducts meet the ceiling or wall grills. That blocks dusty attic air from being pulled in around the edges. You get cleaner vents and a nicer finish. Learn more about professional Duct Sealing.

Balancing airflow for each room

Once new ducts are in place, we adjust dampers and register positions to deliver steady, even airflow. Small changes here make a big difference in comfort. We listen to your feedback room by room and fine tune the system. Have a home office that gets afternoon sun from the west side? We can lean a bit more air that way during work hours and scale back at night. Detailed Airflow Balancing is available when needed.

What about duct cleaning versus replacement

We offer duct cleaning, and it can help if your ducts are in good shape. If ducts are leaking, undersized, or falling apart, cleaning will not fix airflow or energy waste. Replacement is the right fix in those cases. Not sure which bucket your home falls into? We will show you clear photos and explain your choices.

Do I need to be home during the air duct replacement?

Will replacing air ducts help with allergies or asthma?
