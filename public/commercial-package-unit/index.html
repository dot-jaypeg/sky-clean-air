What is a Commercial Package Unit and why it fits San Diego properties

A commercial package unit combines all the heating, cooling, and ventilation parts in one cabinet. Most units sit on the roof. That frees up space indoors and keeps noise out of tenant areas. Learn more about typical rooftop units. In San Diego, this setup is popular for

  • Strip malls and quick service restaurants
  • Medical offices and professional suites
  • Warehouses and light industrial spaces
  • Schools, gyms, and community centers
  • Downtown mixed use buildings with tight footprints

San Diego’s climate stays mild for much of the year, but buildings still need reliable cooling during warm spells and solid ventilation all year. Many sites near the ocean see early coil corrosion. Inland zones see more dust. A smart package unit choice and steady care help avoid breakdowns and high bills. Consider regular commercial HVAC maintenance to stay ahead.

Our process for installs and change outs

  • Site visit and load review We check roof access, curb size, electrical service, gas lines if present, and duct connections. We look at your space use, hours, people count, and any internal heat loads from cooking or equipment. This prevents sizing mistakes that cause hot and cold spots.
  • Options and clear pricing You receive options with efficiency ratings, tonnage, heating type, and control choices. We explain what each choice means for energy use, comfort, and code compliance with California Title 24.
  • Permits and scheduling We pull the required permit with the City of San Diego or your local jurisdiction, coordinate any inspections, and set a crane date if needed. Downtown lifts may need traffic control. We handle it.
  • Removal and curb work We remove the old unit, adapt the curb if the footprint differs, and seal roof penetrations to keep water out. We protect landscaping, vehicles, and pedestrians during the lift.
  • Connections and start up Our team connects power, gas or heat pump lines, controls, and fresh air ducts. We test airflow, heat, cooling, and economizer operation. You receive a start up report and photos.
  • Walkthrough and training We show your building or facilities staff how to use the thermostat or BAS interface and how to replace filters on schedule.

Replacement is a good time to add energy saving features like demand control ventilation, smart controls, and higher efficiency motors. Ask us what fits your building.

Choosing the right Commercial Package Unit for your property

A good unit matches the space, usage, and budget. Are you weighing a simple replacement or a more efficient upgrade. We can help you pick what fits. We can begin with a thorough commercial HVAC inspection.

Factors we review with you

  • Capacity and airflow Tonnage that matches your load, with enough static pressure for your duct system.
  • Heating type Gas heat, electric heat, or heat pump based on utility rates, roof gas access, and comfort goals.
  • Efficiency ratings EER and IEER matter in San Diego because much of the year is part load. Better IEER often lowers real bills.
  • Ventilation requirements Meet fresh air rules while keeping control over humidity and energy. That means a working economizer and sensors that make sense for your zone.
  • Controls From simple programmable stats to BACnet connections. We set up schedules, lockouts, and alarms you can actually use. See our commercial thermostat installation and commercial thermostat programming.
  • Noise and neighbors Rooftop units near residential areas need quiet operation. Fan choices and vibration pads help.
  • Footprint and curb Replacements may need a curb adapter. We check roof structure and drainage to avoid leaks.
  • Power and gas Verify voltage, phase, and gas piping before replacement day to avoid delays.
  • Location Buildings near the coast may need corrosion resistant parts. Inland sites may benefit from extra filtration and easy access for filter changes during dust season.

Indoor air quality and ventilation that matches San Diego days

Your packaged unit does more than heat and cool. It also brings in outside air. On some days, that air needs extra care.

  • Wildfire smoke days We can place a higher MERV filter strategy for those periods and adjust economizers to limit smoke entry.
  • Coastal moisture Early morning fog can load coils with moisture. Clean coils shed water better, which helps airflow and keeps microbial growth down.
  • Pollen and dust season Filter upgrades and regular changes keep occupants more comfortable and protect your coils.
  • Ventilation balance Too little fresh air leads to stuffy rooms. Too much raises bills. We test and set the right minimum and verify operation.

As an air duct cleaning service provider, Sky Clean Air also inspects and cleans connected ductwork. Clean ducts lower static pressure and can boost delivered airflow. Energy, efficiency, and controls that make sense

Not sure if you should switch to a heat pump or stay with gas heat. Wondering if a smart thermostat will really help. We match choices to real usage. Ask about commercial HVAC zoning setup and commercial HVAC control setup to improve comfort and savings.

  • Heat type Gas heat is common and can be cost effective where gas rates are stable. Heat pumps can help where cooling runs most of the year and the building has good duct sealing. Dual fuel can provide flexibility.
  • Controls Smart thermostats and BAS tie ins allow scheduling, remote view, and alarms. We set moderate temperature ranges that reduce energy use and limit tenant tug of war.
  • Economizers On cool dry days in San Diego, the economizer can cool your building with outside air. When smoke or high humidity hits, we limit outside air to protect comfort and indoor air quality.
  • Filtration We select filters that meet indoor air needs without choking the system. MERV 8 to 13 covers many sites. We balance clean air with airflow and energy use.
  • VFDs and ECMs Variable speed control can reduce fan energy use during part load. We confirm duct static and motor compatibility before we recommend a change.

Want a simple energy checkup with your next maintenance visit. Ask us to include it. You will get practical steps you can act on right away.
