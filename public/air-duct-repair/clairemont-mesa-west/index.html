What types of air duct repairs do you handle

Most duct repairs fall into a few categories. The right fix depends on duct type and access.

Sealing leaks at joints and connections

We often find leaks at

  • Plenum connections near the air handler
  • Branch takeoffs
  • Register boots at the ceiling or wall
  • Return transitions

Reconnecting separated duct runs

A duct that slipped off a collar can dump conditioned air into an attic. Reconnecting and properly fastening it is usually the first step, then sealing and supporting it.

Repairing torn or crushed flex duct

Flex duct can tear or pinch. We repair or replace the damaged section when a patch is not the right fit for the situation. If replacement becomes the better path, we can discuss Air Duct Replacement in Clairemont Mesa West, CA based on what you have in place.

Supporting sagging runs

Sag reduces airflow. We add proper support so air can move without fighting gravity.

Fixing return air issues

Return side leaks can pull in dusty attic air or garage odors. This is a common reason people call after noticing a smell that shows up only when the system runs.

Are duct leaks really that big of a deal

Sometimes yes, sometimes it is just annoying. The impact depends on where the leak is and how large it is.

  • A supply leak in the attic can mean less air reaches the rooms
  • A return leak near a garage can mean odors come along for the ride
  • A leak near insulation can add fine particles to the airstream

Here is the simple way to think about it. Your HVAC system is trying to move a set amount of air. Leaks change the path. When the path changes, comfort changes.
