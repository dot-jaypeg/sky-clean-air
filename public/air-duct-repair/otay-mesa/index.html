Duct repair vs duct cleaning

They work together, but they are not the same job. Duct cleaning focuses on removing dust and debris from inside the duct system where accessible. Duct repair focuses on fixing the physical ductwork so it stays connected, sealed, and able to deliver air where it should. If ductwork is leaking, cleaning alone may not address the root issue. Leaks can pull dusty air from attics, crawlspaces, or ceiling voids, and that dust can return after cleaning. Repair is about stopping that unwanted pathway. For ongoing upkeep after repairs, some properties also benefit from Air Duct Maintenance in Otay Mesa, CA.

Do you repair flexible duct, sheet metal duct, or both

Sky Clean Air works with common duct types found in Otay Mesa.

Flexible duct

Flex duct is common in attics. It is lightweight and easy to route, but it can be damaged by kinks, sagging, torn outer jackets, and crushed sections.

  • Replacing a torn or crushed section

Sheet metal duct

  • Sealing joints and seams

  • Improving fit at transitions where leaks tend to happen

What does duct sealing involve and where do leaks usually occur

Duct leaks often show up at joints, transitions, and connections. Not the straight runs. The boring parts are usually fine. The busy junctions are where air escapes.

  • Plenum connections near the air handler
  • Takeoffs where branch lines connect
  • Register boots where ducts meet the room vent
  • Return boxes and return plenums
  • Old tape or failing sealant on joints

Duct sealing is the process of closing those gaps using materials meant for HVAC duct connections. The goal is to keep conditioned air in the ducts until it reaches the rooms, and to keep unconditioned air from being pulled into the system through returns.
