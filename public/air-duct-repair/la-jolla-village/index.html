How we find the problem when ducts are hidden

We start with what we can observe and verify. Airflow symptoms often point to a specific branch run. We also look for physical clues like disconnected collars, crushed flex duct, and gaps around boots.

Clues that help pinpoint the issue

  • A vent that is loud and weak at the same time can hint at a restriction upstream
  • A room that never gets cool can point to a disconnected run in the attic above it
  • Dust streaks around a ceiling register can suggest leakage at the boot

Sometimes the fix is simple. We have seen a duct run supported by a single strap that finally gave up. The duct sagged into a U shape and airflow dropped. A little support work can help.

Are noisy ducts a repair issue

Often, yes. Noise is usually a symptom.

Duct related noises can come from

  • High velocity air through a partially crushed section

If you are hearing a whistle near a vent, it is usually air squeezing through a gap or restriction.
