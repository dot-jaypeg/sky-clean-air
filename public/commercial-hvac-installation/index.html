What Counts as Commercial HVAC Installation

Commercial HVAC Installation covers more than placing a box on a roof. It includes the equipment, the ductwork and airflow paths, the refrigerant lines, the electrical work, the controls, and the testing at the end.

Here are the systems we install and integrate

  • Packaged rooftop units for retail, offices, and mixed use buildings
  • Split systems and heat pumps for flexible zoning and energy savings
  • VRF and ductless mini splits for tenant build outs and remodels
  • Make up air units for kitchens and high exhaust areas
  • Economizers and demand controlled ventilation for fresh air and savings
  • Exhaust fans and dedicated outdoor air systems for code compliance
  • Server room cooling for tech and production spaces with dedicated AC
  • Smart thermostats and light building automation

Every building has different needs. A Pacific Beach salon may need strong ventilation and quiet indoor units. A Kearny Mesa warehouse may need large air volumes, open duct runs, and sturdy rooftop equipment. We match the plan to the use of the space and the local climate zone.

Systems We Install and Why They Work

Packaged Rooftop Units

The workhorse for many retail and office properties in San Diego. Packaged units sit on the roof and include heating, cooling, and supply fans in a single cabinet.

Good fits

  • Retail centers from Chula Vista to Mira Mesa
  • One or two story office buildings
  • Medical waiting rooms and clinics
  • Schools and churches

Benefits

  • Easy service access and minimal indoor footprint
  • Improved airflow with new duct transitions
  • Better energy ratings than older gear
  • Economizer options that pull in cool coastal air on mild days

Heat Pump Systems

Heat pumps move heat instead of burning gas. In San Diego’s mild winters, this is a strong way to lower energy use and emissions.

Good fits

  • Professional offices in La Jolla and UTC
  • Multi tenant spaces that need zone control
  • Buildings with electrification goals

Benefits

  • Heating and cooling from one system
  • High efficiency in our climate
  • Quiet indoor operation

VRF and Ductless Mini Splits

These systems move refrigerant to different zones, letting you heat or cool rooms independently.

Good fits

  • Renovations where ductwork is tight
  • Older buildings in Hillcrest or North Park with space limits
  • Server rooms, conference rooms, and private offices

Benefits

  • Strong zoning
  • Quiet and compact
  • Good control for spaces with varied use

Make Up Air and Exhaust

Kitchens, salons, factories, and gyms need fresh air coming in to match air going out.

We install

  • Make up air units with gas heat or electric heat
  • Grease rated exhaust for commercial kitchens
  • High efficiency fans for salons and fitness spaces
  • Roof curbs, hoods, and curb adaptors for clean retrofits

Economizers and Demand Controlled Ventilation

San Diego’s climate allows fresh air cooling for many days a year. Economizers use cooler outside air to reduce compressor run time. Demand controlled ventilation uses sensors to add fresh air when people fill the room, then scale back when the room empties.

Benefits

  • Lower utility bills
  • Better indoor air quality
  • Meets code for ventilation while saving energy

Server Room Cooling

Sensitive equipment needs stable temperatures all day and night.

Options

  • Dedicated split systems with redundancy
  • Ductless units for small data closets
  • Monitoring tied to alerts

We set up simple dashboards so the right person hears about a problem before it becomes a crisis with system integration.

Our Start to Finish Process

1) Site Walk and Load Assessment

We visit your site, review your current equipment and ductwork, measure key spaces, and talk through problems people notice. Hot conference rooms, noisy dining areas, cold spots under vents, air that smells stale, or humidity problems. We also check roof access, electrical service, gas lines if used, and any structural needs for new curbs or stands.

We size equipment with a right fit approach. Bigger is not always better. Oversized gear short cycles, costs more to run, and wears out sooner. Undersized gear never catches up and makes people miserable. We target the sweet spot.

2) Proposal With Options

You get options that fit your goals. Want the lowest first cost that still meets code? Want the most efficient setup to cut monthly bills and support electrification goals? We explain the differences in clear terms, not a pile of acronyms. You choose the path that fits your budget and timeline.

Each proposal includes

  • Equipment models and capacities
  • Efficiency ratings and expected savings range
  • Scope for ductwork, controls, electrical, and any roof or curb work
  • Schedule and crew size
  • Permit steps and inspection plan
  • Flat pricing with any crane or after hours costs listed

3) Permits and Plan Check

We handle permits with the City of San Diego or other jurisdictions in the county. We prepare drawings as needed, submit paperwork, and track status. Our office keeps you posted so you are never guessing.

4) Scheduling and Prep

We set dates that work for your operation. Many clients pick night or weekend work. We protect floors and furniture, isolate work areas, and set up safe access. If a crane lift is needed, we handle traffic control and coordinate with property managers or neighbors.

5) Installation

  • Remove and recycle old units
  • Set new curbs or reuse with adaptors if they fit the plan
  • Lift new equipment into place with a crane and licensed operators
  • Run new duct transitions and seal joints to stop leaks
  • Install line sets, drains, and electrical connections
  • Set up thermostats or controls
  • Start up the system and tune airflow

6) Testing, Balancing, and Training

We verify that airflow matches the plan, set outside air for code, test safety devices, and dial in control settings. We show your team how to set schedules, change filters, and read alerts. We leave a simple quick start guide with contacts and maintenance tips.

7) Follow Up and Maintenance

New systems run best with routine care. We offer service plans that fit your site. Filters, belts, coil cleaning, and seasonal tune ups go a long way. Call 619-304-8822 to set a plan that fits your hours and staff access.

Energy Efficiency That Cuts Costs

Energy use hurts the bottom line. We focus on equipment and setups that keep people comfortable while using less power and gas.

Energy saving features we recommend

  • High IEER rooftop units that sip energy
  • Heat pumps with inverter compressors
  • Variable frequency drives on fans and pumps
  • Economizers and demand controlled ventilation
  • ECM fan motors and smart scheduling
  • Better duct sealing and insulation
  • Smart thermostat programming with lockouts and alerts

San Diego’s mild days and cool nights make outside air cooling a strong tool. On coastal properties, an economizer can carry much of the load during spring and fall. East County still benefits on many evenings.

Ask us about utility rebates from SDG&E and city programs that may offset part of your upgrade. We keep paperwork simple and timelines clear.

Local Codes and Compliance

Commercial HVAC Installation in San Diego must meet California Title 24 energy rules and local code. We set equipment that fits these rules without making life hard for your staff. That includes

  • Proper ventilation rates for occupancy and space type
  • Economizers where required
  • Demand control ventilation where required
  • Approved controls with setback and scheduling
  • Smoke detector placement in duct systems where required
  • Gas piping and venting rules where gas heat is used
  • Electrical disconnects and working clearances

We coordinate with inspectors, meet them on site, and resolve any notes quickly.

Air Quality and Ductwork Matter

Comfort is not just temperature. Air quality shapes how people feel in your space. Because our company started with air duct cleaning service, we care about clean ducts and clean installs.

We look at

  • Filter type and size including higher MERV options where suitable
  • Fresh air intake placement to avoid pulling in grease, smoke, or vehicle fumes
  • Duct sealing to stop dust and loss
  • Return air sizing so systems can breathe
  • Exhaust paths in kitchens, salons, locker rooms, and labs

Does your office smell like last night’s gyro service next door? Fresh air and proper exhaust can fix that.

Signs You May Need Replacement Instead of Repair

If you are unsure whether to repair or replace, look for these signs

  • System is 12 to 20 years old
  • Rising energy bills with no change in use
  • R 22 refrigerant still in use
  • Frequent breakdowns or hard to find parts
  • Uneven temperatures or poor airflow
  • Loud operation or short cycling

We will run the numbers and give you a fair look at repair vs. replacement. No pressure. If a repair makes sense, we will do it. If a new system saves money over time, we will show you how and why with clear replacement options.
