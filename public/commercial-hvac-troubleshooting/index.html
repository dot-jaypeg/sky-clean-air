What Problems Do We Solve

Rooftop Packaged Units

Common issues we fix

  • Short cycling that trips components or knocks the system offline
  • Warm air from bad compressors low refrigerant or mixed air problems
  • Ice on coils from low airflow or metering faults
  • Loud belts seized bearings or motor failures
  • Dead economizers or stuck outdoor air dampers
  • Corroded condenser coils from sea air and gull roosts

How we solve them

  • Check static pressure belt tension and filter condition with follow up airflow balancing if needed
  • Test contactors capacitors and motors under load
  • Measure refrigerant superheat and subcool to confirm charge
  • Inspect and free economizer linkages and recalibrate sensors with guidance from economizer fundamentals
  • Clean coils and apply protective coatings when needed with scheduled commercial AC cleaning
  • Seal cabinet gaps that pull in rain and salt spray

Have units on a crowded roof in East Village with tight clearances We handle safe access and coordination with building security.

Split Systems And Heat Pumps

Common issues

  • Outdoor fan runs but no cooling inside
  • Heat pump stuck in heat or cool
  • Liquid line restrictions that cause noisy operation
  • Poor airflow from blocked returns or collapsed flex runs

Solutions we provide

  • Verify reversing valve operation and check control boards
  • Inspect line set insulation and look for rub out leaks
  • Measure temperature split and balance zones
  • Clean indoor coils and confirm drain pan slope and trap depth

Chillers And Cooling Towers

Common issues

  • High condenser water temperature from scaled towers
  • Low delta T that kills efficiency and raises energy use
  • Sensor drift that causes unstable staging
  • Pump cavitation or air in the loop that leads to noise and poor flow

Our approach

  • Trend logs and confirm sensor accuracy
  • Inspect strainers check valves and actuators
  • Test glycol mix where needed confirm fill and purge air
  • Review tower fan and fill condition treat scale and biofilm
  • Coordinate with your building engineer to plan stable setpoints

Downtown San Diego sites near the bay need extra attention on tower water quality because of sea air and gull debris. We address that without drama.

Boilers And Hydronic Loops

Issues we see

  • Short cycling from bad control settings or faulty sensors
  • Air bound loops that starve coils
  • Sticking isolation valves or three way valves that bypass coils
  • Soot buildup that drops combustion efficiency

Fixes

  • Balance flows purge air and set correct delta T
  • Calibrate supply and return sensors
  • Service burners and clean heat exchangers
  • Repair or replace aged actuators and sensors

VAV Boxes And Dampers

A Closer Look At Causes And Fixes

Refrigerant Issues

Low charge is often not the starting problem. The real cause could be a slow leak at a flare a rub out or a failing service valve. We use electronic leak detectors and dye only when needed. Once fixed and recharged to proper subcool and superheat performance stabilizes.

Airflow Limits

Filters clog coils load up and duct systems leak. High external static pressure burns energy and shortens equipment life. We measure it adjust fan speed and fix the restrictions. This single step often brings a unit back to spec. Pair this with duct sealing and balancing for lasting results.

Controls Conflicts

Two thermostats fight for the same zone. Minimum outside air set too high. Schedules overlap across tenants. We clean up the logic set realistic setpoints and sort priorities. Comfort improves and equipment runs fewer hours.

Drainage And Humidity

San Diego is usually dry but we still get coil sweat and clogged drains in the marine layer or during June Gloom. We clear traps flush lines and set proper trap heights. No more pan overflows.

Power Quality

Loose neutrals phase imbalance and weak capacitors hit compressors hard. We test incoming power tighten lugs and replace weak parts before they take a motor out. That saves you from big bills later.

Real San Diego Scenarios

  • A rooftop unit in Pacific Beach keeps failing on hot afternoons. The cause is a corroded condenser coil facing the ocean and a stuck economizer damper. We clean and coat the coil free the damper recalibrate sensors and the unit runs steady
