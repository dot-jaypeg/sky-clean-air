Problems We Fix Every Week

Have you noticed any of these issues?

  • The indoor unit runs but the room never reaches the set temperature
  • Power light blinks or you see an error code on the display

Installation Done Right The First Time

A mini split is only as good as the plan and the install. Proper sizing, line routing, and unit placement make a big difference in comfort and efficiency. Here is how we handle it with expert Mini-Split Installation.

  • Home assessment and sizing. We look at room sizes, insulation, windows, solar gain, and the way you use the space. A room that bakes in the late afternoon needs different capacity than a shaded guest room
  • Placement guidance. We choose indoor head spots that avoid blowing air directly on beds or desks, and make sure filters are easy to reach. Outdoor units need clearance, airflow, and quiet operation that keeps HOAs happy
  • Line set routing. Clean runs with the shortest path we can make, sealed wall sleeves, and neat covers that look good on your exterior
  • Electrical and condensate. Dedicated power, proper surge protection, and secure, sloped drains. Coastal areas benefit from extra care on connections
  • Permits and inspections where required. We handle the paperwork and meet the inspector. You enjoy your new comfort with proper HVAC inspection
  • Final walkthrough. We teach you the remote, show you how to clean filters, and set up app controls if your system supports it

Common Repairs We Handle

Mini splits are reliable. Parts do wear over time. Here are fixes we handle often.

  • Refrigerant leaks at flare fittings, coils, or service valves
  • Sensor failures that confuse temperature readings
  • Capacitors and fan motors that no longer start or run at speed
  • Control board issues after power surges
  • Reversing valve problems that block heating or cooling mode
  • Drain clogs that trip float switches and stop cooling
  • Dirty blower wheels that reduce airflow and make noise

We use proper leak detection methods, including nitrogen pressure testing and electronic sniffers. We evacuate the system with a micron gauge and charge to manufacturer specs. That helps your system run the way it should, without short cycling or freezing. For urgent situations, ask about our Emergency HVAC Services.

Deep Cleaning For Indoor Heads

If your indoor head smells musty, drips water, or blows less air than it used to, it may need a deep clean. Pulling the cover and washing the coil and blower wheel removes buildup that simple filter cleaning cannot catch. That buildup traps moisture and can lead to odors and reduced airflow.

We use cleaning methods that protect electronics, keep water controlled, and leave the head looking and working like it should. Many clients are shocked by the dirt we remove. The room feels fresh again, and the unit runs quieter. See our Ductless System Service for more on deep cleaning and care.

Indoor Air Quality Tips For Homes With Mini Splits

Since mini splits have no long duct runs, filter care matters even more. Here are quick tips.

  • Rinse washable filters every 4 to 6 weeks during heavy use
  • Do not run without a filter, even for a day
  • Keep pets out of the intake while the cover is open
  • Wipe the intake grille and louvers with a damp cloth monthly
  • Keep indoor heads at least a few inches from shelves and curtains

Want help setting a simple care schedule? We can label filters with dates, show you how to remove them, and set reminders during your visit.

Energy Use And Your SDG&E Bill

Mini splits are efficient because they move heat instead of creating it. In cooling season, they remove heat from your room. In heating season, they bring heat in from the outside air. In a mild climate like ours, that saves energy.

More tips to save

  • Use the Dry or Dehumidify mode on humid days near the coast
  • Set reasonable temperatures like 75 to 78 for cooling and 68 to 70 for heating
  • Close blinds on west-facing windows during late afternoon
  • Keep filters and coils clean for steady airflow
  • Use timers or app schedules, especially for guest rooms

There may be utility programs or tax credits available for heat pump systems. Availability changes. Ask our team to review current options for your address.

Quick Troubleshooting You Can Try Before We Arrive

A few simple checks can get your unit back online or help us fix it faster.

  • Make sure the remote is set to Cool or Heat, not Fan or Dry
  • Check the set temperature, sometimes it gets bumped
  • Clean the filter if it looks dusty
  • Turn the power off at the breaker for 60 seconds, then back on
  • Look for a full drain line. If water is dripping, place a container under the line and call us
  • Clear leaves or debris from the outdoor unit and give it space

Mini-Split Service Details For The Curious

Want a bit more detail on how we handle the technical side? Here you go, without the jargon overload.

Sizing and load. We match system capacity to your rooms by considering square footage, insulation levels, window size, and sun exposure. Oversized units short cycle and leave humidity behind. Undersized units run non-stop and still miss the set point. Proper sizing gives smooth, quiet operation

Refrigerant charging. Many manufacturers call for precise charge by weight. After leak repair or component replacement, we evacuate to the right micron level and weigh in the charge to spec. We verify with temperature readings so you get full performance in both cooling and heating

Airflow. Clean blower wheels and coils matter. Even a thin film on a coil can drop efficiency. We measure temperature split across the coil and compare to expected values for your model

Drains. A clogged drain can trigger a float switch and shut the system down. We flush lines, confirm proper slope, and add pan tabs where helpful. No more surprise drips on drywall

Controls. If your system uses a Wi-Fi adapter or third-party thermostat, we set it up, update firmware when needed, and walk you through the app

Vibration and noise. We use proper pads and mounting hardware for outdoor units. Indoor heads are leveled and secured to prevent rattles that keep you up at night

Line set care. We use quality flares, torque to spec, and insulate lines fully. Outdoors, UV-resistant covers protect looks and performance

Mini-Split Service: Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mini-split system?

A mini-split system is a ductless heating and cooling unit used to control the temperature in individual rooms or spaces.

How often should I service my mini-split system?

It's recommended to service your mini-split at least once a year to keep it running efficiently.

What does a mini-split service include?

Service typically includes cleaning, checking the system for any issues, and making sure it operates properly.

Why is regular maintenance important for mini-splits?

Regular maintenance helps ensure your system runs smoothly, improves air quality, and can extend its lifespan.

Can Sky Clean Air service all brands of mini-split systems?

Yes, Sky Clean Air provides service for most major mini-split brands.

How do I know if my mini-split needs servicing?

Common signs include strange noises, weak airflow, unusual odors, or the system not cooling or heating effectively.

How long does a mini-split service visit take?

A routine service visit usually takes about one to two hours.

Is it safe to clean my mini-split system myself?

Basic cleaning like dusting the unit can be done yourself, but professional service is recommended for a thorough job.
