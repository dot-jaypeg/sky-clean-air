Stop Energy Waste Above Your Ceiling

Heating and cooling is one of the largest expenses in a commercial space. Yet in many buildings conditioned air escapes through gaps in the attic or roof cavity while hot or dirty air sneaks in. That hidden exchange drives up bills stresses HVAC equipment and creates hot and cold spots that frustrate staff and customers.

San Diego has mild weather for much of the year but the temperature swings are real. Coastal mornings can be cool and damp. Midday sun warms the roof and attic quickly. Inland areas see bigger swings and Santa Ana events bring dry hot air. If your attic leaks your HVAC system is paying for all of it.

Commercial Attic Air Sealing In San Diego stops those leaks at their source. We locate and seal gaps in the ceiling plane and roof penetrations then verify the results. The work is neat safe and planned around your hours.

Why Sealing Matters More Than You Think

Insulation slows heat flow. Air sealing blocks the movement of air. Both matter and together they do the heavy lifting. Many buildings in San Diego have decent insulation yet still lose energy because air finds a path through

  • Openings around electrical and data runs
  • Gaps where walls meet the ceiling
  • Plumbing and vent penetrations
  • Recessed lights and access panels
  • Shaft openings and chases
  • Cracks near rooftop HVAC curbs
  • Leaky duct boots in the ceiling

If you only add more insulation without sealing you can still have drafts dust and uneven temperatures. Seal first then insulate as needed. That order helps your insulation work the way it should. If you plan to add insulation later consider options like commercial blown in attic insulation or commercial attic insulation replacement after air sealing.

Signs Your Building Needs Air Sealing

You do not need a lab to suspect leakage. Look for these clues

  • Temperature swings from room to room or from one end of a suite to the other
  • Rising energy bills with the same hours and staff count
  • HVAC runs longer than it used to or cycles on and off too often
  • Dust streaks on ceiling tiles or near recessed lights
  • Drafts at the top floor or near stairwells and shafts
  • Odors that move between tenants or from the outdoors
  • Pollen and wildfire smoke making it past the ceiling
  • Condensation on supply vents or stains on the roof deck
  • Pest trails or droppings in the ceiling space
  • Water stains or daylight visible around roof penetrations

If you see a few of these attic leakage is likely part of the problem. Quick question. If your AC shut off right now would the space stay comfortable for a reasonable time or get stuffy fast Short comfort time often points to air leakage.

How Sky Clean Air Seals Commercial Attics

We keep the process clear and predictable. You will always know what we are doing and why.

Step 1 Onsite Assessment

  • Walkthrough with your facility lead to understand concerns hours and access
  • Review of building layout roof type and attic or plenum conditions
  • Visual inspection of common leakage points
  • Discussion of safety needs including ladder access and roof permits if needed

Step 2 Diagnostic Testing

  • Blower door testing to measure air leakage when conditions allow
  • Pressure mapping to see how air moves through the ceiling plane
  • Thermal imaging to spot hot and cold pathways
  • Smoke tracing to confirm hidden gaps

When testing is not feasible during operating hours we plan off hours or use a test zone to gather solid data with minimal disruption. For duct system loss we can pair with commercial duct leakage testing or duct leakage testing where appropriate.

Step 3 Sealing Plan

We create a clear scope with line items photos and pricing. You will see what we will seal the methods we will use and how we will protect your operations. We prioritize high impact areas first to deliver fast gains.

Step 4 Active Sealing

  • Seal penetrations and gaps at the ceiling plane
  • Seal around duct boots recessed fixtures and access panels
  • Cap and seal abandoned openings and chases
  • Seal top plates and wall to ceiling joints
  • Address gaps around rooftop equipment curbs and sleeves
  • Add gaskets to service doors and access points where needed

We set up containment where we open the ceiling use HEPA filtration while we work above occupied areas and keep your floors and surfaces clean. If ducts are part of the issue we can pair with duct sealing for best results.

Step 5 Verification

  • Re test to confirm reductions in leakage
  • Thermal imaging after sealing to verify improved boundaries
  • Photo documentation before and after
  • Review with your team including next steps for insulation or duct upgrades if helpful

Materials We Use

We match materials to the task and fire rating needs of your space

  • Foam sealants for small to medium gaps
  • Fire rated sealants where code calls for it
  • Mastic and mesh for seams and joints
  • Rigid blocking for larger openings
  • Gaskets and weatherstripping at panels and doors
  • Mastic and foil tape rated for HVAC at duct boots and plenums

We do not take shortcuts with low grade tapes or materials that dry out. Seals should last and hold up to San Diego heat.

Why San Diego Buildings Lose So Much Energy Through The Attic

Here are local factors that work against you if the attic is leaky

  • Morning marine layer adds moisture that gets drawn into attic cavities
  • Afternoon sun bakes flat roofs driving heat into unsealed spaces
  • Inland zones like El Cajon and Poway see wider temperature swings
  • Santa Ana winds push dry hot air and dust through gaps
  • Many older retail and office buildings were built with little focus on air sealing
  • HVAC units on roofs create many penetrations for wiring condensate lines and refrigerant lines
  • Drop ceilings can hide open shafts and chases that act like chimneys

If your building lies near a busy road like the 805 163 8 or 5 traffic emissions can ride air leaks into your space. Air sealing blocks those pathways. For airflow balance across zones consider airflow balancing after sealing.

What is commercial attic air sealing?

Air duct cleaning is the process of removing dust and debris from the ductwork in your home's heating and cooling system.

Why is attic air sealing important for my business?

Cleaning your air ducts can help improve indoor air quality and keep your HVAC system running efficiently.

How do I know if my commercial building needs attic air sealing?

Most experts recommend cleaning air ducts every 3 to 5 years, or sooner if there are signs of buildup or mold.

What areas in the attic are usually sealed?

Common signs include visible dust, mold, musty odors, or increased allergy symptoms.

How long does attic air sealing take in a commercial setting?

Most appointments take a few hours, depending on the size of your home and the level of buildup.

Will attic air sealing interrupt my business operations?

It can help reduce the amount of dust circulating in your home, leading to a cleaner environment.

Can attic air sealing help with indoor air quality?

Removing dust, pollen, and other irritants from ducts may help reduce allergy symptoms for some people.

Do I need to prepare my building before the service?

Yes, when performed by professionals like Sky Clean Air, the process is safe for your home.

How often should attic air sealing be checked or maintained?

Make sure areas around vents and registers are accessible, and inform the technicians of any special concerns.
