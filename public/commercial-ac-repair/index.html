Signs Your Commercial AC Needs Repair

Not sure if you are dealing with a small hiccup or a bigger problem Watch for these signs.

  • Uneven temperatures across rooms or floors
  • Warm air blowing from supply vents
  • Short cycling or constant cycling that never reaches setpoint
  • Rising energy bills without a change in usage
  • Odd sounds from rooftop units or air handlers
  • Puddles near indoor units or roof drains
  • Musty odors coming from vents
  • Thermostat errors or control alarms
  • Ice on coils or refrigerant lines
  • Poor airflow that makes some areas stuffy

How long has the issue been going on Small issues can turn into bigger repairs if ignored. A quick visit now can save time and money later.

Commercial AC Problems We Fix Every Day

Commercial systems have lots of parts working together. We troubleshoot each part methodically to find the root cause and get you back to cool.

Rooftop Package Units

Rooftop units are common across San Diego in retail centers gyms and office buildings. Common repairs include:

  • Blower motor failures that cause weak airflow or no airflow
  • Burned contactors and bad capacitors that prevent compressors from starting
  • Dirty condenser coils that cause high head pressure and poor cooling
  • Refrigerant leaks that lower capacity
  • Faulty economizers that get stuck and pull in hot air
  • Clogged condensate drains that cause leaks or float switch lockout
  • Belt wear and pulley misalignment that cause squealing and poor airflow

Coastal zones like Pacific Beach and Point Loma face extra wear from salt air. That means faster corrosion on coils and cabinets. Routine coil cleaning and fast sealing of small leaks can extend life.

Split Systems Heat Pumps and Mini Splits

Offices medical suites and server rooms often use split systems or heat pumps. We handle:

  • Outdoor fan motor and control board failures
  • Low refrigerant charge from small leaks
  • Indoor blower issues and dirty evaporator coils
  • Heat pump reversing valve problems that lock the system in one mode
  • Condensate pump failures in interior suites

Mini splits are popular in small storefronts and server closets. Filter cleaning and proper drain routing make a big difference in performance. Learn more about commercial mini split systems.

Air Handlers VAV Boxes and Duct Issues

Comfort depends on airflow. If your VAV boxes stick or your dampers fail rooms go hot or cold no matter what the thermostat says. We repair:

  • Stuck VAV actuators and control signals
  • Leaky or collapsed ducts that starve supply to key areas
  • Return air problems that pull dust from ceiling cavities
  • Fire damper resets after alarms
  • Old flex runs that restrict airflow

Because Sky Clean Air also cleans and seals ductwork we can fix the mechanical problem and clean the airway so the solution lasts. Consider commercial air duct cleaning and duct balancing when airflow is the bottleneck.

Thermostats Controls and Scheduling

A great repair will not help if your controls are off. We work with:

  • Smart thermostats and commercial controllers
  • Faulty sensors and miscalibrated stats
  • Scheduling that fits your business hours
  • Lockouts and alarms that stop cooling overnight

Want your lobby cool by 8 a.m. without running all night We can set staged starts and smarter schedules that match your peak times. Ask about commercial thermostat programming and HVAC control setup.

Refrigerant Leaks and Coil Issues

Low refrigerant charge hurts capacity and can damage compressors. We:

  • Pressure test to confirm a leak
  • Use electronic detection to locate the source
  • Repair tubing and coil leaks where practical
  • Recharge to the correct level and verify superheat or subcool
  • Clean coils to improve heat transfer

If the coil is too far gone we will price repair versus replacement so you can choose what fits your budget and timeline. Diagnostic precision matters. Explore our commercial HVAC diagnostics.

Condensate Drain Problems

San Diego humidity is usually mild but drain clogs still happen from dust algae and construction debris. We clear drains treat pans and route lines to prevent ceiling leaks.

Electrical Failures

From tripped breakers to fried contactors electrical issues are common:

  • Loose lugs and overheated wires
  • Shorted capacitors
  • Failing relays and time delays
  • Worn disconnects on rooftops

We check connections balance loads and replace damaged parts.

Energy Savings and Comfort Tips for San Diego Businesses

Small changes add up. Here are simple steps that help your system and your bottom line.

  • Set your thermostat a bit higher in summer. Even one to two degrees can cut energy use
  • Use scheduled start times so the space is comfortable by opening not hours earlier
  • Keep filters clean. A clogged filter makes your system work harder
  • Shade rooftop units where possible without blocking airflow. Less sun on the cabinet reduces heat load
  • Keep supply and return vents clear of boxes furniture and artwork
  • Add door closers or air curtains in retail spaces with frequent door swings
  • Consider demand controlled ventilation in spaces with fluctuating occupancy
  • Ask about duct sealing if your system never seems to catch up
