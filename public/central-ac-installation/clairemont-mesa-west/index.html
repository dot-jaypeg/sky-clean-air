  • Your comfort priorities such as bedrooms cooler at night and living areas comfortable for gatherings

Indoor air quality and central AC

Central AC mainly cools. Indoor air quality depends on filtration, duct condition, humidity control, and how your home exchanges air. Central AC can support cleaner air when the filter setup fits correctly and is changed on schedule, ductwork is sealed and not pulling dusty attic air, the indoor coil stays clean and drains properly, and airflow is set correctly so filtration happens.

Sky Clean Air often sees the same pattern. A new AC goes in, but the ducts are still dirty or leaky. That can lead to dust settling faster and uneven cooling. Cleaning and sealing decisions are separate from installing the AC, but they are connected to how your home feels.
