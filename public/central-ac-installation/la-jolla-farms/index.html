How do you size a central AC system for my home

Sizing is where a lot of comfort issues begin or end. Too small can struggle on warmer stretches. Too large can cool fast but leave humidity and airflow problems, along with more frequent cycling. When Sky Clean Air helps with central AC installation in La Jolla Farms, we look at factors that actually affect the load

  • Home square footage and ceiling height
  • Window size, window type, and sun exposure
  • Insulation condition in attic and walls
  • Duct layout and return air capacity
  • Number of stories and how heat rises
  • Existing equipment performance history
  • Any hot spots you already know by name

A simple example. Two homes can be the same size, but the one with large west facing glass and a warm upstairs hallway may need different airflow planning than the one shaded by mature landscaping.

Do I need ductwork for central AC

Central AC typically uses ductwork to distribute cooled air. If your home already has forced air heating, you likely have ducts in place. If your ducts are undersized, leaky, or poorly routed, the AC may not feel right even if the equipment is fine. In those cases, homeowners sometimes pair installation planning with Air Duct Replacement in La Jolla Farms, CA or Air Duct Installation in La Jolla Farms, CA.

We commonly see these duct situations around La Jolla Farms

  • Older duct runs with loose connections in attic spaces
  • Returns that are too small for modern airflow needs
  • Flexible duct that is kinked or compressed
  • Supply vents that never really throw air into the room

If you have ever said that vent has never done anything, it is worth addressing during installation rather than hoping the new AC fixes it.

What if I am replacing an old AC system

Replacement is common. Sometimes the old system still runs but it is noisy, inconsistent, or requires frequent attention. Sometimes it is simply at the end of its useful run. If you are deciding between repairing and replacing, it can help to look at AC Repair and AC Replacement as starting points.

Replacement projects often include

  • Checking refrigerant line condition and routing

If the existing setup has creative wiring or a drain line that was installed like a roller coaster, we will point it out and talk through a cleaner path.

Can central AC help with uneven temperatures

It can, but it depends on why temperatures are uneven. La Jolla Farms homes often have large open areas downstairs and more compartmentalized rooms upstairs. Warm air rises, and upstairs hallway ceilings can trap heat.

Uneven temperatures usually trace back to a few common causes

  • Duct runs that are too long or poorly balanced
  • Not enough return air upstairs
  • Leaky ducts dumping cool air into attic space
  • Sun exposure heating certain rooms more than others
  • Thermostat location that does not represent the whole home

Sometimes the fix is as simple as adjusting dampers and confirming airflow at each register. Sometimes it involves adding return capacity or correcting a duct section that is pinched. We focus on what the house is doing, not what we wish it was doing.

Most central AC systems use a wall thermostat with basic scheduling. Some homeowners want more control. Others want simple controls that anyone in the house can use without an engineering degree.

During installation we can review

  • Filter reminders so maintenance does not become a mystery
