What does a central AC installation include

A proper central AC installation is more than placing an outdoor unit and moving on. It is about building a reliable path for air to travel, then confirming the equipment and controls keep it steady. If airflow or duct condition is a concern, Air Duct Cleaning in Liberty Station, CA may be worth discussing as part of the overall system plan.

A typical install may include

  • A site visit to confirm layout, duct routes, and electrical access
  • Equipment selection based on the home needs and constraints
  • Setting or replacing the outdoor condenser
  • Installing or adapting the indoor coil and air handler or tying into an existing furnace
  • Refrigerant line set installation or replacement when needed
  • Condensate drain routing so moisture leaves the system correctly
  • Thermostat installation and setup
  • Duct connections, sealing where accessible, and airflow checks
  • Start up, testing, and a walk through of basic operation

If ducts are damaged or undersized, we will tell you. Central air depends on air movement. If the duct paths are too small or leaky, comfort can suffer and it may point to options like Air Duct Replacement in Liberty Station, CA when the existing ductwork no longer fits the job.

How we size a central AC system for Liberty Station

Sizing is about matching the equipment to the building and how it is used. Bigger is not always better. An oversized system can cycle more often and feel less consistent. For background on how cooling works, you can review air conditioning details.

We look at items like

  • Number of occupants and heat producing appliances
  • Existing duct design and return air paths

A two story townhome can feel hot upstairs even when the downstairs is comfortable. Sometimes the system is not too small. Sometimes the airflow balance is off, or return air upstairs is limited.
