How do you choose the right system size

System size is not about choosing the biggest unit that fits. Oversized systems can cool fast and shut off fast which can leave comfort uneven. Undersized systems can run long and struggle on warm afternoons.

We size based on the home needs and match equipment to the ductwork reality. Golden Hill homes can have shaded lots, strong afternoon sun in west facing rooms, older construction quirks, and converted spaces like garages or rear additions. How do I know if my ducts can handle central AC

We look at airflow signs and physical duct details. Some clues you may notice include rooms that barely get air, loud vent noise, dust buildup near vents, doors that shift when the system runs, or only one small return for a larger home. If changes are needed, we talk through practical options that may include sealing, resizing a few runs, or improving return air capacity.

Will central AC help with upstairs heat and hot bedrooms

It often can, but the details matter. Homes with second stories, lofts, or steep rooflines can trap heat. If the duct layout is weak upstairs, new AC alone may not solve it. We look for adequate supply runs, a return path, and insulation clues, and we can coordinate improvements such as Air Duct Repair in Golden Hill, CA when airflow is the root issue.

Getting ready for your first hot day

  • Replace or clean filters on a regular schedule
