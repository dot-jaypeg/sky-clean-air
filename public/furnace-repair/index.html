Common furnace problems we see across San Diego

Our climate is mild but it brings unique challenges. Here is what we see most in local homes.

  • Salt air corrosion near the coast. La Jolla Pacific Beach and Point Loma homes often show rust on burners and heat exchangers. Early cleaning and anti corrosion steps help.

  • Thermostat placement problems. If the thermostat sits near a window or a drafty door the furnace short cycles or runs too long. We can recommend a better location or a smarter control with Thermostat Installation.
  • Filter sizing and access. Many tract homes use odd filter sizes hidden behind a grille. We can set up a better filter plan so you do not forget it for months with Media Filter Installation.
  • Noisy blowers at night. Townhomes and condos in Mission Valley and University City may have closets near bedrooms. We balance airflow and fix vibration to lower noise with Airflow Balancing.
  • Duct leaks and poor airflow. Homes in Chula Vista El Cajon and La Mesa often lose heat in leaky ducts. Sealing and cleaning ducts can bring rooms back into balance and lower bills with Air Duct Cleaning and Duct Sealing.

Repair tune up or replace

Wondering if your old furnace is worth fixing We give clear guidance that fits your home and budget.

Choose repair if

  • The furnace is under 12 to 15 years old
  • The repair is minor and parts are easy to find
  • The system has been reliable until now
  • Your bills are normal and airflow is strong

Consider replacement if

  • The furnace is 15 to 20 years old or older
  • Major parts fail like the heat exchanger or control board
  • You face repeated breakdowns each season
  • You want safer venting lower noise or better comfort

We also offer tune ups that can stretch the life of your system. Many San Diego furnaces only run at night for a few months each year so a well timed service visit can keep small issues from turning into breakdowns. Ask about HVAC Tune Up or plan ahead with Furnace Installation when replacement makes sense.

A yearly tune up can keep your system stable during our cooler months. What we do during a typical tune up

The filter and airflow connection

Low airflow is the silent troublemaker. Many no heat calls start with a clogged filter. We can help you set a simple filter routine.

  • Change 1 inch filters every 1 to 2 months during heating season
  • Change 4 to 5 inch media filters every 4 to 6 months with Media Filter Installation
  • Use the right MERV rating for your system
  • Keep supply and return vents clear of furniture and rugs

If your furnace shuts off on high limit or makes a whistling sound it might be starved for air. A better filter setup and clean ducts can fix that fast.

Small checks you can try before you call

We are happy to help over the phone if a simple step fixes it. Try these quick checks

  • Thermostat. Set to Heat and a temperature above room temp. Replace batteries if it uses them. Ask about professional Thermostat Installation or Thermostat Programming if needed.
  • Filter. Pull the filter and check for heavy dust. If it is clogged replace it.
  • Breakers and switches. Make sure the furnace switch is on and the breaker is not tripped.
  • Vents. Open all supply and return vents. Do not block them with furniture.
  • Pilot light. If you have an older standing pilot and know how to relight it safely follow the label on the furnace. If you are unsure wait for us.

If it still will not heat call 619-304-8822. We will take it from there.

Real world repair scenarios we handle every week

  • No heat in a coastal bungalow. A rusted flame sensor in Pacific Beach caused the furnace to shut off after ignition. We cleaned the burners replaced the sensor and tightened vent screws. Heat restored in under an hour.
  • Short cycling in a Clairemont attic furnace. A clogged filter and restricted return caused overheating. We replaced the filter adjusted blower speed and opened blocked vents. The system ran smoothly and rooms warmed evenly.
  • Gas smell in a North Park duplex. Tenant noticed a faint gas odor near the closet furnace. We shut off gas tested connections and replaced a leaking flex connector. Safety test passed and heat returned.
  • Loud banging at startup in Chula Vista. Delayed ignition caused a boom at light off. We cleaned burners set proper gap for the igniter and tested flame. Startup became quiet and consistent.
  • Heat pump stuck in cool mode in Sorrento Valley. The reversing valve was stuck. We replaced the valve checked refrigerant charge and verified defrost. The home warmed up right away.
