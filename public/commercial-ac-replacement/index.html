The replacement process step by step

1. On site assessment and load sizing

We visit your property to review existing units ductwork electrical gas and controls. We note access for cranes or lifts and any roof issues like weak curbs or rusted rails. We gather data for proper sizing using ACCA Manual N methods floor area occupancy equipment loads and glazing. Correct sizing avoids oversized short cycling or undersized units that run hot and never catch up. If needed we can include a thorough commercial HVAC inspection and targeted commercial HVAC diagnostics.

Questions we will ask

  • What areas struggle to stay cool or consistent
  • What are your business hours and busiest times
  • Do you have server rooms kitchens or medical suites that need tight control
  • Do you plan renovations or added occupants

2. Options and clear pricing

We present one or more system paths often including

  • High efficiency rooftop packaged units with economizers such as a commercial rooftop unit
  • Heat pump options to reduce gas use that align with our heat pump installation expertise
  • VRF systems for buildings with many zones
  • Split systems for suites labs and server rooms
  • Dedicated outside air systems to improve ventilation and dehumidification

Each option comes with

  • Equipment model and efficiency ratings
  • Scope of work including duct repairs controls and electrical along with commercial HVAC control setup
  • Timeline and install strategy to reduce downtime
  • Potential SDG&E incentives and estimated savings window
  • Total investment and payment options

3. Permits and code compliance

We handle permits with the City of San Diego Development Services or your local city including Chula Vista National City El Cajon La Mesa Poway Carlsbad Oceanside and the County for unincorporated areas. We follow current Title 24 energy rules part of the California Building Standards Code which may require economizers demand controlled ventilation with CO2 sensors and acceptance testing by a qualified technician. We coordinate inspections so you do not have to chase paperwork.

4. Logistics and scheduling

We build a schedule that fits your operations. Many projects happen before opening hours after closing or over weekends. For rooftop changeouts we plan crane lifts street or parking control and early morning access to avoid traffic. Downtown lifts may need traffic control plans. Coastal sites often need corrosion aware rigging and staging. Warehouses in Otay Mesa or Miramar often need forklifts and scissor lifts for high duct connections.

5. Removal install and startup

  • Protect floors desks and inventory where needed
  • Remove old units and haul them away for proper disposal
  • Set curb adapters and verify roof penetration seals
  • Set new equipment connect gas and electric and seal duct connections supported by professional duct repairs
  • Install or tune economizers and CO2 sensors
  • Integrate with your thermostat building automation or new control package
  • Balance airflow using commercial duct balancing and set charge to spec
  • Run functional tests and required acceptance testing
  • Leave the site clean and ready for business

6. Training and handoff

We walk you through operation basics filter schedules and smart thermostat or BMS features. We leave you with the submittals maintenance schedule and a service contact. Ask about commercial thermostat installation and thermostat programming for easier control along with ongoing commercial AC maintenance.

System options for San Diego buildings

Every building has its own mix of spaces operating hours and comfort targets. Below are common solutions we install across the county.

Rooftop packaged units

Great for retail offices light industrial and schools. Explore options for a modern commercial rooftop unit.

  • Capacities from 3 to 100 tons
  • Gas heat or heat pump versions
  • Economizers that bring in cool outside air on mild days
  • Variable speed supply fans for quieter more efficient operation
  • Factory curb adapters to fit existing roof openings

Where they shine

  • Kearny Mesa office parks
  • Mission Valley shopping centers
  • Sorrento Valley tech campuses
  • Chula Vista and National City retail strips
  • Warehouse offices in Miramar and Otay Mesa

Split systems and ducted air handlers

Useful where rooftop access is limited or where you want indoor equipment. Learn more about a commercial split system approach.

  • Condensers outdoors air handlers indoors
  • Good for medical suites server rooms and interior zones
  • Easy zoning with multiple air handlers
  • Pairs well with existing ductwork or new duct runs

VRF systems for multi zone control

VRF gives precise control across many rooms or suites. It supports heating and cooling at the same time in different zones with heat recovery. Learn about the concept of variable refrigerant flow.

Best fit

  • Multi tenant offices in UTC and Downtown
  • Schools churches and mixed use properties
  • Hotels and extended stay buildings
  • Medical and dental offices with varying temperature needs

Benefits

  • High efficiency under part load
  • Quiet indoor units
  • Small refrigerant piping compared to large duct trunks

Dedicated outside air systems

These units condition outside air separately from the heating and cooling system. They control humidity and ventilation volume well. Pairing a DOAS with VRF or packaged equipment often boosts comfort and code compliance.

Controls and building automation

Smart controls cut energy use and make your life easier. We handle commercial HVAC control setup and thermostat programming and can assist with commercial HVAC zoning setup and system integration.

  • Advanced thermostats for small sites
  • BMS integration for larger properties
  • CO2 based ventilation control for meeting rooms
  • Demand limit settings to avoid peak demand spikes
  • Occupancy scheduling and holiday sets
  • Fault detection alerts sent to your inbox

Indoor air quality for wildfire smoke and smog days

San Diego has days when smoke drifts in from fires or smog builds up. Your occupants should not breathe that inside.

  • Filters that support MERV 13 or better when the equipment allows
  • Filter tracks and door seals checked to stop bypass
  • Economizer programming that reduces outside air during poor air days then restores normal intake
  • Optional air cleaners after evaluating your space and goals
  • Regular filter changes and coil cleaning to keep airflow strong supported by commercial AC maintenance

Sky Clean Air got started with air ducts so we know airflow. During replacement we inspect your supply and return ducts seal leaks and correct poor transitions. Better static pressure means better comfort and lower utility spend. Consider targeted services like commercial air duct cleaning or commercial duct leakage testing.

Coastal corrosion and roof survival

Salt air can be rough on rooftop units in Point Loma Pacific Beach Ocean Beach La Jolla and Coronado. We help your equipment last longer near the water.

  • Coil coatings that resist salt
  • Stainless or coated fasteners
  • Protective hail guards that also cut bird damage
  • Regular wash down plans to control salt build up
  • Correct clearances to reduce recirculation on windy days

Seagulls think every roof is theirs. We work around that.

Energy efficiency and SDG&E strategies

Electric rates and demand charges make a big difference in annual cost. We set up systems with efficiency and utility timing in mind.

  • High IEER rooftop units that shine under part load conditions
  • Variable speed fans and compressors that sip power during mild weather
  • Economizers for free cooling on cool mornings and evenings
  • Demand controlled ventilation that cuts fan and cooling load when rooms are empty
  • Setpoint strategies to ease load from 4 pm to 9 pm when time of use rates spike
  • Staggered start sequences so multiple units do not start at once
  • Heat pump options where gas use is limited or costly

We also explore SDG&E rebates for qualifying equipment and controls. Every site is different but we flag the incentives that match your scope.

A quick word on noise and comfort

Newer units with variable speed fans and compressors run quieter. They ramp up and down instead of slamming on and off. That reduces noise over conference rooms and cuts wear. Combined with better duct balancing and diffuser placement your space will feel more even. You should not need a sweater in the bullpen and a fan in the corner office.

Economizers and free cooling in San Diego

San Diego mornings and evenings are often cool enough for outside air to do most of the work. Economizers sense temperature and enthalpy and bring in cool air while dumping warm stale air. When programmed well this trims energy use and keeps spaces fresh. We verify dampers actuators and sensors then lock in the settings so you get the benefit.

Demand control for meeting rooms and classrooms

Big rooms do not always need full ventilation. CO2 sensors can reduce ventilation when spaces are empty then ramp up when people arrive. That cuts fan energy and cooling load. Title 24 often requires this. We handle setup and the acceptance testing so it works from day one. Learn more about demand controlled ventilation.

Temporary cooling plans

Need cooling during replacement for a lab server room or tenant event We can bring in portable units spot coolers or short term splits to bridge the gap. Tell us what cannot get warm and we will protect it. If timing is critical ask about commercial emergency HVAC services.

Reroofing and HVAC together

If you plan a reroof it often makes sense to coordinate AC replacement at the same time. We work with roofers to set curbs seal penetrations and avoid duplicate crane picks. This reduces roof patching and keeps warranties intact on the roofing system.

What makes a good install great

  • Level curbs and vibration isolation
  • Correct refrigerant charge and airflow

  • Controls labeled and schedules programmed
  • Filter access that is easy for your staff to service

Commercial AC Replacement: Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consider replacing my commercial AC system?

If your system is old, frequently breaks down, or isn’t cooling properly, it may be time to consider a replacement.

What are the main benefits of replacing my commercial AC?

New systems are more energy efficient, improve comfort, and can lower your energy bills.

How long does a commercial AC replacement usually take?

Most replacements can be completed in one to two days, depending on your building and system size.

Will my business be disrupted during the replacement?

Sky Clean Air works to minimize any disruption and can schedule work during off-hours if needed.

Do I need to replace the entire HVAC system or just the AC unit?

This depends on your current system’s age and condition—Sky Clean Air can assess and recommend the best option.

How do I choose the right AC unit for my business?

Our team at Sky Clean Air will evaluate your space and needs to help you select the best system.

Can replacing my AC system improve my indoor air quality?

Yes, newer systems can improve air circulation and filtration, helping maintain a healthier indoor environment.

Is financing available for commercial AC replacement?

Sky Clean Air offers various payment options to help make replacement more affordable for your business.

How often should a commercial AC system be replaced?

Most commercial AC systems last 10-15 years, but replacement may be needed sooner if there are issues.
