When should you replace your AC instead of repairing it

Sometimes a repair is the right move. Other times, replacing the AC is the cleaner path forward, especially when the unit is past its prime or repairs are stacking up. If you are still deciding, you may also want to review AC Repair in Hillcrest, CA and how it compares to replacement.

Common signs we see around Hillcrest include

What is included in an AC replacement

AC replacement is more than swapping a box outside. Hillcrest properties often have tight side yards, older electrical setups, and ductwork that has been changed over the years. The job needs to match what is actually there.

A typical AC replacement may include

  • Confirming the current system type and capacity
  • Reviewing thermostat location and controls
  • Removing the old outdoor unit and indoor coil or air handler
  • Setting and securing the new equipment
  • Connecting refrigerant lines and electrical
  • Checking condensate drainage
  • Testing startup, airflow, and temperature split
  • Reviewing basic operation with you before we leave

If your system is paired with ductwork, we also pay attention to what the ducts are doing. A new AC cannot fix crushed flex duct or a return that is starved for air. In some homes, Air Duct Repair in Hillcrest, CA or Air Duct Maintenance in Hillcrest, CA may be part of getting better comfort.

Will a new AC lower energy use
