What happens during an AC replacement

People hear replacement and picture a week of chaos. Most replacements are simpler than that, especially when the ductwork and electrical are in decent shape.

Typical steps for AC replacement in Clairemont Mesa East

  • Walkthrough and system review We look at the indoor and outdoor equipment, thermostat, filter setup, and vent locations
  • Sizing and load considerations We do not guess. We look at your layout, sun exposure, insulation, window types, and how you use the space
  • Options review We talk through system type and efficiency ranges, plus comfort features like variable speed blowers or better humidity control
  • Removal of old equipment We disconnect and remove the old unit safely and keep the work area tidy
  • Installation and connections We set the new outdoor condenser, indoor coil or air handler, and make electrical, refrigerant, and drain connections
  • Startup and testing We verify refrigerant charge, temperature split, airflow, and safety controls. We also check thermostat operation and basic duct performance
  • Homeowner walkthrough We show you filter location, thermostat basics, and what normal operation sounds like

What size AC do I need for my Clairemont Mesa East home

The right size is not just square footage. It is also ceiling height, insulation, windows, and how the home is used. A home office with two monitors and a closed door is a small heat generator, and yes, it counts.

What SEER or efficiency should I choose

Efficiency is about more than the label. Real life results depend on installation quality, airflow, and how the thermostat is used. For more steady performance, pairing replacement with ongoing AC Maintenance in Clairemont Mesa East, CA can help protect comfort over time.

Will a new AC fix hot rooms and uneven cooling

It can help, but hot rooms usually have more than one cause. A new system is a strong start, yet airflow and building layout still matter.

Common causes of uneven cooling in Clairemont Mesa East
