How we choose the right size system

Bigger is not automatically better. An oversized AC can short cycle, which can mean uneven comfort and humidity issues. An undersized unit may run constantly. We consider square footage, windows, insulation, duct layout, and occupancy patterns. A home office that runs all day is not the same as a place that is empty until evening. If you have ever felt the AC is on but it still feels sticky, that is often a sizing and airflow conversation.
