Can a new AC fix hot spots in older College East homes

Sometimes yes and sometimes not by itself. Hot spots are usually tied to airflow and return paths. If duct issues are contributing, options can include Air Duct Repair in College East, CA or airflow improvements found during evaluation.

Common causes of hot spots

  • Undersized or leaky ducts to certain rooms

  • Doors kept closed without a good return air path
  • Poor attic insulation above the hottest rooms
  • Sun exposure through west facing windows
  • A thermostat placed where it does not represent the whole home

Ways we can approach it during replacement
