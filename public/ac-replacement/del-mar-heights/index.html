How do you choose the right size AC system

Bigger is not automatically better. An oversized system can cool fast and shut off fast, leaving humidity control and comfort uneven. An undersized system may run too long and still feel behind on hot days. For background on humidity and comfort, you can review https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indoor_air_quality.

When Sky Clean Air helps size a replacement, we look at

  • Square footage and ceiling heights
  • Window size and sun exposure, especially west and south facing glass
  • Insulation condition and attic heat
  • Duct layout and return air placement
  • Number of occupants and typical usage patterns
  • Special spaces like home gyms, wine rooms, and server closets
  • Remodel changes that alter airflow needs
