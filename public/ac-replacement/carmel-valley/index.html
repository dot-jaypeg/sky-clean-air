Will a new AC fix hot and cold rooms

Sometimes yes. Sometimes it is only part of the story. If your current system is undersized, failing, or has a weak blower, replacement can improve comfort. If the issue is duct related, you may still have uneven rooms unless the ducts are addressed. Duct cleaning during an AC replacement

Replacing an AC does not automatically clean the duct system. If ducts are loaded, that new system is pushing air through the same pathway. Duct cleaning can help when you see dust puffing from registers, after a remodel, after moving into a home with unknown duct history, when filtration has been poor, or when return plenums and grilles show heavy buildup. If this sounds familiar, review Air Duct Cleaning in Carmel Valley, CA or ask about Air Duct Installation in Carmel Valley, CA when airflow changes are part of the solution.

Do you need to replace the furnace too

Not always. If you have a combined heating and cooling setup, we look at the age and condition of the furnace or air handler, compatibility between indoor and outdoor components, blower performance and airflow capacity, duct condition and static pressure concerns, and space constraints in closets and attics. If your heating unit is running well and matches the plan, you may not need to change it.

Heat pump replacement versus AC replacement

An AC cools. A heat pump cools and heats using the same outdoor unit. Heat pumps are common in Carmel Valley because winters are mild and heat pumps can be a good fit for many homes. If you already have a heat pump, replacement means selecting a new heat pump outdoor unit and confirming the indoor match. If you have a gas furnace with AC, replacement may focus on the AC side unless you want to change the heating approach. For background on how heat pumps work, see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_pump.
