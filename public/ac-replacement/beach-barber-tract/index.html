What does AC replacement include

How do we size a new AC system correctly

Sizing is where comfort starts. Oversized systems can short cycle and leave humidity behind. Undersized systems can run nonstop and still feel behind.
  • Home size and layout
  • Insulation and window exposure
  • Ceiling height and attic conditions
  • Shade from trees and neighboring structures
  • Duct design and return air path
  • How you actually use the space day to day
A small real world scenario. You work from home in the front room and cook a lot in the evening. The heat load is different than a house where everyone is out until sunset. We factor that into the recommendation so the system matches your routine, not just the square footage.
