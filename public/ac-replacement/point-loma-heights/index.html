What does AC replacement include

AC replacement is not only swapping a box outside. In Point Loma Heights, many comfort problems come from a mix of equipment condition, duct layout, and airflow limits inside older homes. A typical AC replacement scope can include

  • Duct size and return air location

Will a new AC help with hot spots in older Point Loma Heights houses

It can, but hot spots usually have more than one cause. A new AC may improve overall performance, yet room to room comfort often comes down to airflow and building envelope.
