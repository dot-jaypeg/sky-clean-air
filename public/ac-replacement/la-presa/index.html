Is it time to replace your AC or just repair it

Common signs La Presa customers mention before replacing

How do you choose the right size AC for your home

Bigger is not automatically better. An oversized unit can short cycle and leave humidity behind. An undersized unit can run too long and still not catch up on hot afternoons.

What we look at during sizing conversations

  • Square footage and ceiling height
  • Insulation and window types
  • Sun exposure, especially west facing rooms in late afternoon
  • Number of occupants and typical cooking heat load
  • Duct sizing and airflow limits
  • Layout changes, like a converted garage or enclosed patio

A small example we see in La Presa is a family adding a home office in a converted garage off the side of the house. The old system might have worked okay before, then suddenly it cannot keep up. The fix is not always cranking the thermostat lower. It might mean adjusting duct runs, adding a return path, or choosing equipment that matches the updated load. When duct changes are part of the discussion, Air Duct Repair in La Presa, CA can be relevant.

Will you need ductwork changes for a new AC

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. Ductwork does not have to be perfect, but it has to move enough air. A new system installed on tired ducts can feel disappointing.

Signs ducts might need attention

  • Return grille that seems too small for the system

How do you keep a new AC running smoothly after replacement

New equipment still needs basic care. The goal is steady airflow and a clean system.

Habits that help

  • Change filters on a regular schedule that matches your home and pets
  • Keep supply vents open and unblocked by furniture or rugs
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of weeds, boxes, and stored items
  • Rinse light debris off the outdoor coil when safe to do so
  • Watch for drain line backups if you see water near the indoor unit

If your home is dusty, or you have allergy concerns, duct cleaning may be worth discussing. A new AC moving air through dusty ducts can stir up old buildup. Sky Clean Air can help you decide when duct cleaning is useful and when it is just extra.
