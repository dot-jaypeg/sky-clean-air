When should you replace your AC instead of repairing it

  • Your AC runs long cycles and the house still feels sticky

  • Some rooms are cool and others feel like a toaster
  • The outdoor unit is louder than it used to be
  • You notice weak airflow at multiple vents
  • The system struggles during warm spells that roll in from inland areas

What does AC replacement include

AC replacement is more than sliding in a new box and calling it a day. A clean install is about fit, airflow, and safe connections. If your current setup has airflow issues, pairing replacement planning with Air Duct Repair in Muirlands West, CA or Air Duct Replacement in Muirlands West, CA can make a noticeable difference.

A typical AC replacement in Muirlands West may include

Can you replace just the outdoor unit

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. It depends on equipment compatibility and the condition of the indoor coil or air handler. Mismatched components can lead to poor performance and more service issues.

During a visit in Muirlands West, we check

  • Model compatibility between indoor and outdoor components
  • Refrigerant line condition and sizing
  • Indoor coil cleanliness and age
  • Blower condition and airflow capability

If the indoor side is in good shape and compatible, partial replacement may make sense. If not, a full system replacement is often the cleaner path.

Will AC replacement help with hot and cold spots
