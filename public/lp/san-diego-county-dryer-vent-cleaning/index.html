Questions We Hear

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We look at your vent, explain what it needs, and give you a written price. You decide whether to go ahead. Also check our current specials.

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

It depends on how much laundry you do and how long the vent run is. Big households and long attic or roof runs need it more often. If drying times are creeping up, that's your sign.

Why does winter matter for dryer vents?

Most homes run more loads in winter: heavier clothes, extra bedding, holiday guests. More loads mean more lint in the vent, and a restricted vent makes the dryer run hotter. Cleaning before the busy stretch keeps things safer and faster.

Can you fix a crushed or disconnected vent?

Yes. If we find a crushed hose, a loose connection or a vent that needs rerouting, we'll show you and give you a written price. See our dryer vent services.
