Questions We Hear

What does the $59 whole-house duct cleaning include?

It covers cleaning the ducts throughout your home. Details and terms are on our specials page. Your tech will confirm the scope and give you a written price before starting, so there are no surprises.

Why clean my ducts before winter instead of spring?

Because the heater is about to start moving air through them. The first few heat cycles push out whatever settled in the ducts over summer, right as the house closes up for the season. Cleaning first means that dust ends up in our equipment, not your living room.

Do you really serve Orange County now?

Yes. We've expanded from San Diego into Orange County, from San Clemente to Brea, with the same crew habits and upfront pricing. See the Orange County service area page for cities we cover.

Will you try to sell me a new system?

No. We do a whole-home HVAC inspection during duct cleaning, and if your system is around 15 years or older we'll mention it. That's information, not a sales pitch. What you do with it is up to you.
