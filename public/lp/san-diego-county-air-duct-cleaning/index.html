Questions We Hear

How much is duct cleaning right now?

We're running a $59 whole-house air duct cleaning special this season. See current specials for the terms. Your tech confirms everything in writing before starting.

Does duct cleaning help after wildfire smoke?

Smoke and fine ash can settle inside ducts on bad air days and get pushed back out when the system runs. Cleaning removes that buildup. If you're dealing with ongoing air quality concerns, ask your tech about other options on our air duct services page.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of your home and how many vents you have. Your tech will give you a time estimate when they arrive, along with the written price.

What else do you check while you're there?

We usually do a whole-home HVAC inspection with a duct cleaning. If something looks worn, or your system is around 15 years old or more, we'll tell you honestly. No pressure either way. If your heater needs attention, see our heating services.

Which areas do you cover?

All of San Diego County, from San Ysidro to Fallbrook. Our office is in Kearny Mesa. See the San Diego County service area page, or call 619-304-8822.
