Questions We Hear

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost?

It depends on how long your vent is and where it exits the house, so we give you a free estimate and a written price before any work. Check current specials too in case something applies.

How do I know my dryer vent needs cleaning?

Common signs: clothes need more than one cycle, the dryer or laundry room gets unusually hot, there's a burning smell, or the outside flap barely opens when the dryer runs. Any of those is worth a look.

Is a clogged dryer vent really a fire risk?

Yes. Lint is flammable, and when the vent is restricted, heat builds up around it. Keeping the line clear is one of the simpler safety jobs in a home. It also lets the dryer finish loads faster.

My vent goes to the roof. Can you clean it?

Yes. Attic and roof-exit vents are common in Orange County homes, and they tend to need cleaning more often. We'll check the roof cap as well. More on our dryer vent services page.
