Questions We Hear

What does the $29 furnace safety inspection cover?

A tech checks the parts of your furnace that matter most for safe operation: the burners and flame, the venting, the heat exchanger and the area around the unit for carbon monoxide. If we find something, we'll explain it and give you a written price before doing anything. See current specials for the terms.

Is carbon monoxide something I should worry about?

It's worth checking, not worrying about. A gas furnace that's venting properly and has a sound heat exchanger keeps combustion gases out of your home. The inspection confirms that, and if something isn't right we'll tell you plainly and talk through your options. A working CO detector near bedrooms is a good idea in any home with gas appliances.

Do you work on heat pumps too?

Yes. We repair and tune up heat pumps as well as gas furnaces. If your heat pump cools fine in summer but struggles to heat, that's a common call this time of year. Learn more on our heating services page.

Do you serve my part of Orange County?

We cover Orange County from San Clemente to Brea, including Irvine, Costa Mesa, Anaheim, Fullerton and Laguna Niguel. See the full list on our Orange County service area page, or call 619-304-8822 to check your address.

My furnace is old. Will you push me to replace it?

No. If your system is around 15 years or older, we'll tell you so and explain what we see, but the decision is yours. Plenty of older furnaces just need a repair and a tune-up. If replacement does make sense, we offer free estimates and financing.
