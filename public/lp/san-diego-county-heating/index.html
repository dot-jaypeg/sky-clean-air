Questions We Hear

Why does my heater smell like burning when I first turn it on?

Usually it's dust that settled on the burners and heat exchanger over the summer burning off. It should fade within the first run. If the smell lingers, smells electrical, or you see smoke, turn the system off and call us at 619-304-8822.

What's in the $29 furnace safety inspection?

We check the flame and burners, ignition, venting, heat exchanger and the area around the furnace for carbon monoxide, then tell you what we found in plain English. It's a winter special; see current specials for terms.

What's the difference between the inspection and a tune-up?

The safety inspection focuses on whether your furnace is running safely. A tune-up goes further: cleaning, adjusting and testing so the system runs efficiently through the season. Your tech can tell you which makes sense for your system. More on our heating page.

My heat pump blows cool air in heat mode. Is it broken?

Not necessarily. Heat pumps deliver gentler, steadier heat than a furnace, so the air can feel cooler than you're used to. But if the house isn't warming up, or the outdoor unit is icing over or not running, it's worth a visit. We repair heat pumps across San Diego County.
