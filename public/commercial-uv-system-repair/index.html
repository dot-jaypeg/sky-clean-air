Why UV Systems Matter for Commercial Buildings in San Diego

San Diego is famous for sunshine but inside commercial HVAC equipment dark and damp areas around coils and drain pans can invite growth and odor. UV-C lights target the surface of coils and nearby components to keep them cleaner for longer. That translates into steadier airflow better heat exchange and fewer odor issues. Signs Your UV System Needs Repair

Think your UV unit is failing or due for service. Watch for these warning signs. Do any of these sound familiar.

Common UV System Problems We Fix

Lamp Burnout or Weak Output

UV-C lamps lose output as they age. They may still glow but the germicidal output drops. In many commercial settings lamps need replacement every 9 to 12 months. We test output not just visible light. If the lamp is weak we replace it with the correct model then set a clear replacement schedule. We also offer Commercial UV Air Purifier Replacement when systems are outdated.

Ballast or Power Supply Failure

Ballasts can fail from heat power spikes or water intrusion near the air handler. Common signs include flickering lamps buzzing sounds or lamps that will not stay on. We test voltage and current then install a new ballast if needed. We route wires cleanly and secure them to avoid future chafing.

Wrong Lamp Placement

If the lamp sits too far from the coil or is blocked by a bracket the coil does not get the treatment it needs. We adjust the mount and angle so UV hits the coil face evenly. We also add shield plates where needed to prevent stray light from hitting plastics or wire insulation.

Dirty Coils and Biofilm Buildup

UV helps keep coils clean but if the lamp has been off for a while the coil may need a manual cleaning to reset the system. We can clean the coil drain pan and surrounding surfaces then bring the UV back online so it can keep the surface clean. Pair with Commercial AC cleaning for best results.

Control Board or Timer Problems

Some systems run UV on a timer or tie it to the blower. If the controller fails the UV may never start. We check relays fuses and control signals. If needed we can bypass a not critical control temporarily to restore UV while we order the correct part with your approval.

Faulty Interlocks and Door Switches

Safety switches that disable UV when a panel opens are a good idea but they can fail. If the switch is out of alignment or broken the UV will not stay on. We realign or replace the switch and test operation.

Wiring and Heat Damage

Poor routing or long term heat can crack wire insulation. We repair wiring add protective loom and restore proper grounding.

UV Hitting the Wrong Materials

UV can degrade some plastics foam and wire jackets over time. We add shields or reroute susceptible materials so the lamp treats the coil not the insulation.

Repair vs Replacement

Not sure if a simple repair will do the job. Here is how we help you decide.

  • Age of the lamps and ballast. If both are at the end of their expected life a full refresh can save repeat trips
  • Coil size and air handler design. If your coil is large or double stacked a single lamp may be underpowered. We may suggest adding a second lamp or relocating hardware for better coverage
  • Reported issues. Persistent odors or rapid coil fouling can point to undersized UV or poor placement not just a failed lamp
  • Operating hours. Sites that run long hours may need higher output lamps or a more frequent change schedule
  • Parts availability. If your existing ballast or lamp model is no longer available a simple upgrade kit may be faster and more cost effective than chasing rare parts

We walk you through the options with clear pricing. Many fixes are quick. In some cases a small upgrade in lamp layout or output fixes long standing problems and cuts service calls down the road.
