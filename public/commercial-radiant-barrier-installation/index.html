What a Radiant Barrier Does and Why It Works

Radiant barriers reflect radiant heat from the sun before it enters your building. Think of it like shade for your roof from the inside. The material is a reflective foil or foil faced product that sends heat back out instead of letting it soak into your attic or roof deck. That reduces the load on your HVAC system, keeps indoor areas cooler, and helps you maintain steadier temperatures across your floor plan. Learn more about radiant barriers from Energy Saver guidance at energy.gov and the overview on the Radiant barrier page.

  • Without a radiant barrier the roof deck soaks up heat and radiates it into the building.
  • With a radiant barrier much of that heat is reflected at the roof level.
  • The AC does not have to work as hard which can reduce energy use and extend equipment life. Consider pairing with Commercial HVAC Maintenance for ongoing performance.

Does it still help in cooler months. Yes. Radiant barriers can reduce heat loss from warm spaces to a cooler roof at night which helps control temperature swings in shoulder seasons.

Installation Methods by Roof Type

Different roofs call for different methods. The goal stays the same. Keep radiant heat out keep your building cooler and keep air flowing correctly.

Metal roofs with purlins

  • The barrier can be attached to the bottom of purlins or just below forming a continuous reflective plane.
  • We seal overlaps and create tight edges around beams.
  • Ventilation paths are preserved to prevent moisture issues with options to add Commercial Attic Ventilation Upgrades when needed.

Wood roof decks

  • We staple or fasten radiant barrier foil to the underside of the deck between rafters.
  • Gaps around penetrations are sealed.
  • Special attention goes to skylights and curbs which can be major heat sources.

Low slope commercial roofs over offices

  • In many offices with drop ceilings the plenum space is the target.
  • The barrier is installed above the grid system with fire safe fasteners.
  • Seams are sealed and clearances are maintained around fixtures.

Tilt up concrete with bar joists

  • We attach the barrier along the underside of joists to create a uniform surface.
  • Mechanical units duct runs and sprinkler lines are bridged with precision.
  • Access points are created for future maintenance and Commercial HVAC Maintenance.

Special areas

  • Server rooms under hot roofs may get an extra layer or targeted wrap near the area.
  • Walk in coolers or food storage zones benefit from barriers that reduce radiant load on insulated walls or ceilings.

Radiant Barrier vs Extra Insulation vs Cool Roof Coatings

We get this question a lot. Which option should you pick. You might not need to choose just one. Each option tackles heat load in a different way.

  • Radiant barrier reflects radiant heat before it enters. Good for solar driven gains. Works well in attics plenums and under metal roofs.
  • Extra insulation slows heat transfer by conduction. Helpful for both hot and cool seasons. Often used with a radiant barrier for better results. Ask about Commercial Blown In Attic Insulation Installation or Commercial Batt and Roll Insulation Installation.
  • Cool roof coatings reflect sunlight at the exterior surface. Great for roofs that are due for maintenance and can accept coating systems.

We will explain where your building gets the most benefit. In many San Diego buildings a radiant barrier plus duct sealing produces noticeable results without a major roof project.

Questions You Might Be Thinking About

How intrusive is the install

We work overhead and protect your space below. Many installs happen outside your busiest hours. We organize materials keep walkways clear and coordinate with your team.

Will a radiant barrier trap moisture

We use perforated products where needed to allow vapor to pass and we keep airflow paths open. Our team evaluates ventilation so you get heat control without moisture problems. If upgrades are needed we can add Commercial Attic Ventilation Upgrades.

Can I put radiant barrier over existing insulation

Yes if installed correctly. The barrier should face an air space to reflect heat. We place it under the roof deck or above the ceiling not laying flat on insulation where it would lose effectiveness.

What about lighting and sprinklers

We maintain clearances and follow local fire codes. We place the barrier so lights speakers and sprinklers work as intended.

How soon will we notice a change

Many clients feel the difference the first warm week after install. Utility savings show up on your next cycle especially in summer and early fall.

How long does it last

Quality radiant barrier materials hold up for many years when installed correctly and kept intact. Roof leaks and major remodels are the main threats. We show you how to keep it protected.
