Signs you may need air duct maintenance

Some clues are obvious. Others are sneaky.

What you can do between professional visits

Small habits can reduce how much ends up in the returns.

A practical between visits list

  • Replace filters on a consistent schedule that matches your system and lifestyle
  • Use a vacuum with a good filter, especially on rugs near returns
  • Wipe vent grilles when you dust nearby surfaces
  • Keep doors open in rooms that need better return airflow, when practical
  • Avoid blocking returns with furniture, baskets, or that cute oversized floor mirror
  • If you do a small DIY project, cover nearby vents and clean up well after

A quick note on filters. A filter that does not fit snugly can allow air to bypass it. That can send dust straight into places you would rather keep cleaner.
