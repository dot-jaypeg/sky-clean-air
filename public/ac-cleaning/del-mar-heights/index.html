What is the difference between cleaning and just changing the filter

Changing the filter is regular upkeep. AC cleaning is removing residue that the filter never touched, or could not stop.

Filter changes help with

  • Catching airborne particles before they enter the system
  • Protecting the blower and coil from some dust
  • Supporting basic airflow

Cleaning helps with

  • Buildup already on interior surfaces
  • Dust stuck around return boots and register edges
  • Grime that forms where moisture shows up
  • We just moved in situations where history is unknown

If you want a simple rule of thumb, think of the filter as a net and cleaning as wiping down the area around the net.

What maintenance habits help keep the system cleaner

We like simple habits that actually happen in real homes.

  • Change the filter on a consistent schedule that matches your household
  • Make sure the filter fits snugly and is facing the right direction
  • Keep returns clear, no furniture pressed against them
  • Vacuum return grilles and nearby baseboards occasionally
  • Keep supply vents open unless you have a specific airflow plan
  • If you spot water staining near the indoor unit, mention it early
