How is AC cleaning different from changing the filter

Changing the filter is essential. It is also only one piece of the puzzle. A filter mainly protects the equipment and catches airborne particles moving through the return. It cannot remove the dust already stuck on vents, inside the return cavity, or on accessible components.

Think of it like sweeping your entryway but never wiping the shoes. You helped, but you did not finish the job.

Filter tips we share often

  • Make sure the filter is the correct size and fits snugly
  • Confirm airflow direction arrow is correct
  • Avoid forcing an overly thick filter if the system is not set up for it
  • Replace on a schedule that matches your space and usage, not a calendar app opinion

What should I do between cleanings to keep air fresher

A few small habits go a long way. No one wants to babysit an AC system, so keep it simple.

  • Replace filters before they look like a felt blanket
  • Keep return grilles clear of boxes and furniture
  • Vacuum around registers during normal cleaning
  • Use kitchen and bath exhaust fans when cooking or showering
  • If you are in a warehouse, manage door open time when possible
  • After windy days, check the return grille for quick dust buildup

An old idiom fits here. An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of dust on your coffee table.
