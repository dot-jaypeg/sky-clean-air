How do you know it is time to schedule a cleaning

Most people call after noticing a pattern. It is not usually one single day. It is more like, Okay, this has been bugging me for a month.

A few simple checks you can do before you call.

  • Look at a few supply vents and the main return vent. If the grille has buildup, the inside may too.
  • Check the filter condition and how quickly it gets dirty. If a fresh filter looks rough fast, something is feeding it extra debris.
  • Pay attention to startup odor. A brief dusty smell can happen. A repeating musty smell is a clue.
  • Notice uneven airflow. One back bedroom struggling while the living room blasts can point to airflow limits, sometimes linked to buildup.

If you want a quick gut check, take off one vent cover and look inside with your phone light. If you see clumps or heavy coating, it is worth talking through options.

Is AC cleaning the same as air duct cleaning

They overlap, but they are not always identical. Some people say AC cleaning and mean ducts only. Others mean everything involved with moving and conditioning the air. If the main goal is cleaning the pathways air travels, Air Duct Cleaning may be the right label.
