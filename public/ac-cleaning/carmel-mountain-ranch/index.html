How to tell if your vents or ducts need cleaning

You do not need special gadgets to get a clue. You just need a few simple checks. Returns often show buildup faster since they pull air in, and if buildup is significant, Air Duct Maintenance in Carmel Mountain Ranch, CA may also be worth discussing.

Try these quick signs

  • Look at the supply vent blades with a flashlight. If you see a visible film or debris, there is likely more in the pathway
  • Check the return grille. Returns often show buildup faster since they pull air in
  • Wipe test. Lightly wipe inside the vent opening with a clean white cloth. If it comes out gray fast, you have your answer
  • Sniff test. If you notice a persistent dusty smell only when the system runs, the airflow is stirring something up

If you are not sure, that is normal. Many systems look fine at the vent face but have buildup farther in.
