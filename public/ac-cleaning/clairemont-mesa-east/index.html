Is AC cleaning the same as changing the filter

Filter changes are great. They are also only part of the story.

A filter can catch a lot, but it cannot collect what has already settled in ducts, near vent openings, or in certain compartments. Filters can also be installed wrong, left too long, or sized incorrectly for the return.
