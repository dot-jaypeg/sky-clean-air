Will AC cleaning help with dust in the home

It can help, especially when the dust is coming from inside the system or from returns pulling in debris and recirculating it. It is not magic and it will not stop dust from open windows, pets, or foot traffic. Still, clearing out the airflow path often reduces the why is everything dusty again feeling.
