What parts get dirty and why it matters

Here is the plain language version of what we often find. For background on how these components work, you can also review the basics of air conditioning.

Blower and blower housing

The blower is the fan that moves air through your home. Dust can cling to blades and the housing. When buildup gets heavy, it can affect how smoothly air moves.

Evaporator coil

This coil helps cool the air. It can collect dust and film, especially when filtration is inconsistent or air leaks pull in debris. A dirtier coil can also hold onto odors.

Return area and filter slot

If the filter does not fit snugly, air can sneak around it. That air brings dust with it. Over time, the return pathway and cabinet area show it.

Drain pan and condensate line area

Cooling pulls moisture from air. That moisture drains away through a line. If the area around the drain gets grimy, it can lead to odors and messy buildup.

Supply registers and return grilles

These are what you see day to day. They can collect dust, pet hair, and whatever else is floating around.

AC cleaning is about addressing these areas with care, not just wiping vent covers and calling it done.

How to keep your AC cleaner between visits

You can do a few simple things that make a real difference.

Filters and airflow basics

Keep filters fitted and changed on time. A filter that fits poorly is like a screen door on a submarine. Air goes around it. Dust goes with it.
  • Use the correct size. If it is loose in the slot, it is not doing its job
  • Write the install date on the filter frame
  • Check it more often during hot months and after windy days
Keep return vents clear. Do not block returns with furniture, baskets, or stacked boxes. Many Ramona homes use a central return that needs clear breathing room. Keep supply vents open and unobstructed. Closing too many vents can change how air moves through the system. If a room is too warm, it is better to investigate airflow than to choke the system.

Reduce indoor dust sources

  • Use entry mats and take shoes off when you can
  • Vacuum with a good filter, especially if you have pets
  • If you do projects like sanding or cutting, isolate the area and clean up dust quickly
