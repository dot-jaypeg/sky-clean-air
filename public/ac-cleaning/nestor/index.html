AC cleaning vs changing the air filter

Changing the filter is basic upkeep. It catches a lot, but it does not remove what is already stuck to the inside surfaces.

  • Filter changes help going forward
  • AC cleaning deals with what has already collected in vents and duct paths
  • Filters can be installed wrong, undersized, or left too long
  • Even good filters cannot catch dust that enters through gaps, returns, or leaky areas

Think of it like sweeping your entryway. A doormat helps, but you still have to clean the floor sometimes. For homeowners comparing options, Wikipedia has a useful overview of heating ventilation and air conditioning that explains how airflow moves through a building.

How to keep vents and ducts cleaner after service

You do not need a complicated routine. A few simple habits help a lot. If you are building a routine, AC Maintenance can support long term comfort between deeper cleanings.

Do this

  • Check your filter monthly until you learn how fast it loads in your home
  • Keep supply vents open and unblocked for better airflow
  • Vacuum return grilles carefully with a brush attachment
  • After home projects, change the filter sooner than usual
  • If you store items near the air handler closet, keep a clear space for airflow

Try not to do this

  • Running the system with no filter even for a short time
  • Stuffing furniture directly in front of a return
  • Using a filter that bends or gaps around the frame
