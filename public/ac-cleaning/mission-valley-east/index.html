What counts as AC cleaning and what parts get attention

Most people picture the vents, but AC Cleaning can touch several parts of the system that affect airflow and comfort. What we clean depends on what you have, how accessible it is, and what condition it is in.

Common areas we address during AC Cleaning in Mission Valley East include

  • Registers and grilles that catch everyday dust

How AC cleaning relates to filters

Filters are your first line of defense, but they are not a full cleaning plan. They catch a lot, yet dust can still build up in vents and in parts of the system that see airflow and static.

A few filter tips we share often

  • Use the filter size that fits snugly with no gaps
  • Replace on a schedule that matches pets, traffic, and how often the system runs
  • If your filter gets gray fast, something is feeding extra dust into the return path

If you show us the filter location, we can point out common issues like a bent filter rack, air bypass, or a return grille that is pulling dust from an unsealed cavity.
