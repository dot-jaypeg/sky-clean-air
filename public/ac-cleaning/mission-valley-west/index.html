Will AC cleaning help with allergies or odors

Cleaning can reduce the dust and debris that gets stirred up when air moves. That can be helpful if you are sensitive to airborne particles. Odors can also improve if they are tied to dust, moisture residue, or buildup near the drain area.

A few common odor sources we run into

  • Dust sitting on surfaces that get damp from condensation nearby
  • Organic buildup near the drain pan area
  • Musty smells from a system that has been off for a long stretch
  • Pet dander and hair caught around returns

If the odor comes from something else, like a plumbing issue, carpet moisture, or a wall cavity, AC cleaning may not change it. We will tell you what we see and what seems likely.

The difference between AC cleaning and changing the filter

Changing the filter is a must. It is like taking out the trash. Helpful, but it does not scrub the can. If you suspect air is bypassing the filter because of duct gaps, you may also want to review Air Duct Repair in Mission Valley West, CA.

A filter mainly does two things

  • Captures some particles as air passes through
  • Helps protect equipment from larger debris

What a filter does not do well

  • Remove buildup that already collected on surfaces
  • Fix air bypass around a loose filter slot
  • Clean vent grilles that are visibly dusty
  • Address buildup around the blower area or drain area

If you have been changing filters and still feel dust is winning, you are not crazy. It often means dust is collecting somewhere the filter cannot reach, or air is sneaking around the filter.
