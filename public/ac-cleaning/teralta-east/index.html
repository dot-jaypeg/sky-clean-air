What counts as AC cleaning and what does not

AC cleaning gets used for a lot of different things. In real life, there are a few common areas that matter most for airflow and comfort. If your concerns point more toward the duct system, you may also want to compare with Air Duct Cleaning in Teralta East, CA.

AC cleaning commonly includes

  • Supply vents and return vents cleaning at the room openings
  • Registers and grilles washing and wipe down
  • Dust and debris removal in accessible air pathways
  • Cleaning accessible blower compartment areas when safe and appropriate
  • Coil cleaning when the coil is accessible and the situation calls for it
  • A general cleanup so you are not left with dust trails on the floor

AC cleaning does not replace

  • Refrigerant work
  • Electrical repairs
  • Fixing a frozen coil caused by mechanical or airflow faults
  • Replacing a failing motor or capacitor
  • Correcting a bad duct design that never delivered good airflow
