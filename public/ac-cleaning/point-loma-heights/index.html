What can I do between cleanings

Small habits keep systems cleaner longer. No fancy tools required.

  • Use the correct filter size and make sure it seals in the slot
  • Check the return grille for lint buildup and vacuum it gently
  • Keep supply vents open, even in rooms you do not use much
  • Do not block returns with furniture or heavy curtains
  • After a remodel, replace filters more frequently for a while
