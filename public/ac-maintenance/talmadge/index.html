Common signs your AC needs maintenance

If you are noticing any of the items below, it is usually time to schedule maintenance rather than waiting for a full failure.

  • Cooling feels slower than last season
  • Airflow from vents is weaker in several rooms
  • The system turns on and off more than usual
  • You smell musty odors when the AC starts
  • You hear new rattles, buzzing, or humming
  • The outdoor unit seems louder than normal
  • You see water near the indoor unit or a damp spot by the closet
  • Your filter is dirty again after only a couple of weeks

A short bit of real life dialogue we hear in Talmadge. You think it is the thermostat. Maybe, or it might be the filter, the drain, or airflow. We check the basics first because chasing guesses is a wild goose chase.

Will maintenance help with weak airflow

Often yes, but it depends on the cause. Weak airflow can come from a dirty filter or incorrect filter size, a blower that is dirty or struggling, a coil that is restricted, closed or blocked registers, duct leaks or crushed duct sections in attic spaces, or undersized returns for the amount of airflow your system needs.

Odors and musty smells

Musty smells can come from moisture and dust interacting around the coil area, drain pan, or ductwork. Maintenance focuses on spotting the usual suspects. If the smell is strongest right when the AC starts, that is a helpful clue. If it smells all day, that points to a different mix of causes.

  • Standing water or slow drainage at the condensate pan
  • Dirty filter allowing buildup on internal surfaces
  • Poor airflow causing the coil area to stay damp longer
  • Duct issues that hold dust and moisture

What to do before we arrive

A little prep goes a long way. Make sure we can reach the thermostat and indoor unit, clear a small workspace around the indoor closet or attic access if applicable, unlock side gates if the outdoor unit is behind a fence, and note any patterns such as it struggles after 3 pm or only the front rooms feel warm.

If you know your filter size, set a new one aside. If you are not sure where your filter is, do not worry. Many Talmadge homes have returns in hallways, living rooms, or near bedrooms. We can help you locate it.

Filter guidance

Filter choice depends on your system, your household, and airflow needs. A filter that is too restrictive can reduce airflow. A filter that is too light can let more dust through. We can also show you how to check if the filter is bowing, whistling, or getting pulled into the slot.

Home situation Typical filter approach What to watch
No pets, light dust Standard pleated filter Replace on a steady schedule
Pets or heavier dust Pleated filter with consistent changes Avoid going too restrictive if airflow is already weak
Allergies or smoke sensitivity Higher filtration may help Watch for reduced airflow and longer run times

Here are patterns we hear near neighborhood streets, small apartment buildings, and single family homes around Talmadge.
