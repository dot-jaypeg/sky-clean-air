What about strange smells when the AC turns on

Odors have a way of getting everyone’s attention. The trick is figuring out which kind of smell it is.

Common odor types and what we look at during maintenance.

  • Musty smell at startup often tied to moisture at the indoor coil, drain pan, or nearby insulation
  • Dusty or old house smell can be dirty coils, dirty filters, or dust in the return path
  • Sour smell near the indoor unit sometimes points to drain issues or standing water
  • Outdoor smell pulled inside can happen if the system is pulling air from a leaky return or attic area

We check for moisture where it should not be, confirm the drain is moving water correctly, and look for buildup on coil surfaces. If the odor seems tied to duct conditions, we will say so clearly and explain options.

Does AC maintenance reduce breakdowns

During maintenance we look closely at.

No one can predict the exact day a part will fail, but maintenance gives you better visibility. It is like noticing your car tires are wearing unevenly before you are on the side of the road.

What can I do between maintenance visits

A little bit of attention goes a long way. You do not need to become an HVAC hobbyist.

Here are simple, homeowner friendly steps.

  • Check the filter monthly during heavy use
  • Keep at least two feet of space around the outdoor unit
  • Rinse light dust from the outdoor cabinet area when the unit is off
  • Make sure supply vents are open and not blocked by furniture
  • Keep interior doors cracked if a room has weak return airflow
  • Watch for water around the indoor unit area
