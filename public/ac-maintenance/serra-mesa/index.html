Why filters matter so much

Filters are the easiest maintenance item to overlook and the fastest way to make an AC feel weak. When a filter loads up, the system has to work harder to move air, and some systems will ice up or start short cycling. What you can do between maintenance visits

You do not need a tool belt to help your AC. A few simple habits go a long way.

Monthly or every other month

  • Check the filter and replace as needed
  • Walk around the outdoor unit and clear leaves and clutter
  • Make sure supply registers are open and not blocked

Seasonally

  • Pour a small amount of water into the condensate drain access point if you have one and it is safe to do so
  • Listen for new sounds at startup and shutdown
  • Confirm the thermostat schedule still matches your routine

If you notice ice on the refrigerant line or the indoor unit area, turn the system off and let it thaw, then schedule service using Contact Us. Running it iced up can lead to bigger problems.
