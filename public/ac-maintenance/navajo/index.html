Signs your AC needs maintenance now

Sometimes the system tells you plainly. Sometimes it whispers.

  • Airflow feels weak from certain vents
  • Rooms cool unevenly like the bedroom is fine but the living room sweats
  • AC turns on and off more often than usual
  • Musty or dusty smell when it starts
  • Water near the indoor unit or a clogged drain line
  • Outdoor unit sounds louder than normal
  • Energy use creeps up without a clear reason

A small Navajo style example is coming back from hiking at Mission Trails Regional Park, lowering the thermostat, and the AC runs but the house feels sticky. That is often airflow or coil related, and a maintenance visit can pinpoint the cause.

What you can do between maintenance visits

A little attention goes a long way. You do not need a huge toolbox, just a few simple habits.

  • Check the filter monthly during heavy use
  • Keep at least a couple feet of clear space around the outdoor unit
  • Rinse light debris off the exterior area around the condenser, not inside it
  • Make sure supply vents are open and not blocked by rugs or furniture
  • Listen for new noises and note when they happen
  • If you see water at the indoor unit, turn the system off and get it checked

Quick filter tip is hold a filter up to a light and if it looks like a dark curtain it is time. If it looks like it could star in a dust bunny documentary it is definitely time.

AC maintenance vs repairs

Maintenance is routine care and checking. Repairs are what happens when a part fails or a condition needs correction beyond normal service steps. A simple way to think about it is maintenance is cleaning checking and testing, while repair is replacing or fixing a failed or damaged component.

During maintenance, we can spot issues that may need repair attention. We will explain what we found in plain language. No scare talk. Just the facts and what it means for comfort.
