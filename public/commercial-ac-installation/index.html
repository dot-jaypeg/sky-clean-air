What We Install and Service

Every building has limits and opportunities. We match the system type to the layout, use, and budget.

Rooftop Packaged Units

Rooftop units are popular across San Diego for low rise offices, retail plazas, and warehouses. They keep mechanical equipment off the floor and out of sight. We replace older rooftop units with high efficiency models that use less power and run quieter.

  • Careful crane planning for tight streets in areas like Gaslamp, Hillcrest, and North Park
  • New curb or curb adapter to fit your roof opening and duct connections
  • Economizer set up for cool coastal mornings to bring in free cooling when outside air is just right
  • Demand control ventilation for spaces with changing occupancy

If your rooftop is near the coast, salt air can shorten equipment life. We use coil coatings and corrosion resistant parts to help your system hold up better.

Split Systems

Split systems are a smart fit for suites that need separate controls or for buildings with limited rooftop space. We install and replace:

  • Heat pump and straight cool split systems
  • Air handlers with efficient ECM blowers
  • Condensing units with high IEER ratings
  • Zoning options to reduce hot and cold spots

Have tenants with different schedules or loads? We can set up separate thermostats and schedules so you do not cool empty rooms.

Ductless Mini Split and VRF

Ductless mini split and VRF systems shine in server rooms, labs, dental suites, and offices where ducts are hard to add. They offer precise zoning and quiet operation.

  • Wall, floor, or ceiling cassettes for flexible placement
  • Heat recovery options for buildings with mixed needs
  • Great for retrofits in older buildings that need an efficient solution without major construction

Learn more about variable refrigerant flow systems for complex spaces.

Make Up Air and Ventilation

Comfort is not only about temperature. Fresh air is critical for restaurants, gyms, beauty salons, and classrooms. We set up make up air, energy recovery ventilation, and controls that meet code and keep indoor air fresh.

  • Balanced airflow for kitchens and nail salons
  • CO2 based ventilation control to cut energy use when rooms are empty
  • Filtration upgrades for better indoor air quality

Controls and Thermostats

From simple programmable thermostats to smart building controls, we help you pick the level you need.

  • App based control for multi site management with commercial control setup
  • Lockout features to prevent thermostat wars
  • Scheduling and alerts that help you fix small issues before they become big ones with thermostat programming and thermostat installation

Our Simple Process

You are busy. You want a plan that makes sense without slowing down operations. Here is how we work from first call to first cool breeze.

1. Conversation and Site Visit

We start with a quick call to learn your goals, problem areas, and timing. Then we visit your site to measure, inspect the roof and ducts, and note power and access. Do you need weekend or early morning work to avoid traffic or tenant disruption? We can plan for that. You can also request a commercial HVAC inspection.

2. Load Calculations and Options

Right sizing matters. Too small and the system struggles. Too large and it short cycles, wastes energy, and wears out faster. We run load calculations that factor in square footage, people, lighting, equipment, glass exposure, and San Diego weather patterns. Then we show you options that fit your space and your budget.

  • Good, better, best options with clear pros and cons
  • Payback estimates for higher efficiency gear
  • Upgrade paths if you plan to expand later

3. Clear Proposal and Timeline

You get a written proposal with scope, equipment, controls, any duct changes, crane plan, permits, and a targeted schedule. No surprises. Need a second bid for your landlord or property manager? We can share drawings and specs in a clean format.

4. Permits and Plan Check

We handle mechanical permits, Title 24 forms, and inspection scheduling through the City of San Diego or your local city. If your building falls in a coastal zone, we account for that in the plan. You do not have to stand in line at Development Services. We do it for you.

5. Crane Day and Installation

Crane day sounds intense, and it can be. We make it smooth. We coordinate with the crane company, building management, and neighbors. We post no parking signs if needed and watch the weather forecast. The crew protects the roof, removes the old unit, sets the new one, connects power and ducts, and checks the charge and airflow.

6. Start Up and Commissioning

We test refrigerant charge, confirm airflow, set up the economizer, calibrate thermostats, and verify ventilation rates. If your building has multiple zones, we balance the system so suites feel consistent. We keep a log of static pressure, temperature split, and amperage so you have a baseline. Ask about duct balancing during startup.

7. Training and Ongoing Care

We show your team how to use the controls, change filters, and spot warning signs. We can set reminders and remote alerts. Want us to keep it humming? Ask about a commercial AC maintenance plan that fits your hours and usage.

Energy Smart Choices That Work in San Diego

Cooling needs vary from Pacific Beach to El Cajon. Ocean breeze can help in the morning, then dry heat or Santa Ana wind shows up and loads shift. We plan for those swings with equipment and settings that save energy without sacrificing comfort.

  • High IEER rooftop units that shine in part load conditions
  • Variable speed fans that adapt to zones opening and closing
  • Economizers that bring in cool outside air on mornings and nights
  • CO2 sensors for ventilation only when rooms are occupied with demand controlled ventilation
  • Zoning and thermostats that prevent overcooling empty areas
  • Filtration upgrades for wildfire smoke days, without choking airflow

Have you ever cooled a conference room for hours only to use it twice a week? Zoning and smart schedules fix that. We can show you real numbers so you can see the savings path.

Rooftop Logistics and Safety, Done Right

San Diego roofs come in all shapes. Tilt up warehouses in Otay Mesa, stucco retail centers in Clairemont, older flat roofs in North Park, and concrete decks Downtown. Each one needs a plan.

  • Roof survey for pathways, parapet height, and unit access
  • Curb adapter to fit the new unit to the old opening where practical
  • Proper rigging points and a lift plan with the crane operator
  • Ballasted or anchored equipment per code and manufacturer
  • Sealing and flashing to prevent leaks
  • Trash removal and clean up before we leave

We schedule work early in the day to avoid traffic and heat, and we coordinate with property managers so tenants can plan. Need Saturday work to keep retail sales moving? We can make that happen.

AC For Different Commercial Spaces

San Diego has a wide mix of businesses. Each space asks for something a little different.

Offices and Co-Working

Comfort helps productivity. We zone meeting rooms, open areas, and private offices separately so you are not cooling empty rooms. Quiet operation matters on phone calls, so we select equipment and diffusers that keep noise down.

Retail and Restaurants

Front doors open all day and kitchens add heat. We provide vestibule air curtains, make up air for hoods, and units with faster recovery. If you have a patio that opens to the dining room, we plan for wind and door swings that can throw balance off.

Gyms and Studios

Bodies in motion change the load fast. Ventilation and humidity control are key to comfort. We set up CO2 control and extra filtration to keep air fresh and smells under control.

Warehouses and Light Industrial

Large spaces do not need even cooling everywhere. Spot cooling for workstations, destratification fans, and high bay diffusers improve comfort without waste. Rooftop units with high static fans and smart duct runs reach the floor better.

Medical and Dental

Temperature swing control, filtration, and quiet operation are important. We plan supply placement to reduce drafts on patients and keep exam rooms steady. For dental suites, we consider suction equipment loads that add heat.

Server Rooms and Labs

Constant, reliable cooling is the goal. Ductless mini split or VRF units keep temps steady, with backup where needed. We set alerts for temperature spikes so you know before equipment is at risk.

How We Think About Sizing and Equipment Selection

Right sizing is more than matching the old tonnage. Buildings change. Tenants move walls. Lighting upgrades reduce heat. Computer loads go up. We measure, ask questions, and pick equipment that fits today and tomorrow.

  • Load calculations using building size, glass, insulation, people count, and plug loads
  • Part load efficiency focus since AC runs most of the time at less than full capacity
  • Fan static calculations to make sure ducts and diffusers get the airflow they need
  • Control strategy that matches your operations and budget with commercial control setup and zoning

Do you plan to add staff this year? Are you converting storage into workstations? Tell us now and we will keep expansion in mind.

Signs You May Need Replacement Instead of Another Repair

It can be tough to decide between repair and replacement. These clues help.

  • Frequent service calls in the last 12 months
  • Refrigerant type is outdated or costly
  • Energy bills rising with no change in usage
  • Uneven temps even after service
  • Visible coil corrosion from salt air
  • Short cycling or poor humidity control

We compare repair costs against the value of a new unit with lower energy use and better reliability. If a repair makes sense, we say so. If replacement is the smarter move, we show you why with clear numbers. Explore commercial AC repair or consider commercial AC replacement when needed.
