What dryer vent cleaning includes

Dryer vents are not just the short flex hose behind the dryer. In many Carmel Mountain Ranch homes, the vent continues through a wall, attic, crawl space, or garage ceiling before it exits outside.

  • Checking the dryer connection and vent material
  • Inspecting the vent run for airflow restrictions
  • Removing lint buildup from the vent line
  • Clearing the exterior vent hood including stuck lint at the flap
  • Making sure the connection is secure when everything is put back

If you have a second story laundry room or a longer vent route, we plan for access and take care around finished spaces. No one wants scuffed baseboards because it was just a vent.

How to tell if your Carmel Mountain Ranch dryer vent is clogged

Most people call after they notice a pattern. It starts small and then one day you are running a third cycle and wondering why a towel is still damp.

  • Clothes take longer to dry than they used to
  • The dryer feels hotter than normal on the outside
  • The laundry room feels warm or humid during a cycle
  • A musty or dusty smell shows up when the dryer runs
  • Lint is collecting around the dryer or at the wall connection
  • The outside vent flap does not open much when the dryer is on

Why lint builds up fast

Lint is stubborn. Even with a clean lint screen, small fibers slip through and settle inside the vent line. The buildup rate depends on laundry habits and the vent layout.

  • Long vent runs especially with multiple turns
  • Crushed or kinked flex duct behind the dryer
  • Heavy laundry loads like comforters, dog beds, and bath towels
  • Older vent routes that were not installed with smooth metal ducting
  • Vent hoods that trap lint or do not open freely

If you have pets, you already know the deal. Pet hair seems to teleport into every corner of the house, and dryer vents are no exception.

Fire safety and airflow

Fire safety is a big reason people schedule dryer vent cleaning. Lint is very flammable, and a restricted vent can increase heat and stress on the dryer. For background on lint related dryer fires, you can review https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dryer_vent.

We keep the conversation grounded and practical. Cleaning helps remove fuel from the vent line and supports better airflow. If we see a vent route that is crushed, disconnected, or dumping lint into a wall cavity, we will point it out so you can decide the next step. Between visit habits that help

Cleaning the lint screen is a must, but it only catches part of the lint. The rest moves into the ductwork.

  • Clean the lint screen every load
  • Check behind the dryer every so often for lint or a crushed hose
  • Keep the area around the dryer clear for airflow
  • Take a quick look at the exterior vent flap when the dryer is running
