Will Dryer Vent Cleaning make my dryer work better

A clogged vent can force a dryer to push air through a restricted line. That can show up as longer cycles, hotter operation, and lint escaping into places it should not be. Can a clogged dryer vent be a safety issue

Lint is combustible. A vent that cannot exhaust properly can lead to excess heat and lint accumulation where it should not be. That combination is a reason many people in Alta Vista schedule cleaning even before the dryer starts acting up. For general background on lint and fire risk, see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dryer_vent.

A few safety minded checks you can do

  • Feel for strong airflow outside when the dryer runs
  • Watch for the exterior flap opening freely
  • Notice if the dryer area is getting unusually hot
  • Pay attention to burning smells and stop the dryer if you notice them

If you ever see scorching, melting, or electrical issues, that is not a vent cleaning problem. That is a stop and call an appliance professional situation.
