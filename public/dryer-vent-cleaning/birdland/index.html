How often dryer vents should be cleaned

What you can do between professional cleanings

A few habits help vents stay clearer longer. None of them require fancy tools.

  • Clean the lint screen every load
  • Wash the lint screen occasionally with mild soap and water if you use dryer sheets
  • Do not overload the dryer
  • Listen for changes in sound and cycle length
  • Check the outside vent hood now and then while the dryer runs

If you want a quick test, run the dryer on a timed heat cycle and go outside. You should feel a steady push of warm air at the vent outlet. If it is weak, that is a sign to investigate.
