Signs your commercial HVAC system needs repair

Not sure if you need a repair or just a tune up? Here are signs that point to trouble. Do any of these sound familiar?

  • Uneven temperatures Some rooms feel like a fridge and others feel like a sauna. This often means airflow or control issues.
  • Rising energy bills If usage looks the same but costs climb your system may be working harder than it should.
  • Short cycling The unit starts and stops every few minutes. That wastes energy and wears parts out fast.
  • Strange noises Rattles grinding or loud humming are clues that a fan belt or motor is in trouble.
  • Poor airflow Weak or no air from vents often points to a clogged filter bad blower collapsed duct or damper problem. Airflow balancing can help restore comfort.
  • Odors Musty smells hint at dirty coils or drain issues. A burnt smell can be electrical. Gas odors are an emergency.
  • Thermostat misses the mark The display says 72 but the space feels like 80. That can be a control or sensor problem.
  • Leaks or water near the unit Condensate drain clogs and cracked drain pans can cause water damage and mold growth.
  • Frequent breaker trips This can mean electrical faults or motors drawing too much current.
  • Humidity swings Sticky air even with the AC running can mean coil or airflow problems.

If you see one of these call Sky Clean Air at 619-304-8822. A quick fix now can prevent a major breakdown later.

A closer look at common repairs we handle

Let’s dig a bit deeper into issues we see often in San Diego. Spot one of these at your building? Give us a call.

  • Failed capacitors A small part that starts motors. Heat and age take them out. Easy to test and replace.
  • Worn belts Squeal or no airflow can be a loose or broken belt. We replace and set proper tension.
  • Dirty coils High head pressure and poor cooling often come from dirty condenser or evaporator coils. Cleaning restores performance.
  • Refrigerant leaks Oil stains on tubing or low pressures point to a leak. We find the leak repair it and recharge to the correct level.
  • Faulty contactors or relays If the unit will not start or will not stop controls may be the cause. We test and replace as needed.
  • Economizer failure Stuck dampers waste energy and cause stuffy rooms. We repair actuators and recalibrate dampers.
  • Thermostat and sensor drift If rooms do not match the setpoint sensors may need calibration or replacement.
  • Clogged drains Water dripping from ceiling tiles or pans overflowing is a sure sign of a blocked condensate line. We clear the line and treat the pan.

We always test after a fix to confirm the system is working as it should.
