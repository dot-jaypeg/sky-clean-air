Need Air Duct Cleaning in Broadway Heights, CA

If you are hearing pops from the vents, noticing more dust on surfaces, or smelling stale air after you switch on the AC, it might be time. Air Duct Cleaning in Broadway Heights, CA keeps your system moving air the way it should. Clean ducts help improve airflow, reduce buildup on your AC coil, and keep dust from cycling through rooms.

Think about what flows through your ducts every day. Dust from the freeway, pet hair, pollen from canyon breezes, construction debris from remodels, and fine particles from wildfire smoke days can all settle inside. Once it sticks to the duct walls, it tends to stay there until someone removes it properly.

We use professional equipment and proven methods. No gimmicks. No quick 15 minute blow and go jobs. Just solid work that actually removes the gunk.

What You Get With Our Air Duct Cleaning Service

Curious how the appointment goes? Here is our standard process for homes and small offices in Broadway Heights.

  • Inspection and photos
    • We check supply and return ducts, vents, and the air handler.
    • We show you what we see before any work starts.
  • Set up and protection
    • Drop cloths under each vent and by the air handler.
    • Corner guards and shoe covers to keep floors and walls clean.
  • Negative pressure cleaning
    • We connect a powerful vacuum with HEPA filtration to your system.
    • This pulls debris to our machine so it does not blow back into your rooms.
  • Agitation and brushing
    • Rotating brushes and air whips break loose packed dust inside the ducts.
    • We clean each main trunk and branch line one by one.
  • Return side and blower area
    • We clean the return plenum where dust often piles up fast.
    • We wipe and vacuum accessible blower and cabinet areas.
  • Vent registers and grills
    • Wash and wipe grills, then reinstall.
  • Sanitizing on request
    • We can fog a safe duct disinfectant to reduce odors and microbes after cleaning.
  • Final check and photos
    • We confirm airflow at vents and show you before and after images.

You get a clean system, less airborne dust, and a fresher smell when the AC or heat kicks on. Have a specific allergy or concern? Tell us before we start and we will plan the cleaning to address it. Ready to schedule? Call 619-304-8822.

      The Value Of Professional Cleaning And Maintenance

      Why not just replace filters and call it done? Filters matter, but they do not remove the dust that already sits inside your ductwork. A clogged coil or a filthy return plenum can cancel out a good filter. Proper cleaning

      • Improves airflow across the coil and heat exchanger
      • Helps your system reach set temps faster
      • Reduces recirculating dust and odors
      • Lowers strain on the blower motor

      Combine cleaning with sealing and balancing and your system runs smoother. That often means fewer breakdowns and more consistent comfort.
