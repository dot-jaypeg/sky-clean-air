Our Step by Step Duct Cleaning Process

We keep things simple and clear.

  • Inspection and airflow check We look at vents, registers, and accessible trunk lines. We check static pressure and airflow so we know where dust and buildup are worst.
  • Set up and protect We place drop cloths, corner guards, and coverings to protect floors and furniture. We isolate the duct system so dust does not escape into your rooms.
  • Powerful vacuum and brush cleaning We connect a high power vacuum with HEPA filtration to pull dust and debris out. We use rotary brushes and air whips to loosen stuck on dirt, pet hair, popcorn ceiling dust, and sand so the vacuum can capture it.
  • Clean supply and return lines, registers, and grilles We remove and clean each register and grille and clean every reachable branch line. We also take care of the return, which is often the dustiest path.
  • Optional duct sanitizing If odors or microbes are a concern, we offer EPA registered duct sanitizing products applied safely inside the ductwork after cleaning. Ask if this makes sense for your home.
  • Final review, photos, and airflow test We show you what came out and confirm airflow and static pressure are back in range. You get clear before and after results, not guesswork.

Maintenance Tips For Coastal Homes

Want to keep your system clean longer between services?

  • Use quality filters and change them on time Coastal homes can clog filters faster. We can recommend the right size and MERV level for your system.
  • Keep supply and return vents clear Move furniture that blocks vents and vacuum grilles every month.
  • Rinse outdoor units gently A light rinse removes salt film from coils. Make sure power is off first.
  • Check dryer vent termination Make sure the flap opens freely and there is no lint buildup outside.
  • Schedule seasonal tune ups A spring AC check and a fall heating check help catch issues early.

These small steps help keep ducts cleaner and energy bills steadier.
