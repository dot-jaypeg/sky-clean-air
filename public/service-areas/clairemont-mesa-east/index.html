Air Duct Cleaning in Clairemont Mesa East, CA by Sky Clean Air

Our approach is simple. We clean the whole system, not just what you can see. That means the supply ducts, return ducts, trunk lines, registers, and the indoor unit areas that collect dust.

Here is how a typical visit goes

  • 1. Conversation and walkthrough. We listen to your concerns. Hot rooms, odors near the hallway return, dust on furniture, pets, recent remodeling. These details guide our plan.
  • 2. System inspection. We open a few vents to check buildup. We look at the return drop, plenum, blower compartment, and filter slot. If needed, we use a camera to see deeper.
  • 3. Protection and setup. We cover floors and furniture near work areas. We seal off vents as we section the system. We set a large negative air machine with HEPA filtration to keep dust out of your living space.
  • 4. Source removal cleaning. We use high power vacuums and rotating brushes or air tools to dislodge dust, pet hair, lint, and light debris from duct surfaces. The machine pulls this out safely.
  • 5. Return and supply cleaning. Both sides matter. We clean the return path that feeds the system, and the supply lines that deliver air to rooms. Skipping either side limits results.
  • 6. Air handler and coil area cleaning. Dust around the blower and coil can block airflow. We clean these areas with care so parts stay in good shape.
  • 7. Optional duct sanitizing. On request, we apply an EPA registered product inside ducts to reduce common bacteria and odors. We never fog anything strong while people are in the room.
  • 8. Final test and cleanup. We recheck airflow, replace vent covers, and remove gear. We can show before and after views where possible.
  • 9. Filter check and advice. We fit a clean filter and note the correct size and rating for your system. We show you how often to change it based on pets and use.

Problems we solve every week in Clairemont Mesa East

Here are real world issues we see nearby, along with how we fix them.

  • Dust on every surface a day after cleaning. We find a big leak at the return plenum and gaps at several boots. We seal connections, clean the ducts, and install a better filter. Result is less dust and a quieter system.
  • Rooms near the 52 are always warmer. We find crushed flex duct sections and weak airflow to those runs. We replace sections, balance dampers, and get those rooms in line.
