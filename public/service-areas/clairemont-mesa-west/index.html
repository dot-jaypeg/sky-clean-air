How our air duct cleaning process works

Curious what happens on the day of service? Here is our step by step approach that keeps dust under control and your home tidy.

  • Conversation and scheduling. We listen to your concerns, ask about allergies, pets, remodels, and areas with weak airflow, then schedule a time that fits.
  • On site inspection. We check supply and return vents, look at your furnace or air handler, peek into ducts with a camera where needed, and confirm access to attic or crawl areas. You get a written estimate before we start.
  • Home protection. We lay down floor covers, corner guards, and move light furniture near vents. Registers are covered to maintain negative pressure.
  • Set up negative air and HEPA filtration. A powerful vacuum connects to the duct system to pull debris out, while specialized tools agitate buildup without sending dust into rooms.
  • Source removal cleaning. We clean each trunk line and branch, vent by vent, and brush the return air side. We remove and wash registers, then reinstall them.
  • Optional disinfecting. We can apply an EPA registered disinfectant fog to the duct interior to reduce bacteria and odors. This is helpful after water damage, smoke, or heavy pet activity.
  • Duct sealing and balancing options. If we find leaks or crushed lines, we document them and offer fixes. Proper sealing and balancing improve airflow.
  • Final check and photos. We show you before and after pictures, clean the work area, and review maintenance tips specific to your home.
