The Sky Clean Air process for Air Duct Cleaning in El Cerrito, CA

Clean ducts move more air with less effort. That means better comfort and a system that does not have to work as hard. Here is how we handle Air Duct Cleaning in El Cerrito, CA for homes and businesses.

Step 1 Inspection and photos

We begin with a walkthrough. We locate all registers, returns, and the air handler. We take photos so you can see the starting point. We will plan around any schedule.

Step 2 Set up and protect the space

We lay down drop cloths, wear shoe covers, and protect furnishings. Apartments, storefronts on El Cajon Blvd, and offices near the Horace Mann Middle School all get the same careful setup. We also isolate each zone to control dust during cleaning.

Step 3 Negative pressure and source removal

We attach a high powered vacuum to your system to pull dust and debris out of the ducts. Then we use rotary brushes and air whips to dislodge buildup from the duct walls. The vacuum captures everything.

Step 4 Registers, grills, and blower cleaning

We clean supply and return grills, wipe them down, and clean the blower compartment. If accessible and needed, we clean the evaporator coil and drain pan area to help airflow and system efficiency.

Step 5 Optional duct sanitizing

If odors or microbial growth have been a problem, we can apply an EPA registered sanitizer to the interior of metal or approved duct materials. This helps reduce odor causing bacteria and mild growth on contact surfaces.

Step 6 Final photos and walkthrough

We show you before and after photos, test airflow, and leave your space as tidy as we found it. No mess, no surprises.

Signs your ducts need cleaning

  • Dust puffs from registers when the system starts
  • Black lines on carpet near baseboards
  • Worsening allergies or musty smells
  • Recent remodeling that created dust
  • You cannot remember the last cleaning, or it has been 3 to 5 years
  • You see lint and dust around returns, especially near high traffic areas

How long does it take

  • Condos and smaller homes 2 to 4 hours
  • Larger homes in the hills half day to full day
  • Restaurants or offices varies by size and number of zones
