How we clean ducts so they stay clean longer

A proper cleaning goes beyond vacuuming a few vents. Here is our approach.

  • Whole system mindset
    • We treat the duct system, not just the vent covers. That includes supply and return runs, trunks, and plenums.
  • Source removal
    • The negative air setup pulls debris out of ducts while we agitate it with brushes and air whips. The dust goes straight to a sealed HEPA filter.
  • Protection
    • Vents are covered before agitation so nothing blows into rooms.
  • Detail cleaning
    • Registers are washed and dried, not just wiped.
    • We clean the blower compartment and inspect the evaporator coil area.
  • Disinfecting on request
    • We apply a safe disinfecting step to reduce odors and growth after cleaning. This helps in damp areas near Loma Alta Creek or homes with past leaks.
  • Follow through
    • Airflow readings or a simple static pressure check help confirm improvement.
    • If leaks or crushed ducts are found, we show you options.

Want to keep your ducts clean longer? Use good filters and change them on schedule, and keep supply vents open and clear of furniture. Small steps add up.
