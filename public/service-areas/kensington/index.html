How We Clean Your Ducts

Here is our step by step approach.

  • Walkthrough and setup. We inspect the system, protect floors, and plan access points.
  • Create safe negative pressure. A powerful vacuum unit attaches to the trunk line to pull dust into a sealed HEPA filtered system.
  • Brush and vacuum each run. We move through every supply and return line to loosen and capture debris.
  • Clean the air handler area. When accessible, we clean the blower cabinet interior surfaces.
  • Grille and register cleaning. Each grille gets washed and wiped down.
  • Seal, test, and verify. We reattach access points and test airflow.
  • Optional sanitizing. We apply safe antimicrobial treatments for odor control when requested.
