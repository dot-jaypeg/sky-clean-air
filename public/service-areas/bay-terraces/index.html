What You Get With Sky Clean Air

We keep the process straightforward and respectful of your home or business.

  • Inspection and photos. We check supply and return ducts, registers, and the air handler. If needed, we use a duct camera. You see what we see.
  • Containment and protection. We cover supply vents, protect work areas, and set up negative pressure to keep debris out of your rooms.
  • Source removal. We use high-power HEPA vacuums and rotary tools to lift and extract debris from the entire run of ductwork.
  • Sanitizing and options. We can sanitize the ducts after cleaning. If there are leaks, damage, or poor connections, we offer duct sealing or repair.
  • Final testing and cleanup. We measure airflow and static pressure, reinstall registers, and tidy up. No mess left behind.

The result is cleaner air delivery, better airflow balance, and a system that does not have to fight against dust buildup.

How We Clean Ducts Step by Step

Curious how the cleaning actually works. Here is the simple version.

  • 1 Check in and walkthrough. We discuss your concerns, note hot or cold rooms, pet issues, or recent remodeling.
  • 2 System inspection. We inspect the air handler, coils, blower, filter housing, and duct connections. If needed, we use a camera in the ducts.
  • 3 Protect your space. Drop cloths and vent covers go up. The system is placed under negative pressure with a HEPA vacuum.
  • 4 Agitation and removal. Special tools brush and sweep debris from each trunk and branch while the vacuum pulls everything into a sealed container.
  • 5 Returns and main trunk. We clean returns and the main trunk, which hold a lot of dust.
  • 6 Sanitizing option. If you have odors or past smoke exposure, we can sanitize the interior of the ducts after cleaning.
  • 7 Final checks. Registers are reinstalled, we measure airflow, and we tidy the area. You can see before and after photos for peace of mind.

The goal is real source removal, not just spraying something and hoping for the best.

Indoor Air Quality Upgrades That Make a Big Difference

Duct cleaning is one piece of the puzzle. If you want cleaner air every day, consider these add-ons.

  • Better filtration. A quality media filter traps fine particles without choking airflow.
  • UV light at the coil. Helps keep the coil cleaner, which supports airflow and reduces odors.
  • Whole-home air purifier. Captures tiny particles and can reduce common allergens.
  • Humidity control. A humidifier or dehumidifier can help comfort in dry or sticky seasons.
  • Duct sealing. Stops dusty attic air from being pulled into your supply runs.

We size and install these so they actually help. No gimmicks.

Dryer Vent Cleaning and Safety

Lint buildup restricts airflow, makes the dryer run longer, and can be a fire risk. If your dryer needs two cycles or the laundry room is warmer than the rest of the house, the vent line probably needs service.

We clean and repair vent lines, reroute bad runs, install booster fans for long paths, and add easy to clean lint traps. Many Bay Terraces homes have longer vent runs that snake through the attic. A booster fan with the right pressure switch keeps air moving and reduces dry time.

Insulation, Attic Air Sealing, and Ventilation

A lot of comfort problems start in the attic. If the insulation is thin or patchy, rooms heat up fast on summer afternoons, especially those facing south and west. If there are gaps around can lights or chases, attic air leaks into the home and your AC has to push harder.

We help by

  • Inspecting and measuring your current insulation level
  • Air sealing gaps, top plates, and penetrations before adding insulation
  • Installing blown-in, batt and roll, or spray foam where it fits best
  • Adding radiant barrier for roof heat control
  • Upgrading attic ventilation to move hot air out

This makes a noticeable difference for homes around Skyline and Lomita Village where attics soak up sun most of the day. It also helps your ducts maintain supply temperature as air moves through the attic.
