How our duct cleaning process works

    • We check registers, returns, the air handler, and visible duct runs.

    • Every register and return is addressed, including the ones over the hallway that nobody likes to reach.

    • We verify airflow, reattach registers, and clean up.

A Few Final Tips You Can Use Today

  • Check your filter
    • If you cannot see light through it, it is time to change it.
