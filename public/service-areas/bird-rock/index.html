How We Clean Your Ducts

Here is our common process for residential duct cleaning. We apply a similar approach for commercial spaces, scaled to the size of your system.

Full system check

  • Inspect supply and return ducts
  • Look at the blower, evaporator coil area, and plenum
  • Check registers and grilles for debris and bio growth

Protect your space

  • Drop cloths at work areas
  • Corner guards and shoe covers
  • Register covers to keep dust from escaping

Set up negative pressure

  • Connect a powerful vacuum with HEPA filtration
  • Create strong suction through the trunk lines

Agitate and remove debris

  • Use air whips and brushes to loosen buildup
  • Pull dust, lint, sand, and light debris into the HEPA unit

Clean vents and returns

  • Hand clean registers and grilles
  • Wipe down accessible interior surfaces

Optional add ons

  • Duct Sanitizing for odor control
  • Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting for added hygiene
  • Duct Sealing if we find leaks
  • Airflow Balancing to fix hot and cold rooms

Final check

  • Verify suction and flow
  • Confirm all areas are cleaned and sealed

You get before and after notes and practical tips to help keep things cleaner longer.

Maintenance Tips That Actually Help

  • Change or wash filters regularly
    • Every 1 to 3 months for most homes
    • Monthly if you have pets or run the system often
  • Keep return grills clear
    • Do not block with furniture or baskets
  • Vacuum registers
    • A quick pass keeps surface dust from falling inside
  • Watch your dryer vent
    • If dry times creep up, it is time for a cleaning
  • Check thermostat programs
    • Use a schedule that matches your day
    • Avoid temperature swings that make the system work harder
  • Plan seasonal tune ups
    • Spring for cooling, fall for heating
    • It keeps your system efficient and catches small issues early
