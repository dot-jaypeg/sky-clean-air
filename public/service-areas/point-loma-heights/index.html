Our Duct Cleaning Process For Coastal Homes

We keep it simple and thorough. You want clean ducts and less mess, not a lecture.

Inspection and photos

We start with a visual check of supply and return vents, the air handler, and accessible ductwork. You see what we see, so you know what you are paying for.

Containment and protection

We lay down floor covers, protect corners, and seal vents to control dust. We move light furniture if needed and put it back when we are done.

Negative pressure vacuum

We connect a high-powered vacuum to create strong negative pressure. That keeps debris from floating into your rooms.

Agitation and brushing

We use rotary brushes and air whips to loosen buildup on duct walls. This is the step that gets the gunk moving.

Supply and return cleaning

We clean each branch and the main trunk, then clean registers and grilles.

Duct sanitizing on request

If you want a fresh start after water intrusion, smoke, or pet odors, we can apply a safe sanitizer to the ducts.

Airflow check

We measure airflow and static pressure before and after. If we find leaks or imbalances, we show you and explain options.

That is Air Duct Cleaning in Point Loma Heights, CA done right. Many homes by Sunset Cliffs and Ocean Beach get sand and salt tracked in each day. A proper cleaning can make a clear difference.
