The Sky Clean Air duct cleaning process in detail

Curious about the nuts and bolts of cleaning your ducts? Here is the plain language version.

  • Step 1 We walk the home or business with you. We locate supply and return vents, check the air handler and filter area, and note any problem rooms.
  • Step 2 We protect floors and furniture, then seal off vents so the system can be placed under negative pressure.
  • Step 3 We connect our vacuum to the main trunk line, set HEPA filtration, and verify airflow on the vacuum.
  • Step 4 We agitate dust and debris with rotary brushes or air whips made for your duct type. That debris heads straight to the vacuum.
  • Step 5 We clear the blower compartment and accessible coil housing if needed. Clean components help maintain airflow.
  • Step 6 We offer sanitizing. This helps with odors and microbial growth after water events or long periods without use.
  • Step 7 We reinstall registers, check airflow at each vent, and confirm proper filter fit.
  • Step 8 We review photos with you and share any repair or sealing needs we found.

Our goal is a dust-free duct interior and better airflow through the whole system.

How to keep your ducts clean longer

  • Change filters on time. If you have pets or high traffic, check monthly and replace as needed.
  • Keep supply and return grilles clear of furniture and heavy drapes.
  • Clean registers and grilles with a vacuum brush. Avoid pushing debris into the duct.
  • Schedule regular tune-ups to keep coils and blowers clean.
  • Fix duct leaks so attic dust does not get pulled into returns.
