Our duct cleaning process

We keep it simple, clean, and thorough.

  • 1. Walkthrough and inspection

    We look at supply and return runs, check the air handler, and evaluate access points.

    If needed, we run a video scope to show buildup in the ducts.

  • 2. Set up and protect

    We cover floors and furniture near work areas.

    We seal off registers and set up a negative pressure machine with HEPA filtration.

  • 3. Agitation and vacuum

    We use rotary brushes or air whips to break debris loose.

    The HEPA vacuum pulls dust and contaminants out to our sealed collection unit.

  • 4. Grille and register cleaning

    We remove and clean supply registers and the return grille.

    We hand clean vent openings for a tidy finish.

  • 5. Optional treatments

    Duct sanitizing to reduce odors and microbial growth.

    Coil and blower cleaning to restore airflow and improve efficiency.

    Filter upgrades for better capture of fine particles.

  • 6. Final check

    We reassemble, test airflow, and review before and after results with you.

Want to see what we removed from your ducts? We can show you the collection bin. It is oddly satisfying.

How often should you schedule cleaning

  • Near the beach or boardwalk closer to every 2 to 3 years
  • After a remodel soon after construction dust settles
