How our air duct cleaning process works

Curious about what actually happens during a cleaning? Here is our step by step approach for homes and businesses.

1) Inspection and plan

  • We walk the space with you and check supply and return vents, access points, and the air handler.
  • We note duct material, condition, and any visible damage or leaks.
  • We take photos so you can see the before and after.

2) Setup and protection

  • We cover floors and furniture and seal off vents as needed.
  • We place containment and create negative pressure so dust stays inside our system, not in your rooms.

3) Source removal

  • We connect a high power vacuum at the trunk line.
  • We use rotating brushes and air whips to dislodge buildup inside the ducts.
  • We brush and clean vent registers and grilles.

4) Air handler care

  • We clean the blower compartment and accessible evaporator coil surfaces.
  • We replace or upgrade the air filter if you want.

5) Optional sanitizing and deodorizing

  • We apply safe sanitizing agents when odors or microbial growth are a concern.
  • We can install a UV air purifier or better media filter for ongoing protection.

6) Duct sealing and minor repairs

  • If we find loose joints, gaps, or torn flex, we offer to seal or repair them on the spot when feasible.

7) Final walk through

  • We show you photos of the work and confirm airflow from each vent.
  • We wrap up, clean up, and share maintenance tips.

Have pets, a newborn, or a dust sensitivity? Tell us before the job. We can adjust our process, bring hypoallergenic filter options, and schedule at a time that works best for you.
