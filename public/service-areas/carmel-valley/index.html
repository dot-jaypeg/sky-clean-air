Our duct cleaning process

  • (1) Home walkthrough and vent count. We map out supply and return vents, check the air handler, and inspect accessible ductwork, plenums, and the blower compartment. We also look for kinks, disconnected runs, or obvious leaks.
  • (2) Set up clean containment. We protect floors and furniture, then connect a powerful HEPA negative air machine to pull debris out of the system. Each vent gets sealed before agitation to keep dust from escaping.
  • (3) Agitation and debris removal. We use rotating brushes and air whips inside each duct run to loosen dust, lint, and debris while the HEPA machine collects it. Supplies and returns both get full attention.
  • (4) Vent registers and boots cleaned. Grilles, registers, and boots are cleaned and wiped. If a vent cover is rusty or broken, we can replace it on request.
  • (5) Blower, evaporator, and cabinet care. We clean the blower compartment and inspect the evaporator coil area. A coil that’s matted with dust can be cleaned or scheduled for a separate service if access is limited.
  • (6) Optional duct sanitizing or disinfecting. We can apply a safe antimicrobial treatment for ducts that had smoke, pet accidents, cooking grease, or moisture issues. Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting and Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting are available.
  • (7) Final check and photos. We show you before and after images, confirm airflow, and leave the space cleaner than we found it.

Curious how long it takes? Most Carmel Valley homes take 2 to 4 hours, depending on size and access. Larger homes off Carmel Knolls Drive or multi story townhomes near One Paseo can take longer.
