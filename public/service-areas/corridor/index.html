Our air duct cleaning process

We use proven, step-by-step methods to clean ducts in homes and commercial buildings. Curious what the day looks like?

Inspection and testing

We check supply and return vents, grilles, and the air handler. We look for visible debris, gaps, or signs of moisture. If needed, we use a small camera to see deeper inside.

Containment and protection

We cover floors and furniture, protect corners, and set up negative air pressure with HEPA filtration to keep dust from drifting into your rooms. Learn more about HEPA filtration.

Agitation and debris removal

We loosen buildup with soft brushes and air whips, then pull it out with a high-powered vacuum connected to your duct trunk. We work methodically vent by vent.

Coil and blower cleaning

Dirt on the evaporator coil and blower ramps up energy use. We clean these parts when needed so airflow and cooling improve.

Duct sealing and repair

If we see loose joints or gaps, we can seal or repair them on the spot. Sealing stops dust from getting sucked in and helps balance airflow.

Sanitizing and disinfecting

We offer duct sanitizing and residential HVAC air duct disinfecting to treat bacteria and odor sources. For businesses, we offer commercial HVAC air duct disinfecting.

Final checks

We verify air movement at the registers and do a walk-through with you. We leave the space clean and tidy.

Signs your ducts or HVAC need attention

Not sure if you need a service call? Look for these clues

  • Dust puffs from vents when the fan starts
  • A stale or musty smell that gets worse when the AC runs
  • Rooms on the same floor with large temperature swings
  • AC runs longer than it used to to hit the same set point
  • Higher energy bills with no big change in use
  • Dryer takes two or three cycles to finish a load
  • Visible lint on the exterior vent hood
  • Rattling ducts or whistling registers
  • Rooftop unit short cycling or tripping breakers
  • Thermostat disconnects from Wi-Fi or acts erratically

If a few of these sound like your place, it might be time to schedule Air Duct Cleaning in Corridor, CA or a full system check.

Air quality and comfort tips for Corridor

  • Change filters on time
  • Many Corridor homes do best with a 1 to 3 month change cycle. If you have pets or live close to El Cajon Boulevard, bump up the schedule.
  • Keep supply and return vents open
  • Closing vents can seem smart, but it often hurts airflow and raises pressure in the system.
  • Watch for moisture
  • If you see water near an indoor unit or inside a closet air handler, call right away. Moisture in ducts invites odors and growth.
  • Clear the outdoor unit
  • Keep 2 to 3 feet of space around the outdoor condenser so air can move through it. Trim bushes and clear leaves.
  • Test smoke alarms and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Safety first. Fresh batteries and quick tests go a long way. Learn more about carbon monoxide detectors.
