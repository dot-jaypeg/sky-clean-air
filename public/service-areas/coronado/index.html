  • Spring blooms and fall Santa Ana winds push pollen and fine dust into homes and shops. Many older homes near A Avenue and B Avenue have original ductwork that breathes in all that air.
  • Short term rentals and frequent guests near the Hotel del Coronado and the Ferry Landing mean more laundry, more foot traffic, and more lint and dust.

Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Coronado, CA

We keep it simple, clean, and respectful of your home or business. You get clear communication, a neat workspace, and results you can feel.

1) Conversation and inspection

We start by listening. Are certain rooms worse than others? Any allergy issues? Any odors? We do a visual check of registers, returns, air handler, and accessible ductwork. If needed, we use a camera to show what is inside.

2) Protect and prep

We lay down covers where we work. We seal registers and set up containment to keep dust from re entering the space. We bring in a negative air machine to pull debris out through a sealed route. No mess blowing back into the room.

3) Source removal

We connect a high powered vacuum to your duct system. Then we use tools that brush and gently agitate the duct walls to move dust, sand, pet hair, and buildup toward the vacuum. We clean each run and each supply line, not just the first few feet.

4) Supply, return, and air handler cleaning

We clean supply and return grills, then the return plenums. We address the air handler or furnace cabinet as needed. For many homes near Glorietta Bay with packaged systems, we clean the blower compartment and accessible coil surfaces.

5) Duct sealing check

If we see gaps or leakage during cleaning, we flag them. A small leak in an attic above the Cays or a crawl space near the beach can pull in moist air and dust. We can seal those leaks and balance airflow after cleaning. If needed, we can perform duct leakage testing and airflow balancing.

6) Sanitizing and deodorizing

If you request it, we apply a safe disinfecting treatment inside the duct system. This can help with odors from past smoking, pets, or moisture events. We also offer residential and commercial HVAC air duct disinfecting.

7) Final check and walkthrough

We reassemble registers, remove protection, and show you photos or video of before and after. We test airflow with the system on. Have questions? Ask away. We want you to understand what changed.

Signs you need service now

Not sure if this is the right time? Look for these cues.

Air duct and HVAC clues

  • Dust puffs from vents when the system starts
  • A musty or sour smell with airflow
  • Rooms feel stuffy even with the AC on
  • Weak airflow at far registers
  • Your system cycles a lot but never gets comfortable
  • Allergy or asthma symptoms get worse indoors
  • You hear rattling or whistling from ducts

AC and heating clues

  • Warm air from the AC
  • The outdoor unit runs loud
  • Ice on refrigerant lines
  • Short cycling or frequent resets
  • Thermostat does not match room temp

Dryer vent clues

  • Dryer takes longer than one cycle
  • Burning smell while drying
  • Lint around the vent hood outside
  • Laundry room feels extra hot or humid

Attic and insulation clues

  • Rooms under the attic heat up by noon
  • Drafts on windy days
  • Old or matted insulation visible during a quick attic peek

If any of these ring a bell, a quick inspection can prevent bigger repairs. We are happy to take a look.

Indoor air quality upgrades that actually help

Want cleaner air without hype? Here are upgrades that our Coronado clients like because they work and they are easy to maintain.

  • Whole home air purifiers. These units sit in your ductwork and trap fine particles that standard filters miss. Great for pollen, pet dander, and dust.
  • Media filter installation. A deeper filter cabinet improves filtration without choking airflow. We size it right for your system.
  • UV air purifier installation. UV lights at the coil or in the supply plenum can reduce microbial growth on surfaces and help control odors.
  • Humidifier and dehumidifier installation. Coronado usually has mild humidity, but certain rooms and seasons may need balance. We set levels that keep things comfortable.
  • Duct sealing and airflow balancing. Sealing gaps and then setting each register to deliver the right flow can make rooms feel even and cut energy waste.

Not sure which option fits your home or business? We will explain what each item does and give you a straight answer on expected results.
