Our step by step duct cleaning process

We keep it simple and thorough so you know exactly what is happening in your home or business.

  • 1) Inspection and setup. We start by checking your supply and return ducts, air handler, and filter. We look for loose connections, crushed flex, and dirty coils. We cover floors and furniture near vents, and set protective corner guards where needed.
  • 2) Create safe containment. We seal supply and return registers to control dust movement. A powerful vacuum with HEPA filtration connects to your duct system to pull debris out while we work.
  • 3) Agitation and debris removal. We use industry grade brushes and air whips to loosen dust and debris inside each run. The vacuum pulls everything into a sealed collection unit. We clean each register cover and surrounding area before it goes back on the wall or floor.
  • 4) Return and trunk line cleaning. Returns carry the heaviest load. We spend extra time cleaning return runs, the plenum, and accessible trunk lines.
  • 5) Air handler and coil care. We clean the blower compartment and accessible surfaces. If the evaporator coil or blower wheel is dirty, we can include AC Cleaning for a deeper result and better airflow.
  • 6) Optional sanitizing. We offer duct sanitizing and disinfecting for homes and businesses. This service targets odor causing microbes, cooking smells, and pet dander residue. Ask us to include Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting or Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting with your visit.
  • 7) Final check and photos. We test airflow, confirm system operation, and provide before and after photos where access allows. We leave your space as clean as we found it.

Curious how this would work in your split level near Mount Soledad or your condo off Cass Street? Call 619-304-8822 and we will walk you through it.

Duct sealing, repair, and replacement

Cleaning helps a lot, but air loss from leaky ducts can be the bigger issue. Many homes in La Jolla Mesa have older flex duct in warm attics. Over time, seams loosen, insulation sags, and rodents can cause damage.

We can help with

  • Duct leakage testing to measure actual loss
  • Mastic sealing and metal tape at seams and boots
  • Reinsulation of bare or thin duct sections
  • Duct repair or full replacement when runs are crushed or torn
  • New supply drops to improve airflow in problem rooms
  • Duct balancing and damper adjustments for even temperatures

Sealing stops wasted air, cuts dust, and often fixes that one room over the garage that never feels right.
