Our Step by Step Duct Cleaning Process

Curious how the work is done without making a mess? Here is our typical residential process. Commercial systems follow a similar path with equipment sized for larger ductwork.

  • 1) Inspection and airflow check We start with a visual inspection, a static pressure reading, and a look at registers and returns. If needed, we use a small inspection camera to check hard to see sections.
  • 2) Protection and prep We protect floors, furniture, and vents. We set up a high powered negative air machine with HEPA filtration so dust goes into our equipment, not into your living room.
  • 3) Source removal cleaning We open access points, brush and agitate the ducts, and vacuum debris. We remove and clean registers and grilles. We clean the return side carefully, since that is where a lot of debris collects.
  • 4) Air handler and coil area cleaning If accessible, we clean the blower compartment and check the evaporator coil. A coil coated with dust reduces cooling efficiency. When the coil needs deeper service, we will let you know.
  • 5) Optional duct disinfecting If odors or growth are present, we apply a safe disinfecting agent inside the duct system. We also offer Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting and Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting for added hygiene.
  • 6) Seal, test, and show results We close access points, balance registers if needed, test airflow again, and show you photo results. You get tips for filter changes and system care going forward.

Tierrasanta homes often have laundry in closets or interior rooms, which means longer vent runs with more bends. Lint builds up and blocks airflow. Besides wasted energy, blockage increases the risk of a dryer fire. Learn about safety guidance from the CPSC.
