Signs your system needs attention

Air duct and airflow signs

  • Dust blasts from vents when the fan starts
  • Dark streaks around registers
  • Hot or cold rooms that never match the thermostat
  • Odors that show up with airflow

AC and heating signs

  • System runs longer than it used to
  • Short cycling or frequent restarts
  • Warm air from the AC or cool air from the heater
  • Odd noises from the attic or rooftop

Dryer vent signs

  • Clothes take longer to dry
  • Dryer or laundry room gets very hot
  • Burning smell near the dryer
  • Lint buildup around the outside vent hood

Insulation and attic signs

  • Big temperature swings between floors
  • Drafty rooms even with windows closed
  • HVAC runs often on mild days
  • Dust trails at attic access points

If you see two or more of these, a focused check can save time and money.
