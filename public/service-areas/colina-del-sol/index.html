Our Process for Air Duct Cleaning in Colina del Sol, CA

Curious what actually happens during a proper duct cleaning? Here is how we do it.

1) Inspection and Setup

We start with a visual check of your supply and return ducts, your indoor unit, and registers. We look for heavy dust, debris, damaged flex lines, gaps, or any signs of moisture. We protect floors and furniture, then set up negative air machines with HEPA filtration to capture particles as we work.

2) Source Removal Cleaning

We connect our vacuum equipment to the trunk line and use rotary brushes and compressed air whips to dislodge buildup. We clean each supply run and the main return lines. Grilles and registers are removed and cleaned too. The dust goes straight into sealed filtration, not back into your home.

3) Coil and Blower Area Attention

If your evaporator coil face and blower wheel are accessible and dirty, we can clean them. Cleaner coils improve efficiency and can help your home cool and heat more evenly.

4) Ductwork Sanitizing by Request

If odors or microbes are a concern, we can apply a duct sanitizer approved for occupied spaces. This step helps with musty smells and light bacterial growth. It is not a scent bomb, and it leaves no heavy perfume.

5) Final Check and Photos

We finish with a final walkthrough, reattach and seal access points, and verify airflow. We can show before and after photos so you can see what came out.
