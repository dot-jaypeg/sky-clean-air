Our Process For Cleaner, Safer Ducts

You should know what is getting done in your space. Here is how Sky Clean Air handles duct cleaning for homes, apartments, and commercial properties in Mission Valley West.

Step 1 Inspection and testing

  • Visual inspection of the air handler, supply and return trunks, and accessible branches
  • Ductwork inspection to spot damage, leaks, or disconnected runs
  • Optional duct leakage testing if airflow problems or high bills are reported
  • Photos or video of buildup and problem areas upon request

Step 2 Containment and protection

  • Drop cloths and corner guards to protect floors and walls
  • Sealing supply registers during cleaning so debris stays contained
  • HEPA negative-air machines to capture fine particles

Step 3 Source removal cleaning

  • Agitation tools that loosen dust, debris, and pet hair inside the ducts
  • Powerful vacuum collection at the trunk line to remove buildup
  • Cleaning of supply, return, and main trunks where accessible

Step 4 Sanitizing and deodorizing

  • Duct sanitizing to reduce bacteria and odors
  • Deodorizing for lingering smells from smoke, pets, or moisture

Step 5 Sealing and balancing

  • Duct sealing for joints, seams, and connections that leak conditioned air
  • Airflow balancing to improve comfort room to room
  • Thermostat check and settings review

Step 6 Final walkthrough and tips

  • Show what was removed and what was repaired
  • Maintenance tips that match your building and usage
  • Clear invoice with any recommendations prioritized by need and budget

Want the short version? We clean what you breathe through, seal the leaks, and set your system up to push clean air where you actually need it.
