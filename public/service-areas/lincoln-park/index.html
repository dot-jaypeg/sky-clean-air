Our air duct cleaning process for homes and small businesses

You do not have to guess what we are doing. Here is the typical flow for residential and light commercial duct cleaning.

Inspection and airflow check

We look at supply and return vents, inspect the air handler and filter area, and measure airflow if needed. We also check for signs of leaks, kinks, crushed flex duct, or rodent activity.

Protecting your space

We lay down covers for floors and furniture. Supply and return openings are sealed off to control dust while we work.

Negative pressure setup

A high-powered vacuum with HEPA filtration attaches to the main trunk or air handler, creating strong suction that pulls debris out of the system instead of into your rooms.

Agitation and source removal

Brushes and air whips loosen dust, pet hair, and debris inside the ducts. The vacuum captures it all. We also clean the blower compartment and return cavities if accessible.

Vent register cleaning

We remove and wash registers or grilles, then reinstall them once the duct interior is clean.

Duct Sanitizing

If odors, moisture, or past pest issues are present, we can apply a sanitizer that targets odor-causing bacteria. We only suggest this step when it makes sense for your space.

Duct Sealing and small fixes

We seal obvious gaps at boots and connections and can schedule larger repairs if we find broken or collapsed runs. Fixing leaks can boost airflow to the rooms that need it most.

Airflow Balancing checks

We check airflow again, adjust dampers when available, and point out any issues that may need further service. Ask about airflow balancing if certain rooms need more air.

Before and after photos

We show you what we removed so you know the work made a difference.

Most single-family homes can be cleaned in half a day to a full day, depending on system size and access. Small offices or shops around Lincoln Park are often similar. If you have a bigger building, we will set a plan that fits business hours to limit disruption.

Signs you need duct cleaning or repair

Do any of these sound familiar?

  • Visible dust puffs from vents at start up
  • Musty or smoky odors that do not go away
  • Dark rings around vents or dust on ceiling near registers
  • Uneven airflow or rooms that never feel right
  • Rising energy bills without a clear reason
  • Rattling or whistling noises in the ductwork
  • Recent remodels, pest activity, or water damage

Air Duct Cleaning in Lincoln Park, CA addresses buildup and odor issues. If we find leaks or crushed sections, we can repair or replace those runs and recheck airflow.

How often to clean ducts and tune your system

Most homes benefit from duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Consider sooner if you

  • Completed a remodel or new flooring
  • Have multiple pets that shed
  • Noticed soot or smoke exposure from nearby fires
  • Had rodents or insects in the attic or crawlspace

HVAC tune ups are smart before heavy use. Spring for AC, fall for heat. If your system covers a shop or kitchen near Imperial Avenue with frequent door traffic, twice yearly checks can keep things steady.

Keep your air clean between visits

Small habits go a long way

  • Change filters on schedule. If you have pets or allergies, step up to a better filter and swap it more often.
  • Keep return grilles clear of furniture and clutter.
  • Vacuum registers and grilles gently to stop surface dust.
  • Ask about sealing visible duct gaps in garages and attics.
  • Run bath fans and kitchen hoods to cut moisture and grease that move into ducts.

If you want a filter size upgrade or a media filter cabinet that traps more dust, we can help. Good filtration can stretch time between cleanings.
