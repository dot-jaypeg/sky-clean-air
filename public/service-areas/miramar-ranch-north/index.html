Our duct cleaning process

We use professional equipment with HEPA filtration. The goal is to remove debris without sending it back into the home.

  • 1) System inspection We check supply and return vents, the air handler, and accessible ductwork. If we spot damage, leaks, or loose connections, we note those so you can choose how to handle them.
  • 2) Protective prep We lay down floor covers, set up corner guards, and isolate the work area. We close off registers to control airflow during cleaning.
  • 3) Negative pressure We connect a high powered vacuum to your duct system and create strong negative pressure. This keeps dust from escaping indoors.
  • 4) Agitation and removal We brush and air whip the ducts, dislodging dust, pet hair, dander, and debris. The vacuum pulls it all out through HEPA filters.
  • 5) Return side and blower cleaning We clean the return ducts and the blower compartment area where accessible. This helps with airflow and reduces odors.
  • 6) Optional duct sanitizing We can apply a safe disinfecting treatment inside the ducts after cleaning to neutralize odors and kill microbe growth on contact.
  • 7) Final check We show you before and after photos. You see what we removed and the difference inside your vents.

How often should you clean your ducts

Many homes do well with cleaning every 3 to 5 years. If you live near dusty trails, have pets, or run the system a lot during summer, you may benefit from more frequent cleaning. Commercial spaces often need annual cleaning because of higher foot traffic and longer run times.

Tips to keep ducts cleaner longer

  • Change filters on schedule
  • Many homes do best with high quality pleated filters changed every 1 to 3 months. If you have pets or recent construction dust, check more often.
  • Keep return grilles clear
  • Do not block returns with furniture or curtains.
  • Use your range hood
  • Cooking smoke and grease can drift into returns. Run the hood while cooking and a few minutes after.
  • Seal gaps
  • Small gaps at doors and windows let dusty air in. Sealing helps keep that out.
  • Schedule regular maintenance
  • An annual AC tune up helps keep coils and blower parts clean, which supports clean ducts.
