Our air duct cleaning process

We keep it clear and simple. Here is how a standard residential duct cleaning visit goes.

  • 1. Arrival and walk through. We confirm your concerns, count registers and returns, and check the air handler. We protect floors, move light furniture if needed, and put down corner guards and drop cloths.
  • 2. System inspection. We check filter size and condition, duct material, and access points. If we see rips, loose connections, kinks, or signs of pests, we show you and talk through repair options.
  • 3. Negative pressure setup. We connect a high powered HEPA vacuum to your main trunk line, then close off vents to direct airflow. This pulls dust and debris out without sending it back into your rooms.
  • 4. Agitation and source removal. We brush and air whip each supply and return run, loosening buildup so the vacuum can capture it. Registers and grills get cleaned too.
  • 5. Air handler and coil area cleaning. If accessible, we clean the blower compartment and housing. If the evaporator coil is dirty, we can clean it or schedule a separate coil cleaning.
  • 6. Duct sanitizing or disinfecting. If you ask for it, we apply a fogged product inside the ducts. We offer Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting and Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting. We only use EPA registered products for HVAC use and apply them to label instructions.
  • 7. Before and after photos. You see what we saw. We want you to know your ducts are clean, not just hear it.
  • 8. Filter and run test. We install a new filter if you have one ready or provide one. Then we run the system to confirm strong, even airflow.

Typical visit time is 2 to 4 hours for a home, longer for large homes. Commercial jobs vary by size. We can schedule off-hours service for venues and offices near the Prado or the Old Globe.

Maintenance that keeps your system steady

Small steps keep big repairs away:

  • Replace or wash filters every 1 to 3 months, based on your filter type and pets
  • Book an HVAC tune up twice a year, before summer and before winter
  • Keep 2 to 3 feet of clear space around outdoor condensers
  • Do not block return grilles with furniture or curtains
  • Ask for duct inspections if you remodel or add rooms

We offer maintenance visits that include system cleaning, safety checks, and efficiency checks. We can pair your tune up with a light AC cleaning or a dryer vent cleaning to save a trip.
