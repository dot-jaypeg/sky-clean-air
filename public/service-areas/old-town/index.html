Why Old Town Homes and Businesses Need Clean Ducts

Old Town is full of life and history. The same features that bring visitors can bring extra dust and debris into buildings.

  • Freeways and traffic near I-5 and I-8 increase soot and fine particles.

Signs Your Ducts Need Attention

  • Dust puffs when the system starts
  • Black lines on walls or ceilings by supply vents
  • Musty or stale odors from registers
  • Rising energy bills with the same thermostat settings
  • Hot and cold spots that never balance
  • Allergy or asthma flare ups indoors
  • Recent remodel or new flooring that left fine dust in the system
  • Rodent or pest signs in the attic or crawlspace

If two or more of these sound familiar, it may be time for a cleaning or duct repair.

Air Duct Cleaning in Old Town, CA What We Do And How We Do It

Air Duct Cleaning in Old Town, CA is a complete process. We do not just vacuum the first few feet and move on. Our steps keep debris under control and out of your home.

Step 1 Inspection And Setup

  • We inspect accessible ductwork, grilles, and the air handler or furnace.
  • We place floor and corner protection.
  • We set up a negative air machine with HEPA filtration to keep your space clean while we work.

Step 2 Source Removal

  • Rotating brushes and air whips loosen debris on metal and flex ducts.
  • We connect to the trunk line to capture particles while we brush branch ducts.
  • We clean supply and return runs, registers, boots, and the plenum.
  • If needed, we remove and wash registers and grilles.

Step 3 Duct Sanitizing

  • We can apply a duct safe sanitizer by request to address odor causing bacteria.
  • We keep it targeted and use products made for HVAC use.
  • We never fog random chemicals into a system.

Step 4 Optional Add Ons

  • Evaporator coil cleaning
  • Blower wheel cleaning
  • New media filter or whole home air purifier installation
  • UV air purifier bulb replacement

Step 5 Test And Tidy

  • We reassemble, test airflow, and confirm all registers are open and moving air.
  • We vacuum the work area and haul away debris.
  • You get before and after photos upon request.

Most homes take 2 to 4 hours. Small commercial spaces vary with square footage, number of registers, and whether we work after hours.

Have a tight schedule between lunch and dinner rush? We plan around that. Need us in the early morning near the Old Town Transit Center? We can do that too.

Seasonal HVAC Tips For Old Town, CA

Want fewer surprises through the year? A few simple steps help.

  • Spring Change to fresh MERV 11 or MERV 13 filters before pollen peaks. Schedule a cooling tune up and coil cleaning if the coil is dusty.
  • Early Summer Check refrigerant levels and test thermostat programs. Confirm attic insulation depth before the hottest days.
  • Late Summer Santa Ana conditions can bring particles. Replace filters more often during smoky weeks. Consider a whole home air purifier or a UV system.
  • Fall Heat pump or furnace check, burner or defrost test, and duct inspection. Seal obvious leaks at boots and seams.
  • Winter Use dehumidification on coastal fog days to keep windows clear and odors down. Ventilate kitchens and bathrooms to control moisture.

Do you rent out your home near Heritage Park? Set filter reminders for the day after each guest checkout. That keeps the next stay fresh and the system efficient.

Dryer Vent Cleaning And Safety

Dryer lint builds up faster than most people think, especially in multi family buildings and small inns. A blocked vent can double dry times and raise fire risk.

  • Dryer Vent Cleaning clears the line from the dryer to the exterior cap.
  • Dryer Vent Rerouting shortens long, twisting runs.
  • Dryer Vent Booster Fan Installation helps long lines move lint out.
  • We clean the lint trap housing and check the duct material for safety.

If guests or tenants complain about long dry times, or the laundry room feels hot and humid, a vent cleaning may solve it.
