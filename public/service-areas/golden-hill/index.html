Air Duct Cleaning in Golden Hill, CA

Air Duct Cleaning in Golden Hill, CA is our core service, and we do it right. Our team uses professional equipment to remove the dust, debris, and allergens hiding in your ductwork. We respect your space, lay down protection, and leave your vents clean and tidy.

Our cleaning process

Curious what actually happens during a cleaning? Here is a quick rundown

  • Pre-visit call and on-time arrival
  • Visual inspection of supply and return ducts, plus a camera view if needed
  • Protection of floors and furniture around vents
  • High-powered negative air machine to capture dust safely
  • Rotary brush and compressed air tools to loosen stubborn debris
  • Register and grille cleaning
  • Filter check or replacement at your request
  • Optional duct sanitizing for odor or microbial control
  • Airflow test and final walk-through

You get a clean air path from the return to every supply vent. We can show you before and after results too.

Signs you need duct cleaning

  • Dust puffs from vents when the system starts
  • Musty or smoky odors that never leave
  • Allergy flare-ups inside the home or office
  • Visible dust lines around vents or on ceilings
  • Recent remodel or drywall work
  • Pest droppings or evidence of insects in registers
  • Uneven airflow room to room

How often should ducts be cleaned

Most homes do well with a cleaning every 3 to 5 years. If you have multiple pets, recent construction, smokers, or live next to busy roads like 28th Street or near the 94 on-ramps, you may want service a little sooner.
