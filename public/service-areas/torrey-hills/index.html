Our air duct cleaning process

Clean ducts start with a careful process. Here is how we handle it.

  • Inspection and testing We check grills, registers, and main trunks. We look for buildup, damaged insulation, disconnected runs, and signs of moisture. If you are curious, we show you what we see.
  • Protecting your home We place covers, corner guards, and drop cloths. Boots stay clean. Pets get gates or a safe room if needed. Your home stays tidy.
  • Negative pressure vacuum We attach a high power vacuum to create strong suction. This moves loosened dust out to a sealed collection unit.
  • Source removal agitation We use rotating brushes and air whips inside the ducts. This breaks loose stuck on debris. The vacuum captures it right away.
  • Register and return cleaning Grilles and registers get removed, washed, and reinstalled. We also vacuum the boot areas that collect heavy dust.
  • Optional duct sanitizing If odors are a problem, we offer duct disinfecting for residential and commercial systems. This helps after water leaks, pests, or smoke events.
  • Duct sealing and repairs If we find gaps or loose connections, we can seal them. Less leakage means stronger airflow and better comfort.
  • Filtration and airflow review We check your filter fit, MERV level, and blower condition. We can upgrade filters and tune airflow to reduce new buildup.

Have questions while we work? Ask away. We like curious homeowners. We can show before and after photos so you see what changed.

Airflow, duct testing, and balancing

Airflow is the backbone of comfort. You can have a powerful AC, but if the ducts leak or are out of balance, rooms will still feel off.

Duct Leakage Testing

We pressurize the duct system and measure how much air escapes. Even small gaps add up. Sealing can bring back lost airflow and cut dust that sneaks in from attics or garages. Learn more about Duct Leakage Testing.

Airflow Balancing

We measure supply and return flow and adjust dampers. The goal is steady temperatures from the front room to the back bedroom. Helpful for two story homes off Carmel Country Road where upstairs rooms warm up fast. Explore Duct Balancing.

Duct Repair and Replacement

Crushed flex, long runs with sharp turns, or disconnected boots can choke airflow. We fix layout problems and insulate to reduce heat gain. We handle Air Duct Repair and Air Duct Replacement.

Curious if your ducts are leaking? Put your hand near a return while the blower runs. Feel air pulling in around the grille frame or wall gap? That might be unfiltered bypass air. We can seal that.

Attic insulation and ventilation make HVAC better

Comfort is not just about the equipment. The attic over your rooms acts like a thermal blanket. Small upgrades up there make a big change down here.

  • Blown in, batt and roll, and spray foam. We install the right insulation to stop heat gain.
  • Radiant barriers. Reflect heat from the sun, which matters on clear Torrey Hills days.
  • Air sealing. We seal gaps around lights, chases, and ducts so your cool air stays inside.
  • Attic ventilation. Proper intake and exhaust vents move out hot air, keeping your attic and ducts cooler.

Pair these with duct sealing and your system runs less and lasts longer. Learn about Attic Insulation options.
