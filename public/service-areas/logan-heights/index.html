Our duct cleaning process

1. Walkthrough and inspection

We start with a quick walkthrough to learn your main concerns. We check supply and return registers, the air handler, accessible duct runs, and the blower compartment. If you want, we can show you inside a vent with a small camera so you can see what we see.

2. Protect and prepare

We lay down floor coverings near the air handler and each vent, place corner guards, and cover furniture if needed. We seal off vents to control airflow during cleaning and set up a negative air machine outside or in a safe spot. This creates strong suction so dust goes into our equipment, not into your rooms.

3. Source removal cleaning

We attach the vacuum to the duct system, then move through each run with rotary brushes and compressed air tools. Stuck-on lint, dust, and light construction debris break free and head into our HEPA-filtered collection unit. We clean returns and supplies, not just what you can see at the vent.

4. Component cleaning

If your coil and blower compartment are accessible, we can clean them too. This step helps remove buildup that affects performance and odor. We also wash registers and grilles and reinstall them once they are dry.

5. Sanitizing and disinfecting options

If odors are a concern, we can apply duct sanitizing or disinfecting agents that are approved for residential use. For added protection in high-traffic spaces or after a remodel, ask about Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting or Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting. We will explain the products we use in plain terms so you can choose what fits your needs.

6. Duct sealing and airflow checks

If we find gaps or disconnected sections, we can handle duct sealing and simple repairs. We can also perform duct leakage testing and airflow balancing to help your system move air more evenly. That warm back bedroom off Ocean View Boulevard might finally feel as comfortable as the front room.

7. Final check and photos

We walk you through what we did, share before and after photos if you would like to see them, and make sure your thermostat and system are running correctly. Then we clean up our workspace so your place looks the way it did before we arrived, minus the dust.
