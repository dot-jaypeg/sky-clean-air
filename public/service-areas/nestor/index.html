Our air duct cleaning process

You should know exactly what is happening in your home or building. Here is how we handle duct cleaning from start to finish.

Assessment and photos

  • We check supply and return vents, the blower compartment, and major trunks.
  • We take photos or video so you can see the starting point.

Source removal cleaning

  • We attach a high-powered vacuum that pulls debris out through a HEPA filter.
  • We brush and air whip branch lines and trunks to loosen dust and lint.
  • We remove and clean registers and grills.

Return and blower cleaning

  • We clean the return box, blower, and accessible evaporator coil surface.
  • We replace filters or install new media filters if requested.

Disinfecting treatment

  • On request, we apply a safe disinfecting agent inside ducts after cleaning.
  • Helpful for funky smells, minor microbial growth, and allergy concerns.

Final check

  • We run the system, take after photos, and make basic airflow notes.
  • If we find duct leaks or crushed runs, we show you and explain repair options.

We keep it clean while we work. Drop cloths, shoe covers, and careful handling are part of the job. No mess left behind. No guessing games.

Signs you may need more than cleaning

Cleaning removes dust and light buildup, but it cannot fix damaged ducts. Here are signs your system might need repair, sealing, or replacement parts.

  • Persistent hot or cold spots even after cleaning
  • High utility bills with no change in usage
  • Loud whistling or fluttering noise from vents
  • A faint smell of insulation or attic in the air
  • Visible gaps, crushed flexible ducts, or loose tape

We can test your ducts for leakage, seal gaps, balance airflow, and replace damaged runs. The goal is simple. Even air to every room, less energy waste, and a quieter system. Explore Duct Leakage Testing, Duct Balancing, Air Duct Repair, and Air Duct Replacement.
