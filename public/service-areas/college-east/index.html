Ways to improve air without a full remodel

Not ready for big changes? Try these steps first.

  • Use the right filter. Many systems in College East do well with a pleated media filter that captures fine dust without starving airflow. We can size and install the right type.
  • Keep returns clear. Move furniture and decor away from return grilles so your system can breathe.
  • Vacuum registers and grilles. A quick pass keeps lint and hair from blowing back inside.
  • Run the fan after dusty jobs. After a DIY project or move in, run the fan with a clean filter to help capture fine dust.
  • Check your dryer lint trap and vent hood. Clean both often to reduce risk and speed dry times.
  • Watch for warning signs. Odd odors, new rattles, or water near the air handler need quick attention.

What does duct cleaning include and not include

Included on a standard duct cleaning visit.

  • Inspection of the air handler or furnace, accessible ductwork, and registers.
  • Connection of HEPA collection equipment and cleaning tools.
  • Cleaning of supply ducts and return ducts, registers, and grilles.
  • Light cleaning of blower compartment if accessible.
  • Basic sealing of small gaps using approved materials.
  • Optional duct sanitizing if requested.

Not included unless requested or quoted.

  • Major duct rerouting or full duct replacement.
  • Coil pull and deep clean that requires system evacuation.
  • Asbestos abatement.
  • Drywall or carpentry unrelated to duct access.
  • Roofing work beyond safe access for rooftop units.

If we find bigger issues, we will show you and offer options with clear pricing.

How often should you schedule duct cleaning

Most homes in College East benefit from cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Shorten that cycle if you have

  • Smoker history or heavy cooking with oils.
  • Long duct runs in a dusty attic or crawl.

Businesses with heavy foot traffic or long open hours may need annual cleaning, plus frequent filter service.
