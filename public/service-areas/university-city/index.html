What our air duct cleaning includes

We keep it simple and thorough. Here is how a standard residential duct cleaning visit usually goes.

  • Pre-visit walkthrough and vent count
  • Set down floor and register protection
  • Source removal with a powerful negative-pressure vacuum
  • Mechanical agitation to loosen dust and debris
  • Brush and clean supply and return runs
  • Clean registers and grilles
  • Clean blower compartment area where accessible
  • Apply duct sanitizing upon request
  • Final check of airflow at vents

Signs your ducts need attention

You do not have to guess. Look for these clear signs.

  • Dust puffs from vents when the fan starts
  • Visible lint or fuzz on vent blades
  • Vents look gray or greasy
  • Odors that never quite go away
  • More dust than usual on tables and shelves
  • Family members feel better outdoors than indoors
  • Recent construction or new floors installed
  • Rodents or insects found near duct openings
  • Higher energy bills with no obvious cause
