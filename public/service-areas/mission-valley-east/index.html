Residential Air Duct Cleaning in Mission Valley East, CA

If you live in an apartment near the Green Line trolley stations, a condo off Friars Road, or a single-family home on the hills by Grantville, clean ductwork helps your place feel fresher. Air Duct Cleaning in Mission Valley East, CA is one of our most requested services.

Signs your ducts need cleaning

  • Dust streaks on vent covers and around returns
  • More sneezing or itchy eyes while indoors
  • Musty or smoky smells when the AC or heat starts
  • Rooms that feel stuffy even with the fan on
  • Recent drywall or flooring work
  • Pets that shed and a filter that clogs fast

How we clean your ducts

We follow a clear process from start to finish, and we keep you in the loop.

  • Full system check. We inspect supply and return ducts, registers, and access points.
  • Protect the home. We lay down drop cloths and cover vents not being worked on.
  • Strong vacuum with HEPA filtration. This captures dust and debris from inside the ducts. Learn about HEPA filtration.
  • Agitation tools. Brushes or air whips loosen stuck dirt so the vacuum can remove it.
  • Return and supply lines cleaned end to end. Not just what you can see at the vent.
  • Plenum and trunk attention. Where buildup often hides.
  • Optional duct sanitizing. Helpful for musty odors after water events or smoke events.
  • Final airflow check and clean registers. We tidy up and reinstall covers.
  • Photos and notes. You see what we removed and what we recommend next.

Curious what is inside your ducts? We can show video or photos so you do not have to wonder.

Duct sealing and balancing

A system can lose a big chunk of air through leaky joints in attics or chases. We seal with mastic and foil tape at seams, boots, and plenum connections. Then we verify with pressure readings where needed. Balancing helps even out hot back bedrooms or front offices that get too cold.

Have a room over the garage near Camino Del Rio that never cools right? A quick balancing visit and a proper seal may be all it needs.

Why cleaning ducts helps HVAC efficiency

Think about your ducts like your lungs. If they are clogged, the blower has to work harder. That can cause:
