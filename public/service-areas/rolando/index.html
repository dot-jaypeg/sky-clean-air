What you get with our duct cleaning service

We aim to make the process simple and thorough. Here is how a typical project goes.

Assessment and ductwork inspection

  • We check supply and return vents, the air handler, plenum, and accessible trunk lines
  • We look for visible buildup, damaged duct sections, and weak connections
  • If you want, we can run Duct Leakage Testing to see where air is escaping

Prep to protect your space

  • Drop cloths cover floors and furniture near vents
  • Registers are removed and cleaned
  • The system is isolated so dust does not blow into rooms

Negative pressure cleaning

  • A high capacity vacuum connects to the main trunk
  • Agitation tools break loose stuck debris inside the duct walls
  • Dust moves out to a sealed containment unit, not back into your home or business

Return and blower cleaning

  • We clean returns and the blower cabinet area
  • If the coil needs cleaning, we can include AC cleaning as part of the visit

Add on services as needed

  • Duct Sanitizing or Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting for odors, bacteria, or after smoke events
  • Duct Sealing for loose joints or small gaps
  • Airflow Balancing to even out rooms that run hot or cold
  • Ductwork Repair or replacement for crushed or torn lines

Final check

  • We confirm suction and airflow improvements
  • We reinstall registers and tidy the area
  • You get tips for filter changes and system care

Have pets, allergies, or a recent renovation. Tell us during scheduling so we can plan the right tools and time slot for your situation.

Do you need cleaning, sealing, or both

Cleaning gets rid of dust and allergens. Sealing fixes leaks that waste energy. Many Rolando homes benefit from both. Here is a quick guide.

Cleaning makes sense if

  • Dust puffs out of vents when the system turns on
  • There is a stale or smoky odor from vents
  • You see dust buildup on registers
  • Someone in the home has allergies that get worse indoors

Sealing makes sense if

  • Some rooms never reach the right temperature
  • The system runs long cycles and still feels weak
  • Utility bills jumped but your usage did not change
  • You hear whistling in duct runs or see gaps in the attic

Not sure which one you need. We can start with Ductwork Inspection and Duct Leakage Testing. From there, we give you a clear plan and pricing before any work starts.
