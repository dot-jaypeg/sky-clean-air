Safety checks you can do today

Want to spot early problems before they grow? Try these quick checks.

  • Look for black lines or fuzz around vents
  • Check that your return grille is not blocked by furniture
  • Replace filters every 1 to 3 months, or wash reusable ones monthly
  • Listen for new sounds at start up or shutdown
  • Peek at the outdoor unit. Clear leaves and lint from the coil fins
  • Check the dryer vent outside. Weak air flow means it is time to clean

If something looks off, snap a photo and text or email it to us. We can usually tell you next steps right away.
