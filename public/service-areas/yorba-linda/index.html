HVAC & Air Duct Services in Yorba Linda, CA

Family-owned heating, cooling, duct, dryer vent, attic and indoor air quality service for Yorba Linda homes and businesses.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

Yorba Linda sits at the eastern edge of north Orange County, close to the Chino Hills and the Santa Ana River canyon. Summers are hot, and Santa Ana winds can be fierce here. The city has been hit by wildfires before, most notably the 2008 Freeway Complex fire, so smoke is a real indoor air concern. Many homes are larger two-story houses from the 1970s through the 90s, with long duct runs and attic air handlers.

Since Sky Clean Air expanded into Orange County, Yorba Linda homes get the same crew and the same standards we've kept in San Diego. We clean ducts and dryer vents, repair and replace AC and heating systems, add attic insulation and set up better filtration for smoky days. For larger homes with aging systems, we'll help you prioritize what needs attention now and what can wait.

What We See in Yorba Linda Homes

  • Wildfire smoke makes good filtration worth having in Yorba Linda.
  • Santa Ana winds push dust through gaps and into ducts.
  • Larger two-story homes often have uneven cooling between floors.

Neighborhoods we serve in Yorba Linda: Old Town Yorba Linda, East Lake Village, Vista del Verde, and surrounding areas.
