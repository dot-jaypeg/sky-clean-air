Air Duct Cleaning in Skyline CA What We Do

We make Air Duct Cleaning in Skyline CA simple and thorough. Our approach protects your home while we remove the buildup hiding inside your supply and return ducts.

Our process

  • Inspection and airflow check
    • We look at registers grills return areas the blower compartment and accessible ductwork
    • We check airflow differences room by room
  • Protection and setup
    • We cover registers and set up a powerful negative air vacuum with HEPA filtration
    • We protect floors and nearby furniture
  • Agitation and vacuum removal
    • We use professional grade rotary brushes and air whips to loosen stuck dust and debris
    • High suction pulls contaminants out to our sealed containment
  • Cleaning vents and plenums
    • Registers grilles and accessible plenums get cleaned
    • If needed we can clean the blower compartment and evaporator coil under our AC cleaning service
  • Safe disinfecting on request
    • We can apply a gentle duct disinfectant for odor control in return ducts
  • Final check
    • We re measure airflow look for duct leaks and confirm results with you

Most homes take 2 to 4 hours depending on system size accessibility and how clogged the ductwork is. Larger homes older duct systems or heavy buildup can take longer. We will outline timing before we start.

Add On Fixes That Make a Big Difference

Dust removal is step one. If leaks or restrictions remain the problems come back. We offer practical upgrades that pay off in comfort and efficiency.

  • Duct sealing and repair
    • Seals gaps tears and loose connections that waste conditioned air
    • Helps rooms reach the set temperature faster
  • Airflow balancing
    • Adjusts dampers and registers to even out hot and cold spots
    • Reduces system noise and drafts
  • Duct replacement or rerouting
    • Replaces crushed damaged or severely contaminated duct runs
    • Fixes poor layouts and tight turns that choke airflow
  • Duct insulation
    • Improves efficiency especially in attics with high summer heat
    • Reduces condensation risk and heat gain

Ask us to check your ducts for leaks during your cleaning. We can show you issues right away and prioritize what matters most. If you want data backed answers we can perform duct leakage testing.
