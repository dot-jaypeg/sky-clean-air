How dirty ducts affect your home or business

Ducts act like the lungs of your HVAC system. If they are clogged, leaky, or poorly sealed, your system works harder to move the same air. That can mean

  • Extra dust on surfaces
  • Stale odors that do not go away
  • Hot and cold spots in rooms
  • Strain on your blower motor
  • Higher power bills

Cleaning and sealing your ducts can restore airflow, reduce dust, and protect your HVAC investment. Many mid-century Allied Gardens homes have older flex or metal ducts that collect debris and leak at seams. We fix that.

Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Allied Gardens, CA

Curious how the cleaning actually works? Here is our step by step approach.

1) Home or site check

We walk the home or building with you, ask about any rooms with poor airflow, and map the duct layout where possible. Older homes near Princess View Drive often have attic runs that need eyes on them.

2) Protect your space

We cover registers, lay down drop cloths, and set up containment where needed. We protect floors, counters, and attic access points.

3) Negative pressure and agitation

We connect a powerful vacuum with HEPA filtration to your duct system to pull debris in one direction. While suction is running, we use rotating brush tools and air whips to loosen dust, pollen, lint, and pet hair from the duct walls.

4) Supply and return side cleaning

We clean the return plenum and supply trunk lines, then each branch run that feeds rooms. Grilles and registers are removed and washed.

5) Optional sanitizing

If odors or microbial growth are a concern, we can fog the ductwork with an HVAC safe sanitizing agent after the mechanical cleaning. This targets odor causing contaminants in the ducts.

6) Duct sealing and small repairs

We seal accessible gaps and joints with mastic or foil backed tape. If we find crushed flex duct or disconnected lines, we can fix many minor issues on the spot with your approval.

7) System start up and check

We reinstall grilles, start the system, check airflow from vents, and clean up the work area. You get before and after photos for peace of mind.

Have questions about any step? Ask away. We are happy to explain the why behind the work being done.

What you can do between visits

A few simple habits help keep your system clean longer.

  • Change filters on time. We can set a reminder schedule with you.
  • Keep return grilles clear of furniture and dust.
  • Run your fan on auto, not ON, unless we suggest otherwise for a zoning setup.
  • If you have pets, consider a higher capacity media filter.
  • After big projects or sanding work, schedule a duct cleaning.
