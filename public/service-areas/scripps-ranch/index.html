Air Duct Cleaning for homes and businesses

Air Duct Cleaning in Scripps Ranch, CA helps reduce dust, improve airflow, and cut down on sneezing fits during spring bloom. Clean ducts also help your system move air with less strain, which can lower noise and energy use.

Here is how we clean ducts

  • Inspection. We check registers, returns, accessible trunk lines, and the air handler. We look for lint, debris, pet hair, and signs of damage.
  • Containment and protection. We cover floors and furniture around registers and set up containment as needed.
  • Powerful source removal. We use high-suction equipment with HEPA filtration to pull dust and debris while we brush each run to dislodge buildup.
  • Supply and return cleaning. We clean vents, grills, and the return box, and we vacuum the blower compartment area when accessible.
  • Optional sanitizing. If there are odors or past moisture issues, we can apply a safe disinfecting treatment inside the ducts.
  • Final check. We review results with you. Want before and after pictures? We can provide them.

Benefits you will likely notice

  • Less dust on surfaces
  • Fewer stuffy rooms
  • Cleaner smells from vents
  • Smoother airflow that helps your system run easier

Do you have pets, recent remodeling dust, or a family member with allergies? A thorough duct cleaning helps all three.
