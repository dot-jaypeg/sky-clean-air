Our process for duct cleaning

If you have looked up Air Duct Cleaning in Talmadge, CA, you have likely seen a lot of promises. Here is our step-by-step so you know exactly what we do.

  • 1) Walkthrough and safety check. We review your concerns, count supply and return vents, and confirm access to the attic or crawl space. We cover floors and furniture near work areas.
  • 2) Camera inspection. We take a quick look inside main trunks and a few branches. This sets a baseline, shows buildup, and helps spot torn or disconnected ducts.
  • 3) Negative pressure setup. We connect a HEPA vacuum to the supply and return trunks to pull debris out of the system. Vents are sealed to improve suction.
  • 4) Mechanical agitation. We use brushes and air whips inside each branch to loosen dust, pet dander, lint, and light construction debris. The vacuum holds everything in containment.
  • 5) Source removal. Registers and grilles are removed, cleaned, and reinstalled. The fan blower compartment and evaporator coil area are inspected. If needed, we offer coil cleaning.
  • 6) Optional duct sanitizing. If there is a musty odor or mild microbial growth, we can apply a hospital-grade sanitizer made for ducts. It dries fast and does not leave a perfume smell.
  • 7) Sealing and repairs as needed. If we find leaks, torn liners, or crushed flex, we can seal or replace those sections. Proper sealing reduces dust, improves airflow, and helps the system run smoother.
  • 8) Airflow and leakage checks. We can measure static pressure, temperature split, and run Duct Leakage Testing upon request. Airflow Balancing is available if some rooms run hot or cold.
  • 9) Final cleanup and photos. We show you before and after images. Work areas are cleaned, vents are reinstalled, and the system is started to confirm operation.

Most single-family homes in Talmadge take 3 to 5 hours depending on size, number of vents, and how loaded the ducts are.
