Maintenance Tips For Cleaner Air Year Round

Small habits go a long way between professional cleanings.

  • Change filters on time
  • Standard 1 inch filters usually need replacement every 1 to 2 months. Media filters can last longer. If you live closer to the water or have pets, check more often.
  • Keep returns clear
  • Do not block return grilles with furniture or baskets. A blocked return strains the blower and reduces airflow.
  • Vacuum vent covers
  • Use a brush attachment to remove lint and dust from supply and return grilles every few weeks.
  • Control humidity
  • Run bath fans during and after showers. On damp days, use your system in dry mode if available, or a dehumidifier to keep indoor humidity near 40 to 50 percent.
  • Schedule seasonal tune‑ups
  • Tune‑ups catch weak capacitors, dirty coils, and loose connections before they cause a breakdown.
  • Watch the dryer vent
  • If drying time creeps up, call for a cleaning before it turns into a bigger issue.
