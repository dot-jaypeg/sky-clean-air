Signs You Need Duct Cleaning or Repair

If you notice any of these, it might be time for service.

  • Dust bursts from vents at startup
  • A musty or smoky odor that lingers
  • Pets or kids with more sniffles near vented rooms
  • Vents are noisy or whistling
  • Rooms that are hot on the 2nd floor and chilly downstairs
  • A recent remodel or drywall work
  • High energy bills without a clear reason
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to finish clothes

Have you changed your filters lately? If yes and you still see dust or smell odors, duct cleaning or sealing could help.

Filters, Frequency, and Simple Habits

A few easy habits go a long way.

  • Change filters every 1 to 3 months based on usage and pets
  • Keep supply and return vents clear of furniture
  • Do a quick visual check in the attic for disconnected ducts once in a while
  • Run the fan on your thermostat during heavy dust days to keep air moving through the filter
  • Book seasonal tune-ups before summer heat and before winter nights cool down

If you are not sure what filter to buy, text us a photo of your return grille or the current filter label. We will recommend the right size and rating.

Why Clean Ducts Help Your Energy Bill

Leaky or dirty ducts force your system to work harder. That means longer run times and more wear on parts. Clean, sealed ducts move air without fighting through dust piles or holes. That saves energy and can extend the life of your system.

Think of it like biking on flat pavement vs through sand. Your legs know the difference. Your blower motor does too.
