Our Air Duct Cleaning Process

Curious what happens during Air Duct Cleaning in Tijuana River Valley, CA with Sky Clean Air? Here is how we handle it step by step.

  • 1. Inspection and plan We check supply and return ducts, registers, the blower, evaporator coil, and the air handler. We look for dust load, pet hair, lint, water stains, gaps, and signs of pests. Then we map the system so every trunk and branch gets cleaned.
  • 2. Protect your space We lay down drop cloths, cover registers, and set up containment. We connect a high power negative air machine with HEPA filtration to remove dust and keep particles from escaping into your rooms.
  • 3. Dislodge and vacuum debris We use rotating brushes and air whips to loosen debris inside the ducts while the vacuum pulls it out. Each run gets attention until the debris stops and interior duct surfaces are clear.
  • 4. Clean returns and registers Returns often hold more dust than supply lines. We clean them thoroughly, then wash and reinstall all grilles and registers.
  • 5. Address the air handler With your approval, we clean the blower compartment. If the coil needs attention, we can clean it safely. Cleaner coils transfer heat better and lower energy use.
  • 6. Disinfecting option If we find musty odors or microbial growth, we can disinfect duct surfaces with products meant for HVAC use. We apply this safely and only with your okay.
  • 7. Seal and balance options If we find leaks, gaps, or crushed sections, we can seal or repair them. We can also test duct leakage and balance airflow with duct balancing so every room gets its share.
  • 8. Final test and photos We show you what we removed and the interior of your ducts after cleaning. We run the system, check pressures and temperature split, and leave you with a cleaner, smoother running system.

Want to see how much debris is hiding in your vents? Ask for before and after photos. Some folks are shocked at what we pull out.
