Signs Your Ducts Need Attention

Do any of these sound familiar?

  • A dusty film returns on tables and shelves within a day of cleaning
  • A musty or salty smell when the AC or heat starts
  • Allergy flare-ups after windy days or during high surf
  • Vents look dark or fuzzy around the edges
  • Some rooms run hot while others feel chilly
  • The system runs longer, yet airflow is weak
  • Energy bills creep up without a good reason

If you nod yes to even two of these, your ductwork likely needs cleaning, sealing, or repair. Air Duct Cleaning in Lower Hermosa, CA can restore clean airflow and help reduce odors, especially after foggy mornings or during Santa Ana winds that push more dust inside.

Our Air Duct Cleaning Process for Lower Hermosa Homes

We keep it simple and effective. You get clean ducts without a mess in your living room.

  • 1) Inspection and photos. We start with a visual check of supply and return ducts, registers, and the air handler. We take pictures so you can see the before and after.
  • 2) Protect your space. We cover floors, set corner guards, and move light items out of the way. Our hose lines and HEPA filtration stay contained.
  • 3) Create strong negative pressure. We connect a powerful vacuum to the main trunk line to pull dust toward the machine. This captures particles instead of pushing them back into your home.
  • 4) Brush and agitate. We use soft rotating brushes and air whips to dislodge buildup from duct walls, grilles, and registers. This breaks up sticky dust that settles near the coast.
  • 5) Clean the air handler. We clean the blower compartment and accessible components. If the evaporator coil needs attention, we can add AC cleaning to the same visit.
  • 6) Optional disinfecting. We offer duct sanitizing and disinfecting for homes dealing with moldy smells, after a remodel, or if you had smoke exposure. We choose safe products that do not leave strong fumes.
  • 7) Seal small gaps if needed. If we spot minor leaks at connections, we can apply mastic or metal tape. Larger issues move to repair or replacement options.
  • 8) Final check and photos. We verify airflow at key vents and review the results with you. You get clear photos of the improvement.

Curious how long it takes? Most homes in Lower Hermosa take 3 to 5 hours depending on size, number of vents, and how much buildup we find.

How Often Should You Clean Your Ducts Near the Coast

Most homes do well with duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Some homes in Lower Hermosa benefit from cleaning every 2 to 3 years because the reasons below apply.

  • Salt and moisture make dust cling to duct walls
  • Open windows during the marine layer pull in fine particles
  • Homes close to the beach take in sand and sea spray

Everyday Problems We Solve

Musty odors after foggy mornings

Fog and salt air can leave ducts smelling stale. We clean the ducts, treat the return, and check the coil and drain pan. Sanitizing can help neutralize the odor. We also confirm the filter type fits your system.

Dust reappears hours after cleaning the house

Dust stuck inside ducts blows out when the fan runs. We remove buildup, seal leaks that pull in attic dust, and balance airflow so vents do not blast dirt into certain rooms.

Rooms that never feel right

A sunny front room on Camino de la Costa can run warm, while a shaded bedroom stays cool. We check duct size, airflow, and insulation. Zoning and smart vents can even out comfort without overcooling the rest of the house.

A loud system with weak airflow

The blower may be fighting clogged ducts, a dirty coil, or blocked returns. We clean and tune the system, then measure static pressure. If ductwork is too small for the unit, we can add returns or upgrade runs.

Dryer takes two cycles

Lint buildup is common near coastal areas. We clear the vent, verify the roof cap opens freely, and shorten any long or sagging runs. Dryer vent booster fans help with longer routes.

Maintenance Tips for Coastal Homes

  • Use a high-quality filter that fits your system. Change it every 1 to 3 months.
  • Keep supply and return vents open and unblocked by furniture or rugs.
  • Hose off outdoor condensers gently a few times per season to remove salt film. Power washers are too strong.
  • Run bathroom fans after hot showers to pull out moisture.
  • If you open windows for ocean air, plan to swap filters more often.
  • Schedule a spring or early summer tune-up to prepare for heat waves.

Need help picking the right filter or setting a maintenance plan? Ask us during your visit.

Why Duct Cleaning Helps Energy Use

Clean ducts let the blower move air with less resistance. That can shorten run times and cool or heat rooms faster. Sealing leaks keeps conditioned air inside the system instead of losing it into attics or crawl spaces. Combine duct cleaning with coil cleaning and a smart thermostat, and you get consistent comfort with fewer cycles.
