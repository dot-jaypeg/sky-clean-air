Signs You May Need Duct Cleaning

A few common clues point to dirty ducts. Do any of these sound familiar?

  • Dust on vents and returns shortly after you wipe them
  • Musty or smoky odors when the AC or heat turns on
  • Uneven airflow between rooms
  • More coughing or sneezing at home, especially at night
  • Dust puffs coming from registers on startup
  • Pets, renovation dust, or a recent move in to an older property
  • It has been 3 to 5 years since your last duct cleaning

If you recognize two or more, it is time to take a closer look. We can inspect and show you what is going on inside your system.

How Our Duct Cleaning Works

Here is what to expect from Sky Clean Air on a typical cleaning visit.

  • 1) Check in and walk through We listen to your concerns, look at your vents and returns, and confirm access points. We protect floors and furniture with drop cloths.
  • 2) System inspection We open registers and the air handler to see buildup levels. If there are signs of damage or duct leaks, we point them out and offer options. You can also ask about duct leakage testing.
  • 3) Negative pressure vacuum We connect a high powered vacuum to the duct system to pull dust, dirt, and debris away from your living areas.
  • 4) Agitation and brushing We use the right brushes and air tools for your duct type, then work through each run. This loosens stuck debris so the vacuum can remove it.
  • 5) Vent and register cleaning We clean each supply register and the return grille. If needed, we wash and dry covers before putting them back.
  • 6) Optional duct sanitizing If odors or microbial growth are a concern, we can apply a safe treatment that helps neutralize odor causing bacteria inside ducts.
  • 7) Final check and photos We verify airflow, show you results, and clean up our work area. You get a clear idea of what was removed.

Most homes are done the same day. Larger homes or commercial systems may take longer, and we plan timing with you before we start.

How Often Should You Clean Your Ducts?

For most homes, every 3 to 5 years works well. If you have multiple pets, recent construction dust, or a family member with allergies, you may want to schedule every 2 to 3 years. For restaurants, offices, or student housing near USD and Mesa College, usage is higher, so a shorter cycle can help keep airflow steady and odors low.
