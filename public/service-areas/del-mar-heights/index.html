Our down to earth duct cleaning process

We keep it simple and thorough, so you know exactly what is happening in your home or business.

  • 1) Inspect and measure. We take a look at supply and return ducts, registers, and the air handler. We can use a small camera to spot heavy buildup or debris and check for disconnected runs, crushed flex ducts, and signs of leakage.
  • 2) Protect your space. Furniture gets covered, registers are carefully removed, and vents are sealed during cleaning to keep dust from escaping. We move with care in tight hallways and stairways you find in many Del Mar Heights homes.
  • 3) Create strong negative air. We connect a powerful HEPA vacuum to your duct system to pull debris out while we loosen buildup in every branch.
  • 4) Agitate and remove debris. Rotary brushes and air whips break loose dust and lint from every side of the duct interior. We clean supply ducts, return ducts, and the main trunk lines. Registers and grilles are hand cleaned.
  • 5) Optional disinfecting. If odors or bacteria are present, we can apply a safe disinfecting treatment inside the ductwork. This can be useful after water leaks, smoke exposure, or long vacancies.
  • 6) Seal any access and tidy up. We reseal access panels to an airtight finish. We can also seal duct leaks with mastic on request. Then we vacuum floors around each vent, reinstall registers, and leave the area clean.
  • 7) Document and advise. We can show before and after photos, review any airflow restrictions we found, and talk through filter options, balancing, and routine maintenance.

Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Del Mar Heights, CA fits coastal homes and busy commercial suites, and we plan our work to limit downtime.
