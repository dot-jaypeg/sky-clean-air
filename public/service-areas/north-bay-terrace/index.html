Signs it is time to call

Not sure if your ducts need attention? Here are clear signs your system in North Bay Terrace may be asking for help.

  • Dust builds up fast on furniture even right after you clean
  • Vents blow weak air in some rooms and strong air in others
  • You smell mildew, smoke, or a wet sock smell near vents
  • Your dryer takes longer to dry clothes and seems hotter than normal
  • Your AC runs longer than usual to hit the same thermostat setting
  • You hear rattles or whistling in your ductwork
  • You have pets that shed year round

If your vent puffs dust like confetti every time the air kicks on, that is not a party you want. A quick call can solve it before you waste more energy and time.
