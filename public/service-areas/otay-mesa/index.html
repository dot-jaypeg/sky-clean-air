Air Duct Cleaning in Otay Mesa, CA

Dust builds up in ductwork. So do pollen, pet hair, construction debris, and fine particles from busy roads and industrial corridors. Over time, airflow drops and your system works harder. Your home or building may feel stuffy. You may notice more sneezing or a musty odor when the AC kicks on.

Here is how our duct cleaning helps

  • We offer duct sanitizing to reduce odor and microbial growth after cleaning.
  • We can add duct leakage testing and airflow balancing for better efficiency.
