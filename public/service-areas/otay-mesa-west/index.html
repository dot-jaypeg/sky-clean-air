What to expect from Air Duct Cleaning in Otay Mesa West, CA

Our goal is clear with cleaner ducts, better airflow, no mess left behind. Here is how we handle Air Duct Cleaning in Otay Mesa West, CA.

1) Inspection and airflow check

  • We look over supply and return vents, the air handler or furnace area, and accessible ductwork.
  • If needed, we can use camera tools to see buildup or blockages.
  • We note damaged runs, crushed flex ducts, or signs of leaks.

2) Containment and protection

  • We cover registers, protect floors and furniture, and set up a negative air machine with HEPA filtration. This keeps debris from re entering your home.

3) Source removal cleaning

  • We connect a high powered vacuum to the trunk line.
  • We agitate dust and debris with soft brushes or air whips, then move debris toward the vacuum.
  • We repeat across each branch until the ducts are clean.

4) Vent, grille, and blower area cleaning

  • We clean supply and return grilles.
  • We can add coil cleaning, blower wheel cleaning, and cabinet cleaning if your system needs it.

5) Sanitizing and deodorizing options

If your ducts had odors, moisture issues, or pest activity, we can apply a safe disinfecting agent inside the ductwork after the mechanical cleaning. This targets bacteria and smells.

6) Duct sealing and balancing options

  • We seal minor accessible leaks and recommend a full duct leakage test if airflow is poor.
  • We can also balance airflow to help even out hot and cold rooms.

What makes duct cleaning effective

Not all duct cleaning is the same. The key is source removal using a strong vacuum and proper agitation, not just spraying something into the vents. We combine

  • Powerful negative air machines with HEPA filtration
  • Agitation tools that match your duct type, metal or flex
  • Careful sealing during cleaning so debris does not leak into rooms
  • Optional disinfecting for ducts with odor or moisture history

We can also add duct leakage testing. This shows how much air escapes before reaching your rooms. Less leakage means better comfort and lower energy bills.
