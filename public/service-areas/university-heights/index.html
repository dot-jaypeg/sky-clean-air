Signs you need duct or HVAC help

Not sure whether you need a full repair or a simple tune-up? These clues help

  • Dust bursts from vents on startup
  • Black lines on ceiling near supply vents
  • Musty or smoky odors that get stronger when the fan runs
  • Uneven rooms, like a hot living room and a cold bedroom
  • System runs longer but cools less
  • High power or gas bills compared to last season
  • Dryers that take two or more cycles
  • Lint gathering on the outside vent hood
  • AC freezes or leaks water near the air handler
  • Unit trips the breaker often
  • Thermostat settings do nothing or drift
  • Fan runs but air is weak at far vents
  • Whistling or rattling duct sounds

If you see these signs, call us at 619-304-8822. We can usually schedule a same-week visit in University Heights.

How often should you schedule cleaning or maintenance?

Short answer, it depends on your space and use. Here is a simple guide that fits many University Heights homes and businesses.

  • Air duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years, sooner if you see dust bursts, remodel, or adopt pets
  • Duct sanitizing after major cleaning or if you notice persistent odors
  • AC and furnace tune-ups once a year each, spring for cooling and fall for heating
  • Filter changes every 1 to 3 months for standard filters, longer for high quality media filters
  • Dryer vent cleaning yearly for homes, quarterly or monthly for heavy commercial use
  • Attic check every few years or after roof work
