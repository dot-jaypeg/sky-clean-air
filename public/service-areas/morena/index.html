What You Get With Our Air Duct Cleaning

Our goal is simple. Clear out buildup, protect your system, and leave you with cleaner air. Here is how we do it.

  • System inspection
    • We check supply and return ducts, plenums, registers, grilles, and the air handler.
    • We look for disconnected runs, damaged insulation, or signs of moisture.
  • Containment and protection
    • Drop cloths and corner guards protect floors and walls.
    • Registers are carefully removed and cleaned.
  • High-powered vacuum with agitation
    • We connect a negative air machine to pull debris out.
    • Powered brushes and air whips loosen stuck dust without harming ducts.
  • Supply and return cleaning
    • We work each run back to the trunk line, then clean the trunk.
    • We clean the blower area and accessible coils if needed.
  • Sanitizing on request
    • We can apply a light mist of duct sanitizing solution after cleaning if odors or minor microbial growth were present. See helpful EPA guidance on duct cleaning.
  • Airflow check
    • We check key vents and returns to confirm stronger airflow.
  • Pictures before and after
    • You see the difference.

Expect a clean process, clear communication, and results you can feel. If your ducts need repair, sealing, or replacement, we point it out with photos and straight advice.

Maintenance Tips For Morena Residents

Small habits make a big impact.

  • Change filters regularly
    • Many homes near canyon trails need changes every 1 to 2 months in summer. We can recommend the right filter and schedule.
  • Keep returns clear
    • Do not block return grilles with furniture. Air needs a clear path.
  • Watch the outdoor unit
    • Keep leaves and debris away from the AC. Two feet of open space helps.
  • Mind the dryer vent
    • If clothes take longer to dry, call for a cleaning. It saves energy and reduces risk. Start with Dryer Vent Cleaning.
  • Listen and look
    • New noises or hot and cold spots are early signals. Catching them early saves money.
