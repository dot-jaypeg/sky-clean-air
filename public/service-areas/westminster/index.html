HVAC & Air Duct Services in Westminster, CA

Family-owned heating, cooling, duct, dryer vent, attic and indoor air quality service for Westminster homes and businesses.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

Westminster is mostly postwar single-family homes built in the 1950s and 60s, with some newer infill and townhomes mixed in. Close enough to the coast to stay moderate, it still gets warm spells, and homes of this era often have original or partially replaced ductwork running through lightly insulated attics. Many households also run older furnaces that have kept going well past their expected life.

Sky Clean Air has recently expanded into Orange County with the same crew and the same standards we've built in San Diego. In Westminster, we clean ducts and dryer vents, repair and replace AC and heating equipment, and add attic insulation. We also do a whole-home HVAC check during duct and dryer vent visits, so you'll know if your furnace or AC is showing its age before it lets you down.

What We See in Westminster Homes

  • Original 1950s–60s ductwork often leaks conditioned air into the attic.
  • Aging furnaces should be checked before the first cold nights.
  • Lightly insulated attics make summer afternoons uncomfortable indoors.

Neighborhoods we serve in Westminster: Little Saigon, and surrounding areas.
