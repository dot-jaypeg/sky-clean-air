Easy upkeep tips between visits

Want to stretch the benefits of fresh ducts and a tuned system

  • Change or wash filters on schedule. Many homes near the coast do best at 60 to 90 day changes.
  • Keep supply and return vents open and unblocked. Closed vents create pressure problems.
  • Vacuum grilles gently with a brush attachment every month.
  • Keep the return area clear of storage. Boxes can restrict airflow.
  • Check the dryer vent hood outside. Make sure the flap opens while the dryer runs.
  • Use a smart thermostat schedule. Set a steady temp during school or work hours, with slight adjustments at night.
  • Trim plants near outdoor units. Leave 2 to 3 feet of clear space.
  • After a remodel or sanding project, schedule a duct cleaning. Dust hides in returns fast.
  • If smoke or ash rolls in from regional fires, run the system fan with a high quality filter and consider an extra filter change.
