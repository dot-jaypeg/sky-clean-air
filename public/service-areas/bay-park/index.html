Need Air Duct Cleaning in Bay Park, CA? Let’s clear the air

Air ducts collect dust, pet hair, pollen, cooking film, and sometimes a little bay moisture. Over time that buildup can restrict airflow and keep your system running longer than it should. If you notice extra dust around vents, a musty odor when the AC kicks on, or uneven temperatures room to room, it might be time for Air Duct Cleaning in Bay Park, CA.

Here is how our team handles it.

  • We start with a ductwork inspection and photos, so you see what we see.
  • We test airflow and check for duct leakage and loose connections.
  • We clean supply and return ducts using industry standard agitation and negative pressure, then vacuum with HEPA filtration.
  • We clean registers and grilles, and wipe the plenum where accessible.
  • We offer duct sanitizing with safe products for light microbial growth and odor control.
  • If we find damaged or crushed sections, we recommend air duct repair or replacement with clear pricing.

Do you live near Morena Boulevard or along Clairemont Drive where traffic dust can be heavier? Do you get more allergies during Santa Ana winds or wildfire smoke days? Clean ducts and fresh filters can help.

We also tackle

  • Ductwork inspection and testing
  • Duct leakage testing
  • Duct sealing
  • Duct sanitizing
  • Airflow balancing
  • Air duct maintenance, repair, replacement, and installation

Ask about a filter plan if you want reminders and the right media filter size for your system.

Honest answers to common questions we hear

Is duct cleaning messy?

We protect floors and furniture, run negative pressure, and clean as we go. You get a tidy space at the end.

How long does it take?

Most single family homes take half a day to a full day, depending on duct length and condition.

Do I need new ducts?

Not always. Many ducts just need cleaning, sealing, or a simple repair at a loose boot. We only suggest replacement if the ducts are damaged beyond a good fix or are undersized for the system.

Can you work around my schedule?

We set morning or afternoon windows and call on the way. We also plan around Mission Bay traffic and events when possible.
