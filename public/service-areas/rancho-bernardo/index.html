Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Rancho Bernardo, CA

You should know who is coming into your home and what they will do. Here is how we handle your project.

  • 1) Pre-visit call. We confirm your address, parking details near your street, and pet info. We also ask about any odors, hot and cold spots, or recent remodeling.
  • 2) On-site inspection. We check the air handler, return and supply ducts, and layout. If we notice damaged flex or crushed lines, we will note it and explain next steps.
  • 3) Protect and set up. Drop cloths go down. Vents get covered to control dust. We bring in a negative air machine with HEPA filtration.
  • 4) Agitation and removal. We brush and whip the interior of ducts to lift stuck dust, then vacuum it out. Grilles and registers are washed. If you choose, we can clean the blower and coil area to support airflow.
  • 5) Sanitizing or disinfecting. If you request it, we apply a treatment to freshen ducts. We also offer air duct disinfecting for higher-touch needs.
  • 6) Seal, balance, and test. We can seal leaks, adjust dampers, and run airflow checks so you get the most out of the cleaning.
  • 7) Wrap up. We re-attach vents, clean up, and walk the home with you. If you want, we can show you photos of what we removed. Then we talk about filters and set your reminder for the next check.

How often should Rancho Bernardo homes clean ducts and vents

Air Duct Cleaning in Rancho Bernardo, CA is often due every 3 to 5 years. Many homes in RB fall on the sooner side because of dust, pollen, and seasonal smoke. Consider cleaning sooner if

  • You finished a remodel
  • You have pets that shed
  • A family member has allergies
  • You smell musty or smoky vents
  • You moved into an older home and do not know the last service date

Dryer vents are best cleaned every year. If the dryer is in the center of the home, or if drying takes longer than before, you may need cleaning sooner.

Filter tips for RB homes

  • Use the right size and MERV rating for your system
  • Check filters monthly in summer when AC runs longer
  • Replace every 1 to 3 months based on dust and pets
  • Consider a media filter cabinet for better capture and longer life

Not sure what filter is right? We can look at your system and give you a straight answer.
