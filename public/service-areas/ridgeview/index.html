Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Ridgeview, CA

You want clear steps and clean results. Here is how our techs handle your ducts

1. Inspection and plan

  • We walk the home or business, locate supply and return vents, and check access points
  • Camera inspection helps us see inside trunks and branches
  • We review any hot or cold rooms and a short plan so you know what we will do

2. Protection

  • Floor and furniture covers go down where we will work
  • Vents are sealed as needed to create strong suction

3. Negative air and agitation

  • A powerful HEPA vacuum connects to the main duct
  • Rotating brushes and air whips loosen compacted debris in each run
  • Registers, grilles, and return boxes are removed and cleaned

4. Coil and blower area check

  • We look at the blower compartment and evaporator coil for dust build up
  • If coil cleaning is needed, we can service that during the visit or schedule it

5. Sanitizing and deodorizing on request

  • Plant based products are available for odor control
  • We only use products that are safe for homes, kids, and pets

6. Final review

  • We show you what came out
  • We provide tips on filters and schedules that fit your building

You get clear communication, before and after photos on request, and a clean work area when we finish. The goal is simple. A quieter system, better airflow, and cleaner vents.

Maintenance schedules that fit Ridgeview

Filters and tune ups make a bigger difference than most people think. A simple plan can prevent breakdowns and keep air clean.

  • Change 1 inch filters every 1 to 3 months, depending on dust and pets
  • Change 4 to 5 inch media filters every 6 to 12 months
  • HVAC tune ups twice a year, spring and fall
  • Air Duct Cleaning every 3 to 5 years, sooner if you see dust blowback or after renovation
  • Dryer Vent Cleaning every year for homes and every 3 to 6 months for heavy commercial use
  • UV bulb replacement every 1 to 2 years, depending on model
  • Commercial rooftop unit checks quarterly or per site need

Not sure what you need yet? Ask us. We will build a schedule that is easy to follow and easy on your budget.
