Air Duct Cleaning in Chollas View, CA explained

Here is how our process works for homes and small businesses

  • Inspection and setup

    We inspect supply and return ducts, grilles, and the air handler. We protect floors and furniture before we start.

  • High power vacuum collection

    We connect a HEPA vacuum to your system to pull debris out of the ducts.

  • Agitation and brushing

    We use rotary brushes and air whips to loosen dust, lint, and light debris.

  • Source removal

    Dust and contaminants move into sealed collection, not into your living space.

  • Optional duct sanitizing and disinfecting

    We offer Duct Sanitizing and Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting on request. For businesses, we also provide Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting for offices, salons, shops, and restaurants.

  • Final check

    We confirm airflow, reinstall registers, and clean up.

Why duct sealing pays off in our area

Leaky ducts pull hot attic air into the system and push cooled air into the attic instead of the rooms. That is wasted energy and uneven comfort.

Sealing can often
