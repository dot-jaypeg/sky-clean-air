Our step by step duct cleaning process

Curious how this works in your home off La Jolla Farms Road or near La Jolla Shores Drive? Here is our simple process.

1. Talk and plan

  • We ask about hot or cold rooms, odors, dust, allergies, and any recent remodels.
  • We map your system and count supply and return vents.

2. Inspect and test

  • Visual ductwork inspection.
  • Optional camera and Duct Leakage Testing if airflow is weak or bills are high.

3. Prepare the space

  • Cover nearby furniture.
  • Seal vents after removing grilles to create controlled suction.

4. Place negative air machine

  • Attach HEPA equipment to main trunk.
  • Maintain strong suction to capture debris safely.

5. Brush and agitate

  • Use soft rotary brushes and whips to loosen dust, pollen, and lint.
  • Pay special attention to returns, bends, and transitions.

6. Clean plenums and blower area

  • Remove loose debris in supply and return plenums.
  • AC Cleaning available for coils and blower if needed.

7. Sanitizing and sealing

  • Duct Sanitizing to address odors from moisture or pets.
  • Duct Sealing for accessible joints or a quote for full sealing.

8. Reassembly and cleanup

  • Reinstall grilles, wipe registers, and vacuum work areas.
  • Final airflow check and photos if requested.

9. Tips and maintenance plan

  • Filter type and change schedule.
  • Interval for future cleanings based on your home and lifestyle.

Does your home have long runs to rooms facing the canyon or beach? We bring the right tools and enough hose length to reach everything.
