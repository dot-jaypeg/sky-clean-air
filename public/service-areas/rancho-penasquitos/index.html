What Air Duct Cleaning Involves

Curious what we actually do during a cleaning visit? Here is the typical flow for a single-family home in Rancho Peñasquitos.

  • 1) Walkthrough and inspection. We check supply and return vents, your air handler, filter area, and accessible ductwork. We note any damage or gaps and talk through your goals.
  • 2) Set up protection. Drop cloths, corner guards, and floor protection go down. We keep your home tidy while we work.
  • 3) Create negative pressure. We connect a powerful HEPA vacuum to the main trunk or a return. This pulls dust and debris out so it does not spread.
  • 4) Agitate and dislodge debris. We use industry tools like rotary brushes and air whips to break material free from the duct walls. Each run is addressed from end to end.
  • 5) Clean registers and grilles. Registers are removed and cleaned. We clear supply and return openings and check for buildup.
  • 6) Sanitize on request. We can apply a duct sanitizer to address odor and microbial growth. We only use products made for HVAC systems.
  • 7) Seal and balance options. If you choose, we can seal leaks and balance vents for better airflow consistency.
  • 8) Final check and filter. We reinstall registers, test the system, and replace the filter if you want us to provide one. We share what we found and what to watch.

Most single-family homes take a few hours based on duct size and condition. Larger homes or heavy buildup can take longer. We keep you updated at each step.

Signs You Need Duct Cleaning

Not sure if it’s time? Watch for these.

  • Dust trails or dark rings around supply vents
  • More dust on furniture even with regular cleaning
  • Musty or smoky odor when the AC or heat turns on
  • Increased allergy or sneeze issues indoors
  • Weak airflow at certain vents
  • Odd rattling or debris noises in the ducts
  • It has been longer than 3 to 5 years since the last cleaning
  • You had a remodel that created drywall dust or sawdust
