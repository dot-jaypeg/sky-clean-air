How Residential Air Duct Cleaning Works

  • Inspection and photos. We check supply and return ducts, the plenum, and registers. If needed, we look at the blower and coil.
  • Set up. We cover floors, protect registers, and place the vacuum outside or in a safe area. We use negative pressure to pull debris out.
  • Agitation. We brush and whip the interior of the ducts so dust, lint, and light debris break free.
  • Source removal. The vacuum captures particles through HEPA filtration.
  • Sanitizing. On request, we apply an EPA‑registered sanitizer to help address bacteria and odors.
  • Seal and test. We close access points and check airflow. You see before and after results.

Wondering how long it takes? A standard single‑system home often takes 3 to 5 hours, depending on size and access. Homes near the canyon with a lot of dust may take longer. We will tell you upfront.

Our Step‑By‑Step Process For Cleaner Ducts

Curious what happens on service day? Here is our standard approach for both homes and businesses.

  • 1) Pre‑visit call and prep
    • Confirm access, parking, pets, and areas to protect
    • Review your airflow issues and any hot or cold rooms
    • Set expectations for time on site
  • 2) System inspection
    • Check supply and return trunks, branches, and registers
    • Inspect blower and evaporator coil if accessible
    • Verify filter type and fit
  • 3) Set up negative pressure
    • Attach high‑powered vacuum with HEPA filtration
    • Seal registers to focus suction
    • Protect floors and furniture
  • 4) Brush and whip ducts
    • Use rotary brushes and air whips to dislodge dust
    • Move branch by branch, room by room
    • Vacuum captures debris so it does not spread
  • 5) Return side and main plenum
    • Clean returns where dust is heaviest
    • Wipe registers and grilles
  • 6) Optional services
    • Evaporator coil and blower cleaning
    • Plenum sealing, duct repair, or insulation upgrades
    • Sanitizing treatment to help with odors
  • 7) Final checks
    • Reinstall registers and filters
    • Test airflow and temperature splits
    • Walk‑through with photos of results

We can combine Air Duct Cleaning in Bay Ho, CA with HVAC maintenance so your system gets a fresh start the same day.

Signs Your Ducts Or System Need Help

  • Dust puffs from vents when the blower starts
  • Thick dust lines or dark rings around registers
  • Moldy or musty smells that do not go away
  • Large temperature swings across rooms
  • High energy bills even with light use
  • Filter clogs early or looks black after a short time
  • The dryer takes more than one cycle to finish
  • Rattling or whistling sounds in the attic or walls

Do any of these sound familiar? A quick duct inspection and HVAC check can point to the right fix.

Healthier Air Habits Between Visits

  • Change filters on schedule

    Set a phone reminder or write the date on the filter frame.

  • Vacuum with a HEPA bag or canister

    This traps fine dust instead of blowing it back out.

  • Use the kitchen exhaust fan

    It helps reduce grease and particles that travel into returns.

  • Keep supply and return vents clear

    Do not block them with furniture or rugs.

  • Close doors gently

    Slamming can stir up dust in older registers.

  • On smoky days

    Run the system on circulate with a high‑quality filter, and keep windows closed until air quality improves. Learn more about wildfire smoke and indoor air.
