Air Duct Cleaning in Muirlands West, CA what you get

Our process is careful, clean, and made for coastal homes.

Step 1. Inspection and setup

We look over supply and return vents, check the air handler, and map your duct layout. If you have a crawlspace or attic, we look there too. We lay down drop cloths and wear shoe covers. We protect walls, floors, and furniture.

Step 2. Source removal cleaning

We connect a high power HEPA vacuum to your duct system. Rotating brushes and air whips pull dust, sand, and pet hair free while the vacuum captures it. Returns and supplies get attention from end to end. Grilles and registers are cleaned and rinsed.

Step 3. Coil and blower cleaning

If your evaporator coil or blower wheel shows visible buildup, we clean them so your system moves air the way it should. Learn about an evaporator coil.

Step 4. Optional duct sanitizing

We offer non bleach disinfecting that targets bacteria and odor. This is useful after water leaks, smoking, or heavy pet dander. We also provide residential and commercial HVAC air duct disinfecting on a scheduled basis if you need it.

Step 5. Final check

We show you before and after images, check airflow, and make sure every register is reinstalled and balanced.

Maintenance tips for Muirlands West homeowners

  • Change filters regularly Standard 1 inch filters need changing every 1 to 2 months in coastal areas with open windows. High MERV media filters last longer. We can help you pick the right type for your home.
  • Keep returns clear Do not block return grilles with furniture. A choked return makes your system loud and lazy.
  • Rinse the outdoor unit Lightly rinse coils with a garden hose to remove salt mist and dust. Power washers are too strong. If the unit is on a slope, watch your footing.
  • Watch for water Check the condensate drain near the air handler. If you see water in the pan or wet spots in the closet, call right away.
  • Schedule seasonal tune ups Spring tune ups prep your system for summer. Fall visits get heating ready for cool nights.
  • Dryer care Clean the lint screen every load. If drying times climb, do not wait. A quick vent cleaning saves energy and reduces risk.
  • Use your thermostat Set schedules that match your routine. Smart thermostats learn patterns and can keep you comfortable without guessing.
