How Our Air Duct Cleaning Works

You want results without a circus in your living room. Here is how we make it smooth.

  • 1) Inspection and plan. We check supply and return vents, access points, and the air handler. If needed, we use camera tools to see deep inside the ducts. We tell you what we see and the plan to fix it.
  • 2) Protect your space. We cover floors and furniture near our work area. We set up containment so dust stays inside our equipment, not in your home.
  • 3) Strong vacuum setup. We connect a high power negative air machine to pull debris from your ducts. Think of it as a giant shop vac that can handle a house.
  • 4) Agitate and dislodge. We run soft rotating brush heads and compressed air whips through each duct run. This breaks loose dust, dander, and light construction debris.
  • 5) Clean the trunk lines and return. We do not stop at the vents. We clean the main lines and return side too.
  • 6) Optional disinfecting. If odors, pet dander, or microbes are a concern, we can apply an HVAC safe disinfectant. It helps neutralize odor sources and reduces growth on surfaces.
  • 7) Seal and finish. We seal access points and can seal visible leaks at boots. If you want full duct sealing, we can quote that on the spot.
  • 8) Final check. We review the work with you and go over any next steps that could save energy or improve airflow.

Air Duct Cleaning in Memorial, CA with this process gives you real results. Most jobs take a few hours, and you can use your system right after we finish.

Signs Your Ducts Need Cleaning or Repair

Watch for these common clues in Memorial homes and shops:

  • Vents puff dust at startup
  • Gray lines at vent edges
  • Persistent odor from vents
  • Weak airflow at far rooms
  • Hot or cold spots that never go away
  • Rising energy bills without heavier use
  • Rattling or whistling from registers
  • Extra lint on dryer outlet screen after each load

If you see two or more, Air Duct Cleaning in Memorial, CA may help. If leaks or crushed runs are found, sealing or replacement could be the winning move.
