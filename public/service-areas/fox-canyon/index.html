What We Do During a Duct Cleaning Visit

Inspect and diagnose

  • We check supply and return vents, the main trunk lines, and the air handler.
  • We note any damaged or sagging ducts, missing insulation, or suspected leaks.
  • If needed, we use a camera to look inside runs and show you what we find.

Set up containment and HEPA filtration

  • Drop cloths, corner guards, and shoe covers protect floors and walls.
  • HEPA-filtered negative air machines capture dust so it does not float around your living room.

Agitate and extract debris

  • Rotary brushes and air whips loosen buildup on duct walls.
  • A high-powered vacuum draws debris into sealed HEPA units.

Clean grills, registers, and the blower area

  • We remove and wash grilles and registers.
  • We clean the blower compartment and evaporator coil area if accessible.
  • Filters are checked and replaced at your request.

Duct sanitizing on request

  • We offer residential HVAC air duct disinfecting and commercial HVAC air duct disinfecting with solutions made for occupied spaces.
  • This helps with lingering odors from pets, cooking, or moisture issues.

Final check and airflow test

  • We measure airflow at vents, verify pressure, and make notes about improvements or needed repairs.

Wondering how long it takes? Most homes take a few hours. Larger homes or small commercial spaces can take longer, especially if we are also sealing leaks or replacing sections.

Signs Your Ducts Need Help

Local Tips For Better Air And Comfort In Fox Canyon

  • Mind the filter Replace or wash filters every 1 to 3 months, especially near University Avenue or during fire season.
  • Keep supply vents open Closing too many registers throws off pressure and can cause coil icing or duct leaks.
  • Watch for duct damage in attics Contractors sometimes step on ducts during remodels. If a room suddenly feels off, a crushed duct may be the reason.
  • Control moisture Run bathroom fans after showers and the kitchen hood while cooking. Dehumidifiers help in stuffy rooms.
  • Upgrade old flex ducts Brittle or uninsulated runs lose energy and collect dust. New ducts with tight connections improve airflow fast.
  • Consider zoning or a mini-split If back bedrooms near 52nd Street stay warm, a small zone or a mini-split can fix it without overcooling the rest of the house.
  • Schedule cleanings during shoulder seasons Spring and fall appointments are easier to book and keep summer and winter running smoothly.

What To Expect On The Day Of Duct Cleaning

  • Before we start We do a walk-through, confirm vent counts, and check access to the air handler and attic if needed. Pets can be kept in a quiet room so doors do not open and close all day.

  • After the work We clean registers, re-install them, change the filter if requested, and show you before and after images. We measure airflow and temperatures at the supply vents and talk through any duct repairs or sealing that would help.
