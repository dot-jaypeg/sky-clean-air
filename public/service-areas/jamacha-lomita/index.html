Why your ducts may be the hidden issue

  • Leaky joints that pull hot attic air into the system
  • Insulation that has slumped off round ducts, which causes condensation and mold risk
  • Crushed flex runs that cut airflow to bedrooms
  • Old boot connections with gaps that blow conditioned air into wall cavities

Sealing and correcting these problems can raise airflow, cool your rooms faster, and cut energy waste.

Smart thermostats and zoning for real comfort

If your home spans multiple floors or spreads out along a long hallway, zoning helps. We install zone boards and dampers that split your system into separate areas. That gives you real control and keeps family debates about the thermostat short. We also set up smart thermostats and show you how to use schedules that match your life.

Keep your dryer safe and fast

Lint buildup is a top cause of dryer problems and fires. We measure air velocity at the exterior hood, not just at the back of the dryer. If the vent is too long or has sharp bends, we can reroute it or add a booster fan to meet code and keep airflow strong. A clean vent shortens dry times and keeps the appliance running longer.

Seasonal tips for Jamacha Lomita

  • After Santa Ana winds, check your filter. If it is gray or wavy, swap it and consider a duct clean.
  • Before summer, schedule an AC tune up. A clean coil and proper refrigerant levels matter on triple digit days.
  • Trim bushes around outdoor units to at least two feet of clearance.
  • Keep supply vents open. Closing too many can raise static pressure and strain the blower.
  • Look at your dryer vent hood while the dryer runs. If air barely moves, it is time to clean.

Small steps keep your system running strong.
