How our duct cleaning service works

We follow a steady, clean process that respects your space.

  • Inspection and photos We check supply and return ducts, boots, and the air handler. We look for leaks, gaps, crushed sections, and insulation issues. We share pictures and a plan before we start.
  • Containment and protection Vents are sealed as needed. We protect floors and furniture near registers. Equipment stays on mats.
  • High efficiency vacuum and agitation We connect a powerful vacuum to the duct trunk. Rotary brushes and air whips loosen debris. The vacuum captures dust and contaminants. We also clean grills and registers.
  • Coil and blower check If the evaporator coil or blower wheel is dirty, we recommend cleaning. Moving clean air through a dirty coil still hurts performance.
  • Sanitizing option We can apply a safe sanitizer to help with odor causing microbes. This is helpful in ducts that had moisture issues.
  • Duct sealing and balancing If we find leaks or poor airflow, we can seal joints and balance dampers to even out temperatures. We also offer Duct Balancing.
  • Final photos and test We show the difference and test airflow again.

If you need Air Duct Cleaning in Palm City, CA for multiple units in an apartment building or a mixed use property, we can schedule during off hours to keep tenants and customers happy.
