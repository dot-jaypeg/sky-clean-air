Our Process for Air Duct Cleaning in Hidden Valley, CA

We keep it clear and simple. Here is how a typical visit goes.

  • Inspection and plan We start with a full walk-through of supply and return vents, your air handler, and accessible duct runs. On many homes in Hidden Valley, parts of the system sit in the attic or in a crawlspace behind the garage. We document what we find and explain the best approach.
  • Containment and protection We lay protective coverings at entry points and along paths we will use. We wear clean shoe covers. Grilles and registers are removed carefully and cleaned.
  • Source removal cleaning We use high-powered negative air machines with HEPA filtration and rotating brushes or compressed air tools that push debris toward the vacuum. The goal is to remove the dust, not just stir it up. We clean the supply and return sides, and we wipe the plenum where reachable.
  • Coil and blower cleaning option If your evaporator coil or blower housing has visible buildup, we can clean those components. This can make a big difference for airflow and efficiency.
  • Duct sanitizing If odors or past moisture issues are present, we can apply an HVAC-safe disinfecting treatment. This targets microbes that may cling to duct surfaces. We only suggest this step when it is useful.
  • Final checks and photos We show you before and after images, re-test airflow, and make maintenance suggestions that fit your home.

City streets like Torrey Pines Road can get busy, and driveways in Hidden Valley can be steep. Our crews plan arrival windows that make sense for you. We also coordinate with HOAs when needed.

How Often Should Ducts Be Cleaned in Hidden Valley

Usage, pets, and location all matter. For most households in Hidden Valley, we suggest every 3 to 5 years. Homes with shedding pets, major remodeling, or smoke exposure may need cleaning more often. Small Fixes That Make a Big Difference

  • Filters Swap filters every 1 to 2 months in summer and fall. Use the highest MERV your system supports without restricting airflow. We can help you pick the right one.
  • Sealing attic bypasses Gaps around can lights and chases allow attic air into your home. Attic air sealing keeps conditioned air where you want it.
  • Thermostat schedules Set realistic schedules that match your routine. We can set up geofencing so your home starts cooling before you get back from errands at La Jolla Village.
  • Supply and return balance Make sure furniture does not block vents. Keep 12 inches clear where possible.

These simple steps make your new duct cleaning last longer and help your system run smoothly.
