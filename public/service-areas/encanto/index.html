Air Duct Cleaning in Encanto, CA done the right way

Here is what a typical job looks like

  • 1. Friendly inspection. We look at supply and return vents, measure airflow, and check the air handler. If needed, we use inspection tools to see inside ducts.
  • 2. Prep and protect. We cover floors and furniture near work areas. Vents and registers are sealed to create strong suction.
  • 3. Powerful negative pressure. A high efficiency vacuum is connected to your duct system to pull debris out. This is not a shop vac. It is built for HVAC cleaning.
  • 4. Agitation and brushing. We loosen debris from duct surfaces with soft tools and air whips. This helps remove dust, construction grit, and pet hair stuck to the walls of the ducts.
  • 5. Return and supply lines. Both sides get cleaned, including main trunks and branch lines.
  • 6. Optional sanitizing. If there are odor or microbial concerns, we can apply a safe disinfecting treatment to duct interiors.
  • 7. Final check. We reassemble everything, re measure airflow, and show you the results.
  • 8. Simple tips. We give you practical steps to keep the system cleaner longer, including filter choices and change schedules.

You get clear results without a messy home. Want to see before and after images? Ask during your estimate. Ready to book? Call 619-304-8822.

Signs your ducts or HVAC need attention

  • Dust builds up fast even after you clean
  • Musty or smoky odor from vents
  • Hot and cold spots across rooms on the same floor
  • Airflow feels weak at far vents
  • Rising energy bills with no change in use
  • Allergy symptoms that get worse at home
  • Noisy blower or rattling duct sections
  • Dryer takes too long to dry loads

If a few of these sound familiar, a duct cleaning or system tune up can make a big difference.
