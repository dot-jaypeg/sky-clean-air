What to expect during a visit

We respect your time and space. Here is how a typical air duct cleaning or HVAC visit goes

  • 1) We arrive on time, greet you, and cover walkways and vents. Shoes stay clean, and drop cloths go down in work areas.
  • 2) We inspect the system. That includes supply and return vents, the air handler, coils, blower wheel, and the filter rack. We look for signs of leaks or damage.
  • 3) We set up negative pressure. A high power vacuum connects to the trunk line. Each vent is sealed during the process so dust goes out to our equipment, not into your rooms.
  • 4) We brush ducts and registers. Rotating brushes and air whips loosen debris. We clean grills and returns at the same time.
  • 5) We offer Duct Sanitizing. If you choose it, we apply a safe disinfectant inside ducts. It targets odors and microbes.
  • 6) We reassemble and test. Vents go back on, pressure is checked, and the system runs. We look for good airflow and listen for odd noises.
  • 7) We clean up. Floors and work areas look the same or better than when we arrived. We can show before and after photos so you can see the results.

Got pets, kids, or a sleeping baby? Tell us up front. We will plan the order of rooms so you get peace and quiet where you need it most.
