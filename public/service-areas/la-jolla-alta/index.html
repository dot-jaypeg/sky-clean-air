Air Duct Cleaning in La Jolla Alta, CA

Think about everything that rides the air through your home. Beach sand from sandals, pollen from canyon trails, pet hair, construction dust from remodels, and regular old dust. Over time it settles inside your ductwork. That buildup can slow airflow, nudge up energy bills, trigger sniffles, and leave rooms uneven.

Air Duct Cleaning in La Jolla Alta, CA keeps air moving smoothly and helps your system breathe easier. If your home sits up on Via Capri and you love to leave windows open for the view, you probably pull in extra fine dust. If you live closer to La Jolla Parkway and get traffic residue, it can land in your filter and ducts. Either way, a proper cleaning helps.

Signs you may need duct cleaning

  • You dust, and the dust comes back within a day
  • A musty or stale smell when the AC or heat starts

  • You see debris around vents or dark streaks on grilles
  • Allergy or asthma symptoms feel worse indoors

  • It has been 4 to 7 years since the last cleaning

Have you noticed any of these at your place near Mount Soledad Park or down toward La Jolla Boulevard? A quick inspection can answer that in minutes.

How we clean ducts

We use industry standard equipment and a step by step process to give your ducts a fresh start.

  • Inspection and photos, so you can see what we see
  • High powered HEPA vacuum and negative pressure on the trunk line
  • Rotary brush and air whip tools to dislodge buildup
  • Supply and return vents cleaned and vacuumed
  • Blower compartment, fan blades, and coil area cleared of debris where accessible
  • Optional duct sanitizing on request
  • Final photos and airflow check

We treat dust bunnies like unwanted houseguests. Out they go.
