What does duct cleaning fix?

Air moves through your ducts every time the system runs. Over time, dust, lint, and fine debris settle inside. That buildup can:

  • Reduce airflow and strain the blower motor
  • Spread odors and irritants
  • Feed dust back into rooms
  • Hide early signs of duct damage or leaks

Regular cleaning clears the buildup, and it also gives us a clear look at your ductwork so we can find leaks, loose connections, or crushed runs that need attention.

Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Birdland, CA

Curious how it works? Here is our step by step:

  • 1) System inspection
  • We assess the duct layout, supply and return registers, the air handler, filter slot, and any accessible attic or crawlspace duct sections. We note flexible versus rigid ducts, size, and any visible damage.
  • 2) Containment and protection
  • We protect floors and furnishings, seal registers where needed, and set up negative pressure with high powered HEPA filtration equipment to keep dust from escaping into rooms.
  • 3) Source removal cleaning
  • We agitate and vacuum ducts section by section with specialized brushes and air tools. Debris travels to a sealed collection unit outside. We clean supply and return trunks, branches, and the blower compartment if needed.
  • 4) Sanitizing on request
  • If odors or light microbial growth are present, we can apply an EPA registered sanitizer inside the ducts after cleaning. We only suggest it when it makes sense.
  • 5) Final checks
  • We re seal access points, verify airflow at registers, and review any issues found, like disconnected runs or kinks.
  • 6) Clear, honest report
  • You get a summary of what we did and any recommendations to help your system stay clean longer.

You want proof the job was done right. We are happy to show before and after photos and walk you through the results.

Duct sealing, balancing, and testing

Cleaning is step one. Performance tuning is step two.

Duct sealing

We seal accessible joints, boots, and seams with mastic or approved tapes. This stops air from escaping into the attic or crawlspace.

Airflow balancing

We adjust dampers and measure supply airflow at each register. The goal is steady temperatures and quiet operation.

Duct leakage testing

We depressurize the duct system and measure leakage to quantify loss. Then we target the biggest trouble spots.

These steps often bring quick gains in comfort and energy use.

How often should Birdland residents clean ducts?

Most homes do well every 3 to 5 years. Sooner if:

  • You have pets that shed
  • Someone in the home has allergies or asthma
  • You recently remodeled
  • Your filter clogs faster than expected
  • You notice odors or visible dust puffs at vents

If you are unsure, we can inspect and give an honest opinion. If cleaning is not needed yet, we will say so.

Filters and good habits to keep ducts cleaner longer

  • Use a quality pleated filter with a MERV rating that your system can handle
  • Check filters monthly in peak cooling or heating season
  • Keep supply and return vents clear of furniture and drapes
  • Dust and vacuum regularly with a HEPA vacuum
  • Schedule AC maintenance before the hot season
  • Fix duct leaks so your system does not pull dusty attic air

Small steps make a big difference.
