Troubleshooting Issues You Might Notice

Weak airflow

Check filters. A clogged filter cuts air movement. If the filter looks clean, ducts may be blocked or the blower needs service.

Hot and cold spots

Registers may be closed or misaligned. Duct balancing can fix uneven rooms and help your system run smoother.

AC runs but air is warm

The outdoor unit may be dirty or the refrigerant level may be off. Turn the system off and call us to prevent damage.

Burning smell from heat

A slight dusty smell is common on the first cold night. If it lasts or gets stronger, turn off the system and call for service.

Dryer takes too long

Strong lint buildup is likely. Stop the cycle and book Dryer Vent Cleaning to reduce risk and stress on your dryer.
