HVAC & Air Duct Services in Del Mar, CA

Family-owned heating, cooling, duct, dryer vent, attic and indoor air quality service for Del Mar homes and businesses.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

Del Mar is small, coastal and close to the ocean on nearly every street. Salt air and marine-layer humidity are constant here, so outdoor units corrode faster and ducts can hold onto moisture. Homes range from older beach cottages to modern custom builds on the bluffs. Many older homes have limited ductwork or none at all, and some newer ones have high-end systems that need careful service.

For Del Mar homes, Sky Clean Air focuses on what the coast does to a system. We clean ducts and dryer vents, repair and install heating and cooling systems, and help with air purification and humidity. For homes near the water, we'll check for corrosion and moisture problems. For newer homes, we'll keep your system running well so it's ready when a warm spell hits.

What We See in Del Mar Homes

  • Salt air near the beach corrodes outdoor units quickly.
  • Marine-layer humidity can leave ducts damp and musty.
  • Older cottages may have limited or no ductwork.

Neighborhoods we serve in Del Mar: Del Mar Village, and surrounding areas.
