What our Air Duct Cleaning in Alta Vista, CA includes

We do more than vacuum a vent and call it a day. Our process targets dust, lint, dander, and debris from the entire duct system.

  • Inspection and photos
    • We check supply and return ducts, grilles, and the air handler or furnace cabinet

  • Source removal cleaning
    • Negative air machine creates strong suction to pull debris out of ducts

    • We remove and hand-clean registers and grilles

  • Evaporator coil and blower area inspection
    • If needed, we can clean the blower, cabinet, and coil for better performance
  • Fogging and sanitizing on request
    • We can apply a disinfecting mist to reduce bacteria and odors
  • Final photos and run test
    • You see after photos
    • We power up the system and confirm strong, even airflow

Sanitizing and disinfecting options

If odors linger after cooking, pets, or moisture, a duct sanitizing treatment helps. We fog an EPA registered disinfectant through the duct system after mechanical cleaning. It reaches nooks brushes miss and leaves a fresher smell. We only apply this after a full cleaning for best results.

Duct sealing and repair

Leaky ducts pull hot attic air in summer and cool air in winter. That wastes energy and blows dust where you do not want it. We can:

  • Test for leaks
  • Seal joints and seams
  • Replace crushed or torn flex duct
  • Rehang sagging lines for better flow

Tight ducts mean cleaner air and less strain on your equipment.
