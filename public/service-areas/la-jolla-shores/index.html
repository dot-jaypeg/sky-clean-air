What you get with a proper cleaning

A quick vacuum at the register is not a full duct cleaning. Our approach reaches the entire path of airflow.

  • Inspection and airflow testing

  • Mechanical agitation inside ducts to loosen stubborn dust
  • Cleaning of supply and return trunks, branches, and registers
  • Coil and blower cleaning options if needed
  • Duct sanitizing on request for odors or microbial growth
