Signs you need duct cleaning

  • Dust buildup on vents and returns
  • Musty or stale odors when the fan kicks on
  • Rising allergies or irritation while indoors
  • Visible debris inside registers
  • Recently finished construction or renovations nearby

Our cleaning process

  • Inspection and access
    • We check vents, returns, and plenums.
    • We look for damage, leaks, heavy buildup, and restrictions.
    • We set access ports where needed and protect floors and furniture.
  • Powerful negative pressure
    • A HEPA negative air machine draws dust and debris out of the system.
    • We seal vents in zones so contaminants do not escape into rooms.
  • Agitation and source removal
    • Rotating brushes or air whips loosen stubborn buildup from ducts, trunk lines, and the blower cabinet.
    • We vacuum all loosened debris with HEPA filtration.
  • Coil and blower cleaning
    • Dirty coils reduce efficiency and can lead to foul odors.
    • We clean the coil, blower wheel, and cabinet to improve airflow.
  • Sanitizing and deodorizing
    • Optional treatment to reduce odor causing bacteria and light microbial growth.
    • Safe products, applied with care, and only if needed.
  • Before and after proof
    • We can provide photos of your vents and trunk lines so you see the difference.
    • We label service panels and note any repairs we recommend.
  • Final air filter set up
    • We install a fresh filter and show you the right size and MERV level for your system.
