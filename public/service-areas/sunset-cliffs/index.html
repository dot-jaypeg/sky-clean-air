Need Air Duct Cleaning in Sunset Cliffs, CA

If your vents look dirty, they probably are. Air Duct Cleaning in Sunset Cliffs, CA helps remove dust, dander, lint, and salt residue so your HVAC moves air freely again. Cleaner ducts can reduce odors, ease breathing, and help your system run more efficiently.

Here is how our team at Sky Clean Air tackles a typical job

  • 1. Inspection and photos. We check supply and return lines, registers, and the air handler. You see what we see.
  • 2. Seal and protect. We cover registers, protect floors, and set up safe access.
  • 3. Negative pressure vacuum. A powerful HEPA system pulls debris from the ducts.
  • 4. Mechanical agitation. Brushes and air whips break loose stuck debris and sand film.
  • 5. Return and trunk line focus. These areas collect the most buildup.
  • 6. Optional disinfecting. We can apply a safe disinfecting treatment to reduce odor causing bacteria.
  • 7. Final checks and photos. You get before and after images so you know what changed.

Do your vents run across a crawlspace or attic near the cliffs. We also inspect for holes, poor joints, and crushed sections. Duct leaks waste energy and suck in dust from attics and crawl areas. If we spot issues, we can repair or replace sections to keep your air clean, and we can verify results with duct leakage testing.

How Often Should You Schedule Air Duct Cleaning

In a coastal area like Sunset Cliffs, ducts can collect debris faster. Most homes benefit from cleaning every 2 to 4 years. Rentals with frequent turnover, homes with pets, or spaces near busy roads may need it more often. Businesses with heavy foot traffic often schedule annual or semiannual cleanings.

Dryer vents need attention more often. Clean at least once a year, or twice a year if you wash a lot of towels, bedding, or pet items.
