What you get with Sky Clean Air duct cleaning

We make the process simple and tidy. Curious how it works?

  • Assessment and photos. We check vents, returns, and main trunks, then show you what we find.
  • Containment and protection. We cover registers, protect floors, and place corner guards where we bring hoses inside.
  • Negative pressure cleaning. We connect a high powered vacuum with HEPA filtration to pull debris out of the ducts. While that runs, we brush, whip, and agitate each run to release stuck dust and lint.
  • Supply and return cleaning. We clean every supply run and every return run, plus grilles and registers.
  • Optional duct sanitizing. If odors or microbial growth are a concern, we can apply a disinfecting treatment inside the ducts after cleaning.
  • System check. We replace or clean the filter, then run the system to confirm strong airflow.

The result is cleaner vents, better airflow, and a fresher smell through your rooms. Want proof it worked? We provide before and after photos.

                  AC Cleaning vs duct cleaning

                  AC Cleaning focuses on the equipment that makes and moves cool air. That includes the indoor coil, blower, drain, filter area, and the outdoor condenser. Duct cleaning focuses on the path that air travels through after it leaves the air handler.

                  Why does this matter? If the coils and blower are dirty, they can keep throwing particles into ducts that were just cleaned. If ducts are loaded with dust, they will re-contaminate a clean coil and filter faster. For homes with pets, smokers, or a recent remodel, doing both can make a big difference in comfort and cleanliness.

                  Unsure which to do first? We can inspect and prioritize, so you get the best result for your budget.
