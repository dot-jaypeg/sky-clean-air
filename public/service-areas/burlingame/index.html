Need Air Duct Cleaning in Burlingame, CA?

Air Duct Cleaning in Burlingame, CA is not just about dust. It is about airflow, health, and energy savings. Our process helps remove built-up debris that restricts air movement and strains your system.

Here is how our duct cleaning service works

  • Inspection and testing
    • We check your registers, returns, and main trunk lines.
    • We look for loose connections, leaks, and visible buildup.
    • If needed, we run Duct Leakage Testing and Air Duct Testing to pinpoint issues.
  • Source removal cleaning
    • We connect professional-grade negative air equipment to pull debris out.
    • We use rotating brushes and air whips to dislodge dust inside the ducts.
    • We clean supply and return lines, registers, and the blower compartment when accessible.
  • Sanitizing options
    • If you want added odor control, we can apply Duct Sanitizing and disinfecting solutions that are approved for HVAC use.
  • Final check
    • We verify airflow, check for loose ducts, and make recommendations on sealing or insulation if we see areas to improve.

You get clear communication, photos where helpful, and honest advice. If you only need a few ducts repaired or sealed, we will say so. If your system would benefit from more, we explain why and what it costs before we start.

Signs You Need Duct or System Service

Not sure what is going on with your HVAC? Here are common signs we see in Burlingame.

  • Dust puffs when the system starts.

How We Diagnose Problems The Right Way

Guessing is not a strategy. We follow a clear process to find the cause and fix it.

  • 1) Listen and look
    • We ask about comfort issues, smells, sounds, and recent utility bills.

    • We measure temperature differences across coils and rooms.

Handy Tips To Keep Air And Energy Use In Check

  • Change filters every 1 to 3 months depending on pets and usage.
  • Keep furniture and rugs from blocking vents.
  • Use kitchen and bath fans to reduce moisture and odors.
  • Run your dryer on the shortest cycles that get the job done. Long cycles can hint at a vent issue.
  • Consider a spring and fall HVAC check. Small adjustments can prevent bigger repairs.
  • If you remodel, plan duct changes with the layout. Added walls can choke airflow if vents stay the same.

Small habits add up to better comfort and cleaner air.
