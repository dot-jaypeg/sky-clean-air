What you can expect during Air Duct Cleaning in Shelltown, CA

Wondering what actually happens during an air duct cleaning visit? Our process is careful, tidy, and built to protect your home or business.

Full inspection

We start with a visual check of vents, returns, and accessible ductwork. We note damage, sagging lines, or signs of leaks.

Containment and protection

We set protective covers and corner guards. Floors and furniture near vents get covered.

Negative pressure setup

A high-suction vacuum pulls dust and debris out through a sealed hose while we clean. This keeps mess from escaping into your rooms.

Mechanical agitation

We use rotating brushes and air whips to loosen packed dust along the duct walls. This is key for older ducts or systems that have not been cleaned in years.

Vent and return cleaning

We clean grilles, registers, and returns. If you have decorative grilles, we handle them with care.

Sanitizing by request

If odors or microbial growth are a concern, we can apply safe sanitizing agents inside the ducts after cleaning. This step is optional and based on inspection findings.

Final check and system test

We verify airflow, recheck connections, and make sure your system powers on smoothly.

This is real work, not just a quick vacuum. You will see and feel the difference.

Why Shelltown Homes Often Have Stuffy Rooms

Many houses in Shelltown have older ductwork with long runs and a few sharp turns. Over time, joints loosen and leak air into the attic or crawlspace. Dust settles inside the low spots. Grilles rust a bit from sea air, and filters do not get changed as often as they should. The result is weak airflow, uneven temperatures, and extra dust in living rooms and bedrooms.

We fix this with

  • Duct leakage testing to find hidden leaks
  • Sealing with mastic or approved tapes
  • Adding or adjusting return paths to improve circulation
  • Airflow balancing to match supply to room size
  • Replacing undersized or crushed flex duct
  • Installing quality filters and setting reminders for change-outs
  • Air Duct Cleaning in Shelltown, CA to clear blockage and improve air movement

A few small changes can make that back bedroom a lot more comfortable.

Dryer Vent Cleaning That Prevents Lint Buildup

Lint piles up faster near the coast because laundry runs never stop. You should clean the dryer vent line once a year or more often if you do many loads. We handle simple behind-the-dryer lines and long runs that exit the roof.
