Our air duct cleaning process

You do not need a mess to get a clean system. Here is how our team handles Air Duct Cleaning in Point Loma, CA.

  • 1) Inspection and plan We check supply and return vents, the main trunk, and accessible duct sections. We look for dust levels, damaged connections, and growth risks. If we see gaps or crushed ducting, we point it out and offer options.
  • 2) Containment and protection We cover furniture and protect floors. Vents are sealed during cleaning to control dust. We use negative pressure so debris goes into our vacuum, not into your rooms.
  • 3) HEPA vacuum and agitation Our high powered vacuum creates strong pull through the ducts. We use rotary brushes or air whips to loosen buildup on duct walls, then extract it into sealed HEPA filtration. Learn more about HEPA filtration.
  • 4) Supply, return, and trunk cleaning We do more than the vent covers. We work through each run, including returns and trunk lines. If the blower cabinet and evaporator coil need cleaning, we can quote that too. A dirty coil can undo the benefits of clean ducts.
  • 5) Duct sanitizing or disinfecting Need a deeper clean? We offer duct sanitizing for homes and Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting for extra coverage. For office, retail, and hospitality, we provide Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting. We review product options, scents, and dry times so you know what to expect.
  • 6) Final check and photos We do a walkthrough and share before and after images when possible. You see exactly what was removed. We test airflow and confirm all vents are back in place.

Most single family homes take a few hours. Larger homes, older ductwork, or commercial sites can take longer. Do you prefer morning or afternoon appointments? We can plan around parking near Rosecrans Street or on narrow streets in the Wooded Area.

Signs you need duct cleaning or HVAC help

Not sure where to start? Look for these clues:

  • Dust puffs when your system starts
  • Dark lines on carpet near vents
  • Vent covers with fuzzy buildup
  • Uneven temperatures room to room
  • Musty or smoky smells when air flows
  • Allergy flare ups when the AC or heat turns on
  • Weak airflow from some vents
  • Rising energy bills with no change in use
  • Noises like whistling or rattling from ducts
  • Dryer needs more than one cycle
  • Hot laundry room when the dryer runs
  • Thermostat shows the set temp, but the room feels off

If you see two or more of these, your ducts or system need attention.
