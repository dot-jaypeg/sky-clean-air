Our process for Air Duct Cleaning in Lakeside, CA

You deserve a method that actually works. Here is how we handle Air Duct Cleaning in Lakeside, CA so the results last.

Step 1 Inspection and setup

  • We look at your supply and return ducts, the air handler, and the blower compartment.
  • We check filter size and fit.
  • We plan the best access points for safe cleaning.

Step 2 Protect your home or building

  • Drop cloths and corner guards go down.
  • Vents and registers are covered to control dust.
  • Furniture and breakables stay clear of work areas.

Step 3 Create strong vacuum pressure

  • We connect a high powered vacuum to the trunk line.
  • This pulls dust and debris toward the collector, not into your space.

Step 4 Agitation and source removal

  • Specialized rotary brushes and air whips scrub the interior of the ducts.
  • We move methodically from each branch line back to the main trunk.
  • All debris heads into our containment unit.

Step 5 Clean the blower, return, and supply side

  • We clean the blower housing when accessible.
  • The return plenum and supply plenum get attention too.
  • We clean and wipe down registers and grilles.

Step 6 Optional sanitizing

If odors or microbial growth are present, we can apply an EPA listed sanitizer to duct interiors after cleaning. It targets odor causing contaminants. We only recommend this when it makes sense.

Step 7 Airflow check and final walk through

  • We measure airflow at key vents.
  • We review what we found, share photos, and leave the space as clean as we found it.

How long does it take? A standard home in Lakeside often takes 3 to 5 hours, depending on system size and condition. Commercial projects vary by square footage and access.

Maintenance that fits real life

You do not have to think about HVAC every day. A simple maintenance plan can keep you ahead of breakdowns and help your system last longer.

For homes

  • HVAC tune up twice a year. Once before summer and once before cooler months.
  • Change filters every 1 to 3 months, or longer if you use high capacity media filters.
  • Air duct cleaning every 2 to 4 years. If you have pets or recent remodels, you may need it sooner.

For businesses

  • Quarterly inspections for most commercial systems
  • Filter changes based on hours of operation and traffic
  • Duct testing if you notice rising bills or uneven temps
