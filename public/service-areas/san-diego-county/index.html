HVAC & Air Quality Services in San Diego County

Our home base since 2018. From San Ysidro to Fallbrook, our trucks are in San Diego County neighborhoods every day.

Call Now — 619-304-8822Get a Quote

San Diego is where Sky Clean Air started in 2018 — one rented van, one set of duct-cleaning equipment and two founders who answered the phone themselves. Today our trucks are in San Diego County neighborhoods every day, from San Ysidro and Chula Vista up through Kearny Mesa, the beach communities and North County to Fallbrook.

The county's microclimates matter for your home. Coastal neighborhoods deal with marine-layer humidity and salt air; inland areas like El Cajon, Santee, Poway and Escondido see summer heat that pushes AC systems hard; Santa Ana winds and wildfire season send fine dust and smoke into every home. We service all of it — ducts, dryer vents, attics, heating, cooling and indoor air quality — with the same honest recommendations wherever you live.
