What You Can Expect From a Sky Clean Air Visit

You do not need to prep your whole home or shut down your business for days. Most jobs are done the same day, and we protect your space while we work.

Here is our typical process for Air Duct Cleaning in Hillcrest, CA

  • 1. Walkthrough and inspection We check vents, returns, and the air handler. If needed, we use a camera to see inside the duct lines. We look for dust buildup, lint, pet hair, loose connections, and signs of moisture.
  • 2. Set up clean containment We lay down floor coverings and seal registers. We connect a powerful negative air machine with HEPA filtration to pull debris out of the duct system.
  • 3. Agitation and debris removal We use rotating brushes or air whips to loosen buildup inside the ducts. The HEPA vacuum captures the debris so it does not blow into your space.
  • 4. Return and trunk line attention Returns collect a lot of dust. We clean these thoroughly, along with plenum and trunk lines, to keep debris from recirculating.
  • 5. Optional duct sanitizing or disinfecting For odors or after water events, we can apply duct sanitizing. We also offer Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting and Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting when needed.
  • 6. System check and filter replacement We can replace media filters, review thermostat settings, and check airflow at the registers.

We leave the place clean and tidy. You get a quick rundown of what we found with honest suggestions for next steps, if any.

Signs Your Ducts Need Attention

  • Dust bursts from vents when the system turns on
  • Musty or stale odors that do not go away
  • Allergy flare ups even with regular dusting
  • Uneven airflow from room to room
  • Rising energy bills without a change in usage
  • Rattling, whistling, or visible gaps in ductwork
  • Recent remodel, roof leak, or pest activity
  • It has been 3 to 5 years since the last cleaning

If any of these sound familiar, a duct cleaning or inspection can make a big difference.
