Our duct cleaning process

We keep the visit clean, organized, and easy to follow. Before we start, we walk the space with you and ask about rooms with weak airflow, odor complaints, recent work, pets, allergies, or business hours that affect timing. Then we check supplies, returns, the HVAC area, and access points.

  1. Walkthrough and inspection. We count registers, locate returns, review duct access, and note comfort problems.
  2. Protection and setup. We protect floors and nearby furniture, remove vent covers when needed, and prepare the system for cleaning.
  3. Negative pressure cleaning. Professional vacuum equipment pulls debris away from the occupied space while agitation tools loosen dust inside the ductwork.
  4. Return cleaning. Returns collect a large share of household dust, pet hair, and lint, so we give them careful attention.
  5. Accessible HVAC area check. We look near the blower area, cabinet, and filter path where debris often collects.
  6. Optional duct sanitizing. If odor is a concern, ask about Duct Sanitizing on Adams Avenue, CA.
  7. Final review. We reinstall covers, clean the work area, check airflow, and share what we found.

If your ductwork is damaged, loose, sagging, or undersized, cleaning alone may not solve the full issue. In that case we explain the options and can also help with Air Duct Installation on Adams Avenue, CA or Air Duct Maintenance on Adams Avenue, CA.
