Air duct services that keep air moving

Air Duct Cleaning

If you are searching for Air Duct Cleaning in Shelter Island, CA, Sky Clean Air provides a thorough process that targets dust, debris, and light microbial growth inside your ductwork.

Here is how we clean

  • 1. Inspection and assessment. We check supply and return ducts, grilles, the air handler, and coils. We look for loose connections, damage, or signs of moisture.
  • 2. Containment and protection. Grilles and registers are covered. Drop cloths protect flooring. We keep common areas clean and passable.
  • 3. High-powered negative air and brushing. We set up negative air machines and use rotary and manual brushes to loosen debris inside ducts. Debris is pulled into HEPA-filtered collection.
  • 4. Supply and return cleaning. We clean the entire path, not just the first few feet behind registers.
  • 5. Optional disinfecting. For homes and businesses that need it, we apply safe disinfecting products in ducts after cleaning.
  • 6. Final checks and cleanup. We show findings, answer questions, and clean the area. Vents are reinstalled and the system is started to verify airflow.

A closer look at Air Duct Cleaning in Shelter Island, CA

We talked about the general process. Here is how a typical job flows in your area.

  • 1. Pre-visit call. We confirm access, parking near Shelter Island Drive, and any special instructions, like quiet hours around event times.
  • 2. On-site inspection. We walk the property with you. We look at supply and return ducts, grills, plenums, and the air handler or furnace.
  • 3. Containment setup. We protect floors and furniture. Equipment stays neat and out of the way of guests and staff.
  • 4. Negative air and brushing. HEPA-filtered negative air keeps debris contained. Rotary brushes and whips loosen buildup throughout the duct runs.
  • 5. Coil and blower inspection. If needed, we clean coils and blower components so the system does not re-contaminate freshly cleaned ducts.
  • 6. Sanitizing as requested. We can apply safe treatments to reduce odors and microbial growth after cleaning.
  • 7. Final walk-through. We show what we did and point out any duct leaks or hot spots that could use sealing or balancing.

This approach to Air Duct Cleaning in Shelter Island, CA fits homes and businesses and minimizes downtime.
