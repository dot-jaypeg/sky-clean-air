Our Residential Duct Cleaning Process

We use a straightforward, careful process that focuses on source removal and clean results.

  • 1. Inspect and plan. We start with a visual inspection and airflow check. We look at supply, return, grilles, the blower compartment, and accessible trunk lines. If duct leakage is suspected, we can test for leakage and discuss sealing options.
  • 2. Protect your space. Drop cloths go down, and registers get covered as needed. Our aim is to keep dust under control from start to finish.
  • 3. Create negative pressure. We connect a high powered vacuum with HEPA filtration to pull dust and debris out of the duct system, keeping it contained.
  • 4. Agitate and remove. We use brushes and air tools to loosen buildup on duct walls, then capture it with the vacuum. We also clean grilles and registers.
  • 5. Treat only if you want. If odors are a concern, we can apply safe duct sanitizing products. We discuss it first, and you decide.
  • 6. Clean the blower compartment. If accessible, we clean the blower housing, blower wheel, and the evaporator coil area. This helps airflow and overall performance.
  • 7. Final checks. We verify results, show you what we captured, and give you recommendations for filters, sealing, or simple maintenance steps.

Want before and after photos? Just ask. Seeing the difference can be satisfying, almost like a spring cleaning for your lungs.
