Do you see puffs of dust when the AC kicks on, or notice a musty smell on Stonebridge mornings? That is a sign your ducts need attention.

How Our Duct Cleaning Process Works

We do the job the right way so you get real results, not a quick vacuum at the vents.

1) System inspection and setup

  • Check supply and return ducts, furnace or air handler, and coil access
  • Protect floors, coverings, and furniture under vents
  • Seal vents to build proper negative pressure

2) Powerful vacuum and agitation

  • Attach a high power vacuum to the trunk line
  • Use soft rotary brushes and air whips to loosen debris without damaging ducts
  • Remove dust, pet hair, construction lint, and loose insulation

3) HEPA capture and cleanup

  • Collect particles in HEPA-filtered equipment
  • Clean vent covers and returns
  • Wipe down reachable surfaces around vents

4) Optional disinfecting and deodorizing

  • Apply a safe disinfectant to help reduce microbes in the duct interior
  • Helpful after smoke exposure or heavy pet dander
  • Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting and Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting available

5) Airflow and leak checks

  • Basic airflow check at key vents
  • Recommend duct sealing or balancing if we detect leakage or poor distribution

6) Before and after photos

  • See what we removed and how clean your ducts look now

Most homes take a few hours, larger homes may take longer. You get clear pricing before we start.

How Often Should You Clean Your Ducts

Most homes in Rancho Encantada do well with duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. You may want it sooner if:

  • You completed a remodel or interior painting
  • You have multiple shedding pets
  • You notice more dust or allergies
  • You had smoke exposure from wildfires
  • Someone in the home has asthma or respiratory issues

Between cleanings, change filters on schedule. We can set you up with the right size and MERV rating so you do not choke airflow.

Helpful Maintenance Tips

  • Replace your filter every 1 to 3 months, or on the schedule we recommend
  • Keep return grilles free of furniture and clutter
  • Vacuum vent covers and floor registers
  • Trim plants around outdoor condensers
  • Do not close too many supply vents, it can cause duct leakage and coil freeze
  • Schedule spring AC and fall heating tune-ups

Small habits make a big difference. Your system will run quieter and use less energy.
