How often should you clean ducts and service your system

Every home and business is a little different, but here are simple guidelines that work well in Fairmount Village

  • Air duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years for most homes, sooner with pets, smokers, recent remodels, or wildfire ash exposure
  • HVAC tune-ups twice per year, one cooling check in spring and one heating check in fall
  • Filter changes every 1 to 3 months for standard filters, or 6 to 12 months for thicker media filters, depending on usage
  • Dryer vent cleaning every 1 year for single family homes, every 6 months for heavy use or multi-unit buildings

Not sure what you need? We can check your system and give a simple plan based on what we find.

Practical tips for cleaner air and better airflow

  • Use a MERV 8 to 13 filter that fits your system. Higher is not always better if your blower cannot handle it.
  • Mark filter change dates on a calendar or set a phone reminder.
  • Keep furniture and rugs away from returns so your system can breathe.
  • Vacuum vent registers and grilles every few months.
  • During wildfire smoke days, keep windows closed and run the system fan on low with a clean filter.
  • If you have pets, brush them outside and empty vacuum canisters outdoors.
  • Look at the exterior dryer vent monthly. If airflow is weak or lint collects, schedule a cleaning.
