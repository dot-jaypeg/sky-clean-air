What you can expect from our duct cleaning

We keep the process clear and simple, and we clean up after ourselves.

  • Inspection and plan We walk your home or business, identify supply and return vents, look at the air handler, and check access points.
  • Protection We wear shoe covers, lay down covers where needed, and seal off registers during the process.
  • Negative pressure setup A high powered HEPA filtered vacuum pulls debris out of your ducts so dust goes into our machine, not into your rooms. Learn more about HEPA filtration.
  • Agitation and cleaning Rotary brushes and air whips break loose debris inside supply and return trunks and branch lines. We clean each register and grille too.
  • Air handler attention We wipe and vacuum the blower compartment and cabinet. If you want coil cleaning, we can include AC Cleaning for the evaporator and drain pan.
  • Optional disinfecting By request, we apply an EPA registered sanitizer for Residential HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting or Commercial HVAC Air Duct Disinfecting. See EPA guidance on antimicrobial products.
  • Final check We verify airflow, reattach registers, and walk the space with you. You see what we removed and what improved.

Want the short version? We get the dust out, we do not make a mess, and you can breathe easier.

Signs you need duct cleaning

Not sure if you need Air Duct Cleaning in San Carlos, CA right now? Look for these signals.

  • Dust streaks or gray rings around vent covers
  • Excess lint on return grilles near hallways
  • Musty or smoky odor when the system starts

If any of that sounds familiar, a duct inspection will give you answers. We can show you inside with camera tools and recommend cleaning, sealing, or replacement.

Our step by step for Air Duct Cleaning in San Carlos, CA

Here is a closer look at the steps we follow.

  • 1) Walkthrough and setup. We map out supply and return runs, confirm access to the air handler, and plan equipment placement without blocking exits or parking. On busy streets near Jackson Drive, we stage gear to keep sidewalks clear.
  • 2) Protect your space. Furniture and floors get covers where needed. Registers are sealed to manage airflow. We wear masks and gloves, and we treat your space with care.
  • 3) Negative air machine on. A powerful vacuum with HEPA filtration creates negative pressure inside the duct system. That draws loosened dust into the machine.
  • 4) Brush and air whip cleaning. We feed rotary brushes and air whips through each branch and trunk line. This breaks loose caked on debris and moves it to the vacuum.
  • 5) Return and supply cleaning. Returns often hold the most dust. We clean them thoroughly, then clean supplies, registers, and grilles. We also vacuum the plenum and cabinet areas we can reach.
  • 6) Air handler service. We clean the blower wheel compartment and cabinet surfaces. If coil or drain line cleaning is needed, we can add AC Cleaning during the visit.
  • 7) Disinfecting by request. We offer safe, HVAC approved disinfecting for ducts. This is a helpful add on for homes with allergy concerns and for clinics, salons, and gyms.
  • 8) Final checks and wrap up. We test airflow, re install registers, and show you what we collected. We leave your place tidy, not dusty.

Maintenance that fits San Carlos seasons

San Carlos weather is mild, but your system still needs attention on a regular schedule.

  • Filters Check monthly, replace at least every 2 to 3 months. High pollen or smoke days can shorten filter life.
  • Duct inspection Every 2 to 3 years, sooner after remodeling or if you notice dust issues.
  • HVAC tune ups Twice a year. Spring for cooling, fall for heating.

Pro tip for locals Book cooling tune ups before late spring heat. Book heating tune ups before cool fall nights. If wildfire smoke has been heavy, schedule duct cleaning sooner.
