What Air Duct Cleaning Actually Does

We make Air Duct Cleaning in Grantville, CA simple and thorough. Here is our step by step process for most homes and light commercial spaces

  • Inspection and photos We open supply and return vents, check the air handler, and take pictures so you can see what is inside.
  • Protect the area Drop cloths under each register, corner guards where needed, boot covers on our feet.
  • Strong negative pressure We connect a powerful HEPA vacuum to your duct system to pull dust and debris into a sealed container.
  • Agitation and brushing Rotating soft brushes and air whips knock loose stuck dirt, pet hair, and drywall dust without harming the ducts.
  • Clean each supply and return line We work branch by branch so nothing is left behind.
  • Grilles and registers We remove and wash vents, then reinstall them so they look fresh.
  • Optional air handler cleaning We can clean the blower wheel and evaporator coil area to improve airflow and reduce odors.
  • Sanitizing on request After cleaning, we can apply a safe sanitizer to reduce bacteria and mildew in the duct interior.
  • Final photos and review You see the before and after results.

You get cleaner air, better airflow, and less dust on your tables and TV stand. Think of your ducts like the lungs of your building. If they are clogged, everything works harder.
