It Started With One Rented Van

Sky Clean Air was founded in San Diego by Nadav “Nadi” Offer and Daniel Lezmy with a rented van, a set of duct-cleaning equipment and a simple idea: do honest work and treat every home like your own.

01

Our Humble Beginnings

In {{founded}}, Nadav and Daniel started Sky Clean Air with what they could afford: one rented van and the equipment to clean air ducts. They answered the phone themselves, did the work themselves, and made sure every customer knew exactly what they were paying for before a single vent cover came off.

That approach stuck. Happy customers told their neighbors, the neighbors called, and one van turned into two. Duct cleaning led to dryer vents, dryer vents led to attics and insulation, and before long homeowners were asking the people they already trusted to take care of their heating and cooling too.

How We Work

Most of our customers meet us on a small job first. That's on purpose — we'd rather earn your trust than ask for it.

1

We Start With The Basics

Air duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, attic cleaning and insulation. These are the jobs that make an immediate difference in how your home feels and breathes — and they're how most families first get to know us.

2

We Inspect The Whole System

While we're there, our technicians do a full home HVAC inspection. We check the furnace, the AC, the air handler and the ductwork, and we look at how old everything is — systems over about 15 years old are usually where problems start.

3

We Tell You What We Found

You get a straight answer: what's working, what needs a repair, and what's close to the end of its life. If your system is fine, we say so. If it's time to replace it, we'll show you why and give you a written price. No pressure either way.

04

Where We're Headed

San Diego is home, and it always will be. But the same honest, inspect-first approach works anywhere families want cleaner air and a system they can count on — so we're bringing it to more of Southern California.

Over the next five years our goal is to grow Sky Clean Air to $50 million in revenue (or $5 million in EBITDA) — without losing the personal touch that got us here. That means more trucks, more trained technicians and more neighborhoods, with the same people answering for the work.
